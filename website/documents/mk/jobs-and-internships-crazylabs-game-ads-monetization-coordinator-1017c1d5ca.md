# \[CrazyLabs\] Game Ads Monetization Coordinator

Do you love mobile games and have a passion for understanding how they grow and generate revenue? CrazyLabs Macedonia is opening its Internship Program for Game Ads Monetization Coordinator — your chance to gain hands-on experience optimizing in-game ads, analyzing performance data, and helping maximize revenue across top-tier mobile titles.

If you’re analytical, detail-oriented, curious about AI tools, and eager to learn how to build smart monetization strategies that power successful games — this internship is for you!

#### What you’ll do

- Analyze and optimize in-game ad performance
- Monitor key revenue metrics and propose improvements
- Work with ad networks and analytics tools
- Collaborate with developers and product teams

#### What you bring

- Final year student or recent graduate
- Strong analytical mindset
- Passion for mobile games
- Good English communication skills

#### Additional Information

**Location:** CrazyLabs Skopje
**Duration:** Full-time, 6h/day
**Deadline:** December 20, 2025

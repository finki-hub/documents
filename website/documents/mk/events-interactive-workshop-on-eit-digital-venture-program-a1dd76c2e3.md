# Interactive Workshop on EIT Digital Venture Program

We are excited to announce that **[Macedonia2025](https://www.macedonia2025.com/)** and [Startup Macedonia](https://startupmacedonia.mk/), in partnership with **[EIT Digital](https://www.eitdigital.eu/venture-program/)** are organizing an interactive workshop on the EIT Digital Venture Program in Skopje. This is an excellent opportunity for all entrepreneurs with deep tech business concepts to be better acquainted with the program’s specifics and become motivated to apply for international growth support. The workshop will take place on **18 February 2020 (Tuesday)**, from **5 pm** to **7 pm**, at the premises of **[Europe House Skopje](https://www.google.com/maps?q=europe+house+skopje&uact=5&um=1&ie=UTF-8&sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwjJ7Zeohb_nAhVO06YKHRbhBdwQ_AUoAnoECBEQBA)**.

More information on this [link](https://www.macedonia2025.com/mk/activities/single/a-great-opportunity-for-all-deep-tech-businesses-interactive-workshop-on-eit-digital-venture-program-in-skopje/).

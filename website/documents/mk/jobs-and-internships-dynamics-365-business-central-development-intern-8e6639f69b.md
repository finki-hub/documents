# Dynamics 365 Business Central Development Intern

If you’re excited about ERP systems and building business applications that solve real-world problems, this internship is your entry point. At Motion Source, you’ll work alongside experienced developers on international projects, gaining deep practical experience in Business Central customization and development from day one.

WHAT YOU’LL GAIN
• Financial compensation throughout the internship
• Real project experience with international clients
• Structured onboarding and dedicated mentorship
• Immersive exposure to Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central
• A collaborative, growth-focused working environment
• Team events, networking opportunities, and company culture
• Career development pathways within Motion Source

WHAT YOU’LL LEARN & WORK WITH
• Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central architecture and development lifecycle
• AL language — the native language for Business Central customization
• Object-oriented programming principles applied to ERP environments
• C# and .NET concepts as they relate to Microsoft ecosystem integrations
• ERP data structures, business logic, and workflow design
• SQL for data querying and reporting within Business Central
• Best practices for enterprise ERP implementation and client delivery

WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR
• Student or recent graduate in Computer Science or a related field
• Basic understanding of programming and object-oriented concepts
• Familiarity with C#, .NET, or AL language is a plus, but not required
• Strong analytical thinking and clear communication skills
• Eagerness to learn ERP systems and understand real business processes
• Good English language skills and a team-first mindset

YOUR DAY-TO-DAY
• Assist in customizing and extending Business Central functionalities
• Support development, testing, and quality assurance activities
• Participate in troubleshooting and resolving technical issues
• Collaborate with consultants and developers across implementation projects
• Learn and apply Microsoft Business Central development best practices

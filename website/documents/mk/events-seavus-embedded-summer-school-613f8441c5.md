# Seavus Embedded Summer School

Seavus, in collaboration with ST Microelectronics, will again organize its annual summer embedded school for all the students from the technical faculties in Macedonia.

![poster_embedded.png](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/poster_embedded.png)

During the summer school, the students will have the opportunity to work with Seavus experts in embedded technologies and gain unique skills for developing state of the art embedded systems. Also, the students will have the possibility to use advanced development boards from ST Microelectronics on which they will make practical projects. This summer school will introduce the students to:

- C programming language with focus on functionalities required to develop SW for embedded systems
- Creation and development of projects with System Workbench Software Development Kit
- Installation and debugging of applications on target HW systems
- Use of diverse external sensors, memory cards, communication with PC’s, LCD devices and similar.
- Development of diverse IoT nodes and sensor devices

Eligible for this Summer school are students in their second, third and fourth year of studies.

Requirements:

- Basic Knowledge in Software Algorithms
- Basic knowledge of C programming language
- Basic knowledge of electronics

Advantage will be given to graduates from the following faculties:

- FEIT
- FINKI
- FIKT
- SEEU

The number of participants is limited, therefore the candidates that have applied will undergo a selection process.
The Summer School will be organized in the period of **3th until 28th of July, 2017**.

The deadline for applying is **28th of June, 2017**.

You can find more info on the following [link.](http://career.seavus.com/job-opportunities/job/seavus-embedded-summer-school)

# Scalefocus MKD Student CTF \(Capture The Flag\)

Join Scalefocus’ MKD Student CTF – Compete, Learn, and Win Big!

Calling all students with a passion for cybersecurity! Scalefocus is organizing the MKD Student CTF Challenge (&lt;https://ctf.scalefocus.com&gt;) where you will have the chance to show off your skills, represent your faculty, and compete for amazing prizes. But who will be victorious and who will be crowned as the best flag hunter among you?

What is CTF?

Capture The Flag (CTF) is a fun and educational cybersecurity competition where participants solve a series of security-related puzzles to find hidden pieces of information called “flags”. These flags are typically strings of text, such as FLAG{example}, that can be hidden in files, code, or network traffic.
The challenges are designed to test problem-solving, cryptography, web security, and ethical hacking skills.

Whether you’re a beginner or an experienced coder, the MKD Student CTF has challenges for every skill level!

Challenge Details:

Start Date: 02.12.24
End Date: 16.12.24

Duration: 2 weeks of non-stop problem-solving

Who Can Participate?

Participation in the Competition is restricted to students who are currently enrolled at Faculty of Electrical Engineering & Information Technologies, Skopje (FEIT), Faculty of Computer Science & Engineering, Skopje (FINKI) or Faculty Of Information And Communication Technologies, Bitola (FICT). This is your chance to showcase your skills, learn new techniques, and have fun with fellow students.

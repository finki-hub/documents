# Workshop: “Publishing in High\-Impact Journals: Mapping the Web of Science and Scopus”

**Date:** Wednesday, April 9, 2025
**Time:** 12:30-13:30
**Venue:** FINKI, Big Amphitheatre

*Activity as a part of cooperation between Ss. Cyril and Methodius University in Skopje and UN International Organization for Migration (IOM) for engagement of Macedonian Academic Scientific Diaspora.*

**Topics Covered:**

- Mapping the Scopus & Web of Science rankings.
- Identifying Q1/Q2 journals for publication.
- Writing for high-impact journals (structure, quality, citations).
- Handling peer review & revisions successfully.

Who Should Attend? Professors, teaching assistants, researchers, and PhD students looking to enhance their publication record.

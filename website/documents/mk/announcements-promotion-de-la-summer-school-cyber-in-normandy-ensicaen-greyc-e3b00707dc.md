# Promotion de la Summer School Cyber in Normandy – ENSICAEN GREYC

We are pleased to announce that, **ENSICAEN, will be organizing its very first Summer School this year, titled “Cyber in Normandy”**, from the **24th of June, until the 5th of July 2024**.

As you may have guessed, the focus of this Summer School will be cybersecurity, and it will bring together experts in the field from all over Europe. The target audiences are master’s students, doctorates and post-doctorates with prior knowledge and experience in cybersecurity.

**Deadline for applications**: 1st of March

**Participation fees**: 800€ (including housing, breakfasts, and lunches from Mondays to Fridays and social activities to discover Normandy)

You will find all the information on the website: [Summer school in Cybersecurity in Normandy 2024 – Sciencesconf.org](https://cyberinnormandy2024.sciencesconf.org/)

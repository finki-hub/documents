# Dynamics 365 Developer Intern

Join Motion Source and gain hands-on experience in enterprise-grade Microsoft Dynamics 365 solutions. You’ll be embedded in real international projects from day one, guided by senior consultants and developers who are invested in your growth. This is more than an internship — it’s the foundation of a meaningful career in enterprise technology.

WHAT YOU’LL GAIN
• Paid internship with structured onboarding
• Direct exposure to live international client projects
• Dedicated mentorship from senior Dynamics 365 professionals
• Practical experience with the full Microsoft ecosystem
• A collaborative, knowledge-sharing team culture
• Networking opportunities, team events, and company socials
• A clear pathway to long-term employment upon successful completion

WHAT YOU’LL LEARN & WORK WITH
• Dynamics 365 platform architecture, configuration, and customization workflows
• Development and implementation of AI Agents with Microsoft Copilot
• Client-side scripting and automation with JavaScript
• Data querying and management with SQL
• Microsoft Power Platform tools and integration patterns
• Agile project delivery practices and client collaboration

WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR
• Final-year student or recent graduate in Computer Science, or a related discipline
• Basic familiarity with programming concepts — experience in any language is a great start
• A problem-solving mindset and genuine curiosity about enterprise systems
• Strong communication skills in English, both written and verbal
• Proactive attitude and the ability to work effectively within a team

YOUR DAY-TO-DAY
• Assist in the development and customization of Dynamics 365 solutions
• Contribute to the implementation of new features and enhancements
• Participate in debugging, testing, and quality assurance tasks
• Collaborate with senior developers to support project delivery
• Engage with Microsoft Dynamics 365 frameworks and build working knowledge over time

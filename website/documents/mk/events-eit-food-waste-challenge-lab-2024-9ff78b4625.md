# EIT Food Waste Challenge Lab 2024

Do you know that waste is not waste until you waste it?

If you’re passionate about transforming food waste into valuable, marketable products, we invite you to join the Food Waste Challenge!

For five weeks (total of 20 effective hours), with **[EIT Food](https://www.linkedin.com/company/eit-food/)** and **[BAU Accelerator](https://www.linkedin.com/company/bauaccelerator/)** support, you’ll participate in a boot camp to learn, innovate, and develop food waste into innovative new products. Work on real challenges presented by industry representatives.

Ready to make a difference? Let’s create a future where nothing goes to waste!

Deadline for application 22nd of October -&gt; **&lt;https://shorturl.at/wtLbE&gt;**

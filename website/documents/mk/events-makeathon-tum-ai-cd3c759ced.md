# Makeathon TUM\.ai

TUM.ai is a profit-free student initiative at the Technical University of Munich (TUM) (located in Munich, Germany). One of its signature events is the Makeathon, a 48-hour virtual challenge in which participants use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to develop real-world business cases in multi-disciplinary teams.

Yet another time, TUM.ai is organizing the AI Makeathon. Students and young professionals from business, IT, medical and other backgrounds will be working together on challenges in Education, Environment or MedTech tracks.

You will work in interdisciplinary teams on projects supported by NetApp, Deloitte, Infineon Technologies, ImFusion, MI4People and others! Furthermore, the Makeathon will be powered by Amazon Web Services (AWS)! In addition, you will get special access to Codex and GPT-3 by OpenAI, a company founded by Elon Musk. This opportunity is also open to both techies & non-techies, since no intensive programming skills are required to participate.

So, hurry up and join the 48-hour virtual challenge under the topic of AI4SocialGood in which innovators of tomorrow develop a working AI solution for a real-world business case. The application is open until April 6th, 2022 (deadline is about to be extended, so feel free to apply after 06.04)!

For applying, click here: &lt;https://makeathon.tum-ai.com/apply&gt;

# AI Engineer Internship @ Advertisable

AI Engineer, Internship at Advertisable

Location: Fully remote | Duration: 3-month internship, with a long-term full-time path

WHO WE ARE

Advertisable is an AI video SaaS platform. We let brands and marketers generate, edit and scale video ads with AI, turning a product, a page or a brief into ad creative that actually ships, at a volume no human production team can match.

Real product. Real users. Real ad budgets running through it every day.

We’re growing fast. The pace is intense, the bar is high, and results are measured, not talked about.

THIS IS NOT A REGULAR INTERNSHIP

We have exactly two engineering spots on a small team.

The internship runs three months, but we’re not hiring for three months. We take interns we intend to keep. If you want a line on your CV before you move on, this isn’t it. We invest in people who want to grow into full-time engineers here, and we choose accordingly.

You write production code in your first week. There is no tutorial phase.

We build to the industry standard, not the local one. There is no faster place to become a serious AI engineer. You’ll be in a room with people who are better than you, shipping every day, and you’ll be expected to catch up fast.

We want the best. A-players only.

WHAT YOU’LL ACTUALLY DO

– Build agentic, self-improving systems: pipelines that evaluate their own output, catch their own failures and get better without a human in the loop
– Build and integrate LLM and generative-video features: prompting, RAG, tool use, model orchestration
– Work with APIs, data pipelines and the automation layer behind our video generation stack
– Rapid prototyping: idea to working version in days, not months
– Debug, optimize and own what you build

WHO WE’RE LOOKING FOR

– Obsessed with AI. Not curious, obsessed. You try models the week they drop, you have opinions about them, and there’s a trail of things you built because you couldn’t not build them.
– Vibe coder. You build with AI, not by hand. Cursor, Claude Code, agents. You ship at a speed that isn’t otherwise possible, and you still own and understand every line that goes out.
– You want the hardest problems. Agentic, self-improving systems that evaluate and correct their own output. That’s the actual work here. If that sounds intimidating rather than exciting, this isn’t your role.
– You move fast. Short cycles, direct feedback, no hand-holding.

Bonus: video/image models, FFmpeg, computer vision, generative media APIs.

THIS IS NOT FOR YOU IF

– You want a quiet, predictable 9-to-5
– You need someone standing over your shoulder
– AI is something you use occasionally, not something you think about on your own time

WHAT YOU GET

– Working shoulder to shoulder with senior engineers: code review, mentorship, an actual industry standard
– Access to real data, real problems and a live product with paying users
– An exponential learning curve
– A genuine path to a full-time role.

HOW TO APPLY
Send an email to : alexander@advertisable.ai
Email with the subject line: AI Internship – Full Name

Your current year of study and grade average (GPA from 6 to 10).

1. CV + GitHub / LinkedIn

2. Answer three questions (max 200 words each):

– What makes you passionate and obsessed with AI? Where did it come from, and what specifically pulls you in?
– Why are you a great addition to this team? What do you bring that someone else doesn’t?
– How do you plan to compete with, and learn from, engineers who are better than you today?

3. Links to everything you’ve built. All of it: projects, repos, experiments, scripts, half-finished things. Unfinished is fine, rough is fine. What matters is that you can explain how you built it and why you made those choices.

If reading this made you think “that sounds too intense,” it’s probably not for you, and that’s fine. If it made you think “when do we start,” write to us.

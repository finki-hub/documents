# Dynamics 365 Developer Intern

Motion Source is looking for a student or recent graduate interested in ERP systems and business application development.
During the internship, you’ll work with experienced developers on international projects and gain practical experience in Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central.

WHAT WE OFFER
• Paid internship
• Structured onboarding and mentorship
• Experience working on real client projects
• Practical training in Business Central development
• Supportive and collaborative working environment
• Team events and networking opportunities
• Opportunity for long-term career development at Motion Source

WHAT YOU’LL LEARN
• Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central architecture
• AL development and Business Central customization
• Object-oriented programming in an ERP environment
• C# and .NET concepts used in Microsoft integrations
• ERP data structures, business logic, and workflows
• SQL for querying and reporting
• ERP implementation and development best practices

YOUR RESPONSIBILITY
• Assist with Business Central customizations and extensions
• Support development, testing, and quality assurance
• Help troubleshoot and resolve technical issues
• Work with developers and consultants on implementation projects
• Learn and follow Business Central development standards

WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR
• Final-year student or recent graduate in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related discipline
• Basic knowledge of programming and object-oriented concepts
• Familiarity with C#, .NET, or AL is an advantage, but not required
• Good analytical and communication skills
• Interest in ERP systems and business processes
• Good command of English
• Willingness to learn and work as part of a team

APPLICATION PROCESS
After this internship posting closes, selected applicants will receive an email from Motion Source inviting them to complete the next stage of the application process. This will include submitting additional documents and a short 30–90 second video in English.

Please ensure that the email address provided in your application is correct and monitor your inbox, including your spam or junk folder.

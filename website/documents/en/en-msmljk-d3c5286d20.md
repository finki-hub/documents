| Primary activity The seminar has six main activities:  ● organizing and overseeing the work of the Macedonian language lectorates abroad;  ● organizing a summer school with courses in the Macedonian language and lectures on Macedonian language, literature, history, and culture;  Organizing a winter school with courses in the Macedonian language  ● organizing an international scientific conference on topics of the Macedonian language, literature, and culture;  ● own publishing activities for the needs of the lecture series, the summer school, and the scientific conference; |

| A little history The International Seminar for Macedonian Language, Literature, and Culture was founded on December 22, 1967.  To date, 56 summer schools and three winter schools have been held, in which more than 4,000 participants from over 60 countries have participated,  including prominent Macedonists, professors of Macedonian language and literature at foreign universities, Slavists, Balkanists, translators, scholars, and students studying the Macedonian language in our lectorates and at universities around the world.  In the past, the Seminar was led by:  ● Aleksandar Spasov (1968-1970);  ● Bozhidar Vidoeski (1971-1977);  ● Tome Sazdov (1978–1983);  ● Kiril Koneski (1984-1985);  ● Tomeislav Todorovski (1986-1989 and 1997-2000);  ● Kosta Peev (1990-1993);  ● Maksim Karanfilovski (1994-1996, 2001-2005 and 2009-2013);  ● Emilija Tzervenkovska (2006–2009);  ● Slavica Veleva (2013 – 2019);  ● Vesna Mojsova – Chepishhevska (2019-2023);  ● Kristina Nikolovska (2023-present). |

# Dr\. Sashko Ristov

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/sashko.ristov.jpg)

[sashko.ristov@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:sashko.ristov@finki.ukim.mk)

He was born in Kavadarci on 20 November 1976. He completed his primary education at Dimkata Angelov Gaberot Primary School in Kavadarci and his secondary education at Dobri Daskalov Mathematics and Science Secondary School as the top student of his generation, receiving more than 100 certificates of commendation and awards in municipal, regional and national competitions in mathematics, physics and informatics, as well as medals at national mathematics olympiads. In 1995, he participated in the 37th International Mathematical Olympiad.

He graduated in 2000 from the Computer Engineering, Informatics and Automation programme at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering in Skopje, with a grade average of 9.29, and was recognised among the best students of his generation in every academic year. On 11 May 2011, he defended his master’s thesis, “Analysis of Web-Service Security and Its Impact on Web-Server Performance”, at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technologies.

From 2000 to 2008, he worked at Makedonski Telekom AD (MTnet, AD Makedonski Telekomunikacii) as a software-application project manager, senior IT security specialist, and the first security officer of the MTnet CA PKI system. In 2002, he was named Employee of the Year.

From December 2008 to September 2011, he worked as an expert associate at the Institute of Informatics within the Faculty of Natural Sciences and Mathematics in Skopje and conducted practical classes in Computer Architecture, Microprocessors and Microcontrollers, Microprocessors, Computer Networks, Computer Network Design, Internet Programming, Software Quality and Testing, and Teamwork. He is an instructor for the  Cisco CCNA  courses at the Regional  Cisco  Academy.

Since 2011, he has been working on his doctoral dissertation, “Performance and Security in Cloud Computing”.

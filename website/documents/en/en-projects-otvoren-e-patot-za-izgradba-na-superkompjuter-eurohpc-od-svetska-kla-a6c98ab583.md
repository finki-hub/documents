# Отворен е патот за изградба на суперкомпјутер EuroHPC од светска класа во Грција – ФИНКИ

A hosting agreement has been signed between the EuroHPC Joint Undertaking and GRNET in Greece, where DAEDALUS, a new mid-range supercomputer, will be hosted.

DAEDALUS will be operated by GRNET and is expected to become available in early 2025. It will be capable of performing more than 30 petaflops, or 30 quadrillion calculations per second.

HPC\_DAEDALUS will serve a wide range of users from the scientific community, industry and the public sector in Greece and throughout Europe. The supercomputer will power new applications in areas including engineering, chemistry and health sciences, and will be used for visualisation and the solution of scientific problems.

DAEDALUS is funded by EuroHPC, through the European Union’s Digital Europe Programme, and Greece. Cyprus, Montenegro and North Macedonia participate in the project as members of the DAEDALUS consortium.

Further information is available at the following [link](http://bit.ly/3XGiOGL).

Partners in DAEDALUS:

- GRNET-  National Infrastructures for Research and Technology, Greece: owner, operator, manager and coordinator
- The Cyprus Institute, Cyprus: participation in the high-level support team
- University of Donja Gorica, Montenegro: participation in the high-level support team
- Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering, Ss. Cyril and Methodius University in Skopje: participation in the high-level support team

# Правила за запишување на предмети – ФИНКИ

When registering for courses, the student first registers for failed required and elective courses from the corresponding previous semester (summer/winter), then the required courses for the current semester, and finally, the elective courses for the current semester. but in such a way that their total academic workload in one semester does not exceed the number of ECTS credits specified in the study regulations.

The courses offered in the respective semester will be announced by the Faculty two weeks before the semester begins.

Upon re-enrolling in a failed elective course, the student has the right to replace it with another elective from the courses offered in the respective semester.

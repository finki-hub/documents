# Хакатонот DigiEduHack ги прогласува победниците\! – ФИНКИ

Under the motto “Cyber Balance – Well-being in Digital Education,” this hackathon was held for the first time in Macedonia at FINKI on November 8 and 9, under the patronage of the European Commission through DigiEduHack, with support from MKSafeNet – Safer Internet Center.
The first-place award is shared by the teams:

- “ScreenSense” with participants: Nikita Mitevski, Dimitar Janev, Daniel Janev, Matej Stoilkovski and Mite Atanasov
- “SafeSpace” with participants: Kiril Cvetkov, Andrej Trendov, Filip Mihailov and Kire Angelov

Special awards were presented to the following teams:

- Most innovative application of AI: “CyberSafe” with participants: Diar Saliu, Jetmir Ramadani, Lek Lleshi, Blend Etemi, Erion Ramadani
- Young Innovators: “AMi-Planner” with participants: Arbin Cerkezi, Adem Ademi
- Socially responsible idea: “Hear&See Safe” with participants: Margarita Dimitrijeska, Sandra Ilievska, Marija Joveva, Kristina Markoska
- Most unique idea: “TokenTown” with participants: Eva Kjurchiska, Sara Stojanova, Stefan Frchkovski, Tamara Todorovska
- Most creative presentation: “DvorUM/MindGarden” with participants: Marin Naumoski, Jovan Cirkoski, Tamara Chavkoska, Kiril Pereski

All high-school students from the teams that won first prize, as well as fourth-year high-school students from the teams that won special awards (Kiril Pereski from SOU Gymnasium Dr. Ibrahim Temo, Struga; Jovan Cirkoski from SOU Gymnasium Dr. Ibrahim Temo, Struga; Jetmir Ramadani from “Mathematics and Informatics Gymnasium – MIG”) receive vouchers for studying at FINKI.

We congratulate all awarded teams and all other participants for taking part in the initiative to balance mental well-being in the digital era!

# ICT Innovations 2024 – ФИНКИ

ICT Innovations 2024

The ICT Innovations 2024 conference, entitled “TechConvergence: AI, Business, and Startup Synergy”, was held in Ohrid from 28 to 30 September. The conference addressed artificial intelligence, business strategies, start-ups and their ethical implications. Researchers, industry experts and entrepreneurs participated and shared their latest research and innovations. Particular emphasis was placed on cross-sector collaboration to make better use of technology and create sustainable solutions.

Further information is available on the [official website](https://ictinnovations.org)

# Zhivko Atanaskoski

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/zivko.atanaskoski.jpg)

[zivko.atanaskoski@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:zivko.atanaskoski@finki.ukim.mk)

Zhivko Atanaskoski was born on June 24, 1997, in Skopje, Republic of North Macedonia. He completed his high school education at the „Rade Jovchevski – Korzhagin“ Gymnasium with consistently excellent grades.

He continued his higher education at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering, majoring in Computer Science and Engineering. He graduated in 2022 with a thesis titled „Priority Systems for Mass Service,“ under the mentorship of Prof. Dr. Verica Bakeva Smiljkova.

In the 2023/24 academic year, they are enrolling in a master's program at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering, in the Security, Cryptography, and Coding track.

FINKI holds lecture and laboratory exercises in the courses: Mathematics 1 / Calculus, Mathematics 2 / Discrete Mathematics, Selected Topics in Mathematics, Object-Oriented Programming, and Multimedia Technologies.

His areas of scientific research interest are the application of probabilistic models, information theory, coding, and cryptography.

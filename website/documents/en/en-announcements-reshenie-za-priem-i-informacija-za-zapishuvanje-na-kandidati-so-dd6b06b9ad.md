**2.**Registration form from the State Statistical Office of the Republic of Macedonia (form Voi. 20) (purchased from a bookstore or download and print it from **&lt;https://www.stat.gov.mk/obrasci/Obrazovanie/VOI.20.pdf&gt;**)
**3.a.**Candidates admitted to **state quota** Submit proof of payment for the first installment of **One hundred euros** in denar equivalent at the central rate of the National Bank of the Republic of North Macedonia (**[Example payment slip](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/finki-uplatnica-pp50-irata-100evra_1.png)**)

# Информации и продолжување на рокот за пријавување за подготвителна настава – ФИНКИ

We inform you that based on the number of registered candidates, the preparatory course in mathematics and programming will be held. **Due to the very small number of students registered for in-person math classes, math instruction will be held entirely online.**

**Due to the great interest and messages we have received, the application deadline is extended to the 1st.****4.9.202****Fourth year (****Saturday) to** **16:00** (You can find details about the course and how to apply on this [link](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/mk/content/%D0%BF%D0%BE%D0%B4%D0%B3%D0%BE%D1%82%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BB%D0%BD%D0%B0-%D0%BD%D0%B0%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B0%D0%B2%D0%B0-2024?fbclid=IwY2xjawFQ8BdleHRuA2FlbQIxMQABHVZuIi6Y-REV4Gh77fNHz7eCNJFkaCn1MikYIkhi_VQQ89HnxjW4ar0lMA_aem_dWWL0NaQJz1DyI2d9ZFY1A&sfnsn=mo)

The preparatory math course will begin on Monday (9/16/2024) and will run through Saturday (9/21/2024) online, starting at 9 AM. Details on how to access the classes via FINKI's remote learning systems will be sent to registered candidates on Saturday after 4 PM.

The preparatory course in computer science will be held hybrid from September 23, 2024, to September 27, 2024. The detailed schedule, along with information on how to access the courses via FINKI's remote learning systems, will be sent to registered candidates during the next week.

In order to attend the preparatory course, you must submit a payment receipt for the appropriate amount.

– 3000 denars for preparatory programming instruction (if you are only taking programming)

– 5000 denars for preparatory math classes (if you are only taking math)

– 8000 denars for preparatory instruction in programming and mathematics (if you have selected both courses)

The payment slip (available [here](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/mk/zafakultetot/finansii)) should contain the following information:

Recipient title: RM Budget – UKIМ – Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering, Skopje

Recipient's bank: NBRM

**Payment slip** (photo or document for electronic payment) you need to send it to [podgotvitelna@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:podgotvitelna@finki.ukim.mk)**by** **17.9.202****4 (Tuesday) at 9 AM** if you have selected mathematics or both courses, or by September 23, 2024 if you have selected only programming.

For any questions, you can write to us at [podgotvitelna@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:podgotvitelna@finki.ukim.mk).

We wish you a successful teaching.

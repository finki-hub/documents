# Заверка и упис на семестар за втор циклус студии за учебната 2025/2026 – ФИНКИ

Заверката на зимскиот и уписот на летниот семестар во учебната 2025/2026 година за студентите од II циклус на студии ќе се врши од **26.02.2026** to **06.03.2026** year of **09:00** to **12:00 PM** at the Student Service desk of FINKI in the TMF building.

**Documents required for enrollment in the winter semester:**

1. Index
2. Semester Endorsement Form
3. Semester enrollment form (to be submitted by all students who have not yet graduated, i.e., for enrolling an empty semester)
4. [Internal](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/interen_prijaven_list_magisterski2025.pdf) Registration form (for students enrolling in courses only)
5. [Payment slip](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/untitled_9.png) for the next tuition installment, in accordance with the study agreement

For students who are retaking a course, the following is additionally required:

Payment slip (for the value of the credits for courses being retaken according to the study program)
The cost of 1 credit = 1/60 of the value of the study program for one-year programs, and 1/120 for two-year study programs.
Payment is made in the denar equivalent according to the NBRM average exchange rate on the date of payment.

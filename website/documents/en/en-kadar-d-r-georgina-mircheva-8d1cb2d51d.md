# Dr\. Georgina Mircheva

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/von.-prof.-d-r-georgina-mircheva--731x1024.jpg)

[georgina.mirceva@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:georgina.mirceva@finki.ukim.mk)

She was born in 1984 in Shtip. She graduated and received her master's degree from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje in 2007 and 2009, respectively. During her undergraduate and graduate studies, she was regularly awarded for her academic achievements. In 2014, she earned her doctorate from the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering in Skopje.

She has worked at the Institute of Computer Science and Computer Engineering at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje since 2007 as a demonstrator, and in 2009 she was appointed junior assistant. Since 2011, she has worked at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering in Skopje as a junior assistant, and in 2012 she was appointed assistant. She has been involved in teaching exercises for several courses: databases, databases 1, artificial intelligence, data mining, multimedia systems, information systems management, machine intelligence and learning, geographic information systems, systems analysis and design, object-oriented analysis and design, and others.

As part of the scientific research work to date, over 30 research papers have been published in international journals and in the proceedings of foreign and domestic conferences. Research areas of interest include bioinformatics, computational biology, artificial intelligence, data mining, multimedia, machine learning, and databases.

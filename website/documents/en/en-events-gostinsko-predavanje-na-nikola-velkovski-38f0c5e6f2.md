# Гостинско предавање на Никола Велковски – ФИНКИ

On **17.10.2024** (Thursday) at **18:00 in FCSE Amphitheatre G**, Nikola Velkovski will deliver a guest lecture.

Nikola Velkovski is a former FCSE student who turned his seminar assignment for the Business and Management course into a successful start-up company.

His company, Howitzer, develops a SaaS product that currently generates more than USD 2 million in annual revenue. During the lecture, he will share further details about his turbulent journey to date.

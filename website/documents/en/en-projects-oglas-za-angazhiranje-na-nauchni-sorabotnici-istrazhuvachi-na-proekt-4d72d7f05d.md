# Оглас за ангажирање на научни соработници – истражувачи на проект од областа на компјутерска невропатологија на психијатриски болести – ФИНКИ

*[The Research Centre for Computer Science and Information Technologies (RCCSIT)](http://manu.edu.mk/knit/)* *at the* Macedonian Academy of Sciences and Arts (MASA) seeks to engage ***research associates for a project***for a period of three years, within a research programme in the field of **computational neuropathology of psychiatric disorders**in cooperation with the *[New York State Psychiatric Institute](https://nyspi.org/) at the [Columbia University in New York](https://www.columbiapsychiatry.org/molecular-imaging-and-neuropathology)*.

We seek motivated candidates with proven computing skills to work in:

**Position 1** – **a)** protocols for quantifying cellular structures in microscopic images of the human brain by applying a broad range of morphometric techniques; and **b)** innovative solutions for optimising and accelerating depth focusing in laser microscopy through machine learning. Previous experience in programming *deep neural networks* on GPUprocessors will be considered an advantage;

**Position 2** – statistical models of different types of biological data—molecular expression of RNA and proteins, DNA methylation, and cellular-structure parameters—to identify potential biomarkers of psychiatric disorders. Previous experience with *graphical models* and machine learning will be considered an advantage.

Candidates are expected to hold a degree in computer science, electrical engineering, physics, mathematics or a related technical discipline, and to have acquired programming and computational skills relevant to the research problems described above through undergraduate and/or postgraduate study or professional engagement.The collaborative nature of the project requires an advanced command of English.

Please submit your CVtogether with a motivation letter in English of no more than one page to **Academician Professor Ljupcho Kocarev**, the project leader, at **[lkocarev@manu.edu.mk](mailto:lkocarev@manu.edu.mk)**no later than Friday, 5 February 2021.

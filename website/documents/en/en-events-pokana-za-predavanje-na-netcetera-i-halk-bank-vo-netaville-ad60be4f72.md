# Покана за предавање на Netcetera и Halk bank во Netaville – ФИНКИ

On December 19, 2023 (Tuesday), starting at 6:00 PM, a lecture will be held at Netaville on the topic: „Fundamentals of Security in Online Payments, in partnership with HalkBank and Netcetera.“.

In the lecture, Senior Software Engineer Nenad Kocev from Netcetera will reveal the technical solutions used to overcome the challenges associated with implementing 3DS in the banking sector, while representatives from Halkbank will present how this service is used and how customers are protected during online transactions.

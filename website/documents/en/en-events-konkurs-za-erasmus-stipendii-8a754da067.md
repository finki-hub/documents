# Конкурс за ERASMUS\+ стипендии – ФИНКИ

As part of the European Commission's ERASMUS+ program, the University „St. Cyril and Methodius” is announcing a call for applications for scholarships for students and faculty for academic stays at higher education institutions in the EU.

Only candidates with Macedonian citizenship who, at the time of the mobility, are enrolled in at least their second year of studies may apply.

The deadline for submitting applications is October 20. You can find more details about the requirements and how to apply at [this link [PDF]](http://beta.finki.ukim.mk/Content/contentFiles/news/Prilog/erasmus-plus-konkurs-2015-2016.pdf).

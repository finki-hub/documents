kostadin.mishev@finki.ukim.mk

He was born in 1990 in Skopje. He completed his primary and secondary education in Negotino. During his high school years, he won numerous awards at national competitions in computer science, physics, and mathematics. He graduated in 2013 from the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering in the Informatics and Computer Engineering (ICE) major with a grade point average of 9.72. During his studies, he was continuously awarded for academic excellence. In 2016, he defended his master's thesis at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering and earned a Master of Science in Informatics and Computer Engineering in the field of computer networks and e-technologies. He enrolled in doctoral studies in September 2017 in the Computer Science program at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering.

From 2014 to 2017, he worked as a software engineer at the FINKY Computer Center and was also employed as a teaching assistant for several courses. In December 2017, he was selected as a PhD assistant at FINKY. His teaching responsibilities include courses in Operating Systems, Computer Networks, Web Programming, E-commerce and Mobile Commerce, Computer Architecture and Organization, and Mobile Platforms and Programming.

In his career to date, he has participated in numerous software and research projects. As part of his research work to date, he has published several scientific papers as an author and co-author. He is actively working in the field of machine learning and deep neural networks for natural language processing of data in finance and medicine.

He was born in 1988 in Prilep. He graduated from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje in 2010. During his studies, he was continuously awarded for his academic achievements. He has worked as a teaching assistant at the Institute of Computer Engineering and Informatics at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje since 2010. His teaching responsibilities include preparing and conducting lectures and laboratory exercises for courses in the fields of computer networks, network operating systems, WAN computer networks, and wireless computer networks. He has participated in several international projects. In his research work to date, he has published two scientific papers and is the co-author of one book. His active fields of scientific interest are: digital system design with HDL, the semantic web, web-based systems, system software, and computer networks.

# Упис на нови студенти на втор циклус на студии за академската година 2025/2026 – ФИНКИ

Dear,

Below are the required documents for the enrollment of admitted students at FINKI for the second cycle of studies for the 2025/2026 academic year.

**Enrollment for new students in the second cycle of studies for the 2025/2026 academic year will take place from October 29 to November 7, 2025. from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM at the FINKI Student Affairs Office.**

**Documents required for enrollment:**

Index

2. 3 photos

3. Proof of citizenship of the Republic of Macedonia (certificate or decision)

4. Extract from the birth register

5. Semester Registration Form

6. **[Internal Application Form](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/interen_prijaven_list_magisterski_2025.pdf)**

7. **[Statistical Form for the Second Cycle of Studies – SW.80A](https://www.stat.mk/media/tq5j5ylk/voi80a_2025.pdf)**

8. Study Contract for **[annual](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/2_ciklus_1_godishni_2025.pdf)**and **[biennial](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/2_ciklus_2024-25.pdf)**studies

9. Proof of payment of the first installment of 900 euros for the one-year studies or 625 euros for the two-year studies, in the Macedonian currency equivalent based on the average exchange rate of the National Bank of the Republic of Macedonia.

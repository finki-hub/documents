# Прелиминарни ранг листи од втор уписен рок за прв циклус студии за академската 2022/2023 година – ФИНКИ

They have been published [Preliminary rankings](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u616/preliminarna-rang-lista-vtor-upisen-rok.pdf) for the second enrollment period for the 2022/2023 academic year.

The submission of [Objection to the preliminary ranking list](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u616/prigovor_za_preliminarna_raspredelba8751.pdf) The selection of applicants for the first admissions period will be conducted from September 20, 2022, at 12:00 PM to September 21, 2022, at 12:00 PM.

Objections are submitted by email. [upisi-finki@ukim.edu.mk](mailto:upisi-finki@ukim.edu.mk) with the message subject: PRIGOVOR[ID], where ID represents the number of the application to which the objection pertains.

All candidates who are NOT ranked have the opportunity to fill out [statement](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u616/izjava_za_dopolnuvanje_ili_promena_na_izbranite_studiski_programi8753.pdf) for ranking the other positions for which there are vacancies. Statements will be accepted by email. [upisi-finki@ukim.edu.mk](mailto:upisi-finki@ukim.edu.mk) with the message subject IZJAVA[ID] by September 21, 2022, by 12:00 PM.

# Dr\. Natasha Ilievska

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/von-prof-d-r-natasha-ilievska--731x1024.jpg)

[natasa.ilievska@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:natasa.ilievska@finki.ukim.mk)

She was born in 1978. She completed her elementary and secondary education in Skopje with excellent results. She enrolled at the Institute of Informatics, applied track, at the Faculty of Natural Sciences and Mathematics in Skopje in the 1996/97 academic year. She graduated in 2001 with a grade point average of 9.94, as the top graduate of her class at the Institute of Informatics, earning a degree as a graduate engineer in informatics. She enrolled in graduate studies in mathematics in the 2002/2003 academic year and completed them in 2009 with the defense of her master's thesis, “Error-Detecting Codes and the Application of Quasigroups for Their Design.” She defended her doctoral dissertation in the field of coding theory in 2015 at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering – FINK.

In the winter semester of the 2001/2002 academic year, she was a teaching assistant; in 2002, she was appointed junior assistant; and in 2009, she became an assistant at the Institute of Informatics at the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics. From 2011 to 2015, she was an assistant at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering – FINK, and in 2015 she was appointed associate professor at the same faculty. She has taught exercises for over twenty subjects from various fields to students at FINK, as well as to students at the Institute of Informatics and the Institute of Mathematics at PMF, Skopje: Discrete Mathematics 1, Discrete Mathematics 2, Probability and Statistics, Discrete Structures 1, Discrete Structures 2, Discrete Structures 3, Probability, Probability Theory, Algebra, Algebraic Discrete Structures, Sets and Logic, Linear Algebra, Vectors and Matrices, Information Theory, Fundamentals of Information Theory, Fundamentals of Programming, Databases, Mathematical Methods for Computer Scientists, Mathematical Statistics, Functions of Several Variables, Real Analysis, Programming of Computing Machines, Computer Laboratory II, Computer Laboratory III. As a lecturer, she teaches Discrete Mathematics 1, Discrete Mathematics 2, and Probability and Statistics to students at FINK. She has written several scripts available to students in electronic form through the respective web pages. She has participated in twenty or so congresses, conferences, workshops, schools, and courses in the country and abroad. She has been a participant in several research projects and is the author of numerous scientific papers.

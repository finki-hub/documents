# EIT Food Waste Challenge Lab 2024 – ФИНКИ

Did you know that waste isn't waste until you waste it?

If you're passionate about transforming food waste into valuable, marketable products, we invite you to join the Food Waste Challenge!

For five weeks (a total of 20 effective hours), with **[EIT Food](https://www.linkedin.com/company/eit-food/)** and **[Build-A-Upper](https://www.linkedin.com/company/bauaccelerator/)** As part of the support, you will participate in a boot camp to learn, innovate, and develop food waste into innovative new products. Work on real challenges presented by industry representatives.

Ready to make a difference? Let's create a future where nothing goes to waste!

Application deadline: October 22nd -&gt; **&lt;https://shorturl.at/wtLbE&gt;**

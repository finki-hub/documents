# Покането предавање: Computational biomedical discovery from spatial omics data – д\-р Јован Таневски – ФИНКИ

Dear colleagues,

It is our special honor and pleasure to invite you to the lecture by Dr. Jovan Tanevski, titled „Computational biomedical discovery from spatial omics data,“ which will be held on April 2, 2026, starting at 5:00 PM in the large amphitheater of the TUMF.

The lecture will be held as a joint event organized by the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering (FINKI), the local IEEE sections, and: The Department of Computers, the Department of Education, Robotics and Automation, Systems, Man and Cybernetics, the Department of Telecommunications, and the Department of Information Theory, as well as the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology (FEIT), the ICT-ACT Association, and the EuroCC2 and CyberMAC projects.

You can find more information about the event, the topic, and the speaker on this [link](https://events.vtools.ieee.org/m/551712 "https://events.vtools.ieee.org/m/551712"). Attendance at the event is free and open to all interested parties, and it is advisable to register on this [link](https://events.vtools.ieee.org/event/register/551712 "https://events.vtools.ieee.org/event/register/551712").

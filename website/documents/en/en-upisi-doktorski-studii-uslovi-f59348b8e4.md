# Услови – ФИНКИ

In accordance with [The Call](http://www.ukim.edu.mk/mk_content.php?meni=152&glavno=8) for the enrollment of students in third-cycle studies in the study programmes of Ss. Cyril and Methodius University in Skopje in the 2022/23 academic year, persons who have completed appropriate study programmes and meet the following basic requirements and criteria have the right to enroll in doctoral studies:

- completed second-cycle studies aligned with the European Credit Transfer System (hereinafter: ECTS credits);
- completed postgraduate studies in study programmes before the introduction of the European Credit Transfer System;
- acquired professional education under study programmes for regulated professions, with at least 300 ECTS credits earned;
- average grade of at least 8.00 (eight) in first-cycle studies and at least 8.00 (eight) in second-cycle studies, or at least 8.00 (eight) for integrated studies;
- knowledge of at least one of the three most commonly used languages of the European Union (English, French, German), proven by a certificate issued by the Blaze Koneski Faculty of Philology at Ss. Cyril and Methodius University in Skopje for level B2 according to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages of the Council of Europe, or by an appropriate certificate for the same level issued by an official European testing provider that is a member of the ALTE association of European language testers.

# Заверка и упис на семестар за втор циклус студии за учебната 2025/2026 – ФИНКИ

Заверката на летниот и уписот на зимскиот семестар за тековните студенти во учебната 2025/2026 година за студентите од II циклус на студии ќе се врши од **28.10.2025** to **07.11.2025** година, заклучно со 07.11.2025, од **09:00** to **12:00 PM** at the Student Service desk of FINKI in the TMF building.

**Required documents for enrolling in the winter semester:**

1. Index
2. Semester Endorsement Form
3. Semester enrollment form (to be submitted by all students who have not yet earned their master's degree, i.e., for enrolling an empty semester)
4. **[Internal Application Form](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/interen_prijaven_list_magisterski_2025.pdf "https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/interen_prijaven_list_magisterski_20207337_1.pdf")** (only for students registering for courses)
5. **[Статистички образец за II циклус на студии – ШВ.80А](https://www.stat.mk/media/tq5j5ylk/voi80a_2025.pdf)**
6. **[Payment slip](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/untitled_9.png "https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/untitled_9.png")** for the next tuition installment, in accordance with the study agreement

For students who are retaking a course, the following is additionally required:

Payment slip (for the value of the credits for courses being retaken according to the study program)
The cost of 1 credit = 1/60 of the value of the study program for one-year programs, and 1/120 for two-year study programs.
Payment is made in the denar equivalent according to the NBRM average exchange rate on the date of payment.

**Рокот за носење на документите е заклучно со 07.11.2025**

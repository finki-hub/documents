# Објавени се прелиминарни ранг листи за вториот уписен рок за академската 2021/2022 година – ФИНКИ

They have been published [Preliminary rankings](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u616/preliminarna-rang-lista.pdf) за вториот уписен рок за академската 2021/2022 година.

The submission of [Objection to the preliminary ranking list](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u616/prigovor_za_preliminarna_raspredelba_0.pdf) на пријавени кандидати во прв уписен рок ќе се врши од 21.09.2021 од 15:00 часот до 22.09.2021 до 15:00 часот.

Objections are submitted by email. [upisi-finki@ukim.edu.mk](mailto:upisi-finki@ukim.edu.mk) with the message subject: PRIGOVOR[ID], where ID represents the number of the application to which the objection pertains.

All candidates who are NOT ranked or who wish to change or supplement their choice of study programs or mode of study (state quota or co-financing) have the opportunity to apply for study programs with available seats and be ranked for a program that is not fully filled. The new ranking will be made without changing the order of the previously ranked candidates. The change of the chosen study program or mode of study is made by filling out the [Declaration for the Supplementation or Change of Selected Study Programs](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u616/izjava_za_dopolnuvanje_ili_promena_na_izbranite_studiski_programi.pdf).

Declarations to supplement or change selected study programs (enrollment in a study program with available seats) or to modify a declaration for studying under a co-financing quota (if this was not done on the internal application form) will be accepted via email. [upisi-finki@ukim.edu.mk](mailto:upisi-finki@ukim.edu.mk) најдоцна до 22.09.2021 до 15:00. Во насловот на пораката треба да стои IZJAVA[ID] каде што ID претставува бројот на пријавата за која се однесува изјавата.

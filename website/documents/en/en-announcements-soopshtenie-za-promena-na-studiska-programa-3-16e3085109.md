# Соопштение за промена на студиска програма – ФИНКИ

All FINKI students who wish to change their study program, in accordance with [the decision](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/Content/contentFiles/studies_menustructure/pravila%20za%20promena%20na%20studiska%20programa.pdf) At the NNS of FINKI, the same can be done from September 2, 2020, to September 10, 2020.

Requests to change a study program are submitted through the application system. &lt;http://molbi.finki.ukim.mk&gt;.

After the application is approved, students should print and sign the document they receive by email and submit it together with the payment receipt for 1000 MKD. [the bill](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/mk/zafakultetot/finansii) to FINKI with a note for changing the study program (in accordance with [price list](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/Content/contentFiles/about_faculty_menustructure/PravniAkti/Cenovnik_FINKI.pdf) to FINKI) to scan and electronically send them to [studentski@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:studentski@finki.ukim.mk) by September 20, 2020.

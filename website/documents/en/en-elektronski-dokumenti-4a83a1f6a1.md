# Упаства за издавање електронски документи – ФИНКИ

**[Electronic Document Depot
(issued last year)](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/depo/)**

##

## A. Certificate of regular student status:

- The student submits a request through the iKnow system for a document.„**Certificate of Regular Student Status – FINKI**“
- Student Affairs is processing the request.
- The completed (notarized) PDF document is automatically sent to the student's student email through the notarization system.

## B. Certificate of passed exams:

- The student submits a request through the iKnow system for a document.„**Certificate of Passed Exams FINKI**“
- The student pays electronically for the document.
  (or)
- The student sends an email to the address. [uverenija@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:uverenija@finki.ukim.mk) with an attached proof of payment from the bank.
- Student Affairs is processing the request.
- The completed (notarized) PDF document is automatically sent to the student's student email through the notarization system.

## V. UPPI:

- The student submits a request through the iKnow system for a document.„**Uppix form**“
- Student Affairs is processing the request.
- The completed (notarized) PDF document is automatically sent to the student's student email through the notarization system.

The digital documents issued in this process are digitally signed (notarized) with a digital seal of FINKI in accordance with the Law on Electronic Documents, Electronic Identification, and Trust Services.
The process of digital signing (notarization), verification, and transparency is guaranteed through the platform. [verify.it](https://www.verify.it/)

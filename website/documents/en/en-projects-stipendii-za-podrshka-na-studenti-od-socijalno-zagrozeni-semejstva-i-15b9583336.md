# Стипендии за подршка на студенти од социјално загрозени семејства и ранливи категории – ФИНКИ

For the past three years, the LEORON Foundation has actively supported students from vulnerable groups enrolled at public universities in the Republic of North Macedonia.

![poster.png](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/poster.png)

Our aim is to reduce the barriers arising from social circumstances that young academics face and that may affect their academic development. We believe that every child has the potential to fulfil their dream. We therefore do our utmost to encourage our students to achieve their dreams through academic success.

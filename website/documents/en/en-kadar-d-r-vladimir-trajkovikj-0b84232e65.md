# Dr\. Vladimir Trajkovikj

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/prof.-d-r-vladimir-trajkovikj--731x1024.jpg)

[vladimir.trajkovik@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:vladimir.trajkovik@finki.ukim.mk)

He was born in 1971 in Skopje. He graduated, received his master's degree, and earned his doctorate from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering in Skopje in 1994, 1997, and 2003, respectively. He has worked at the Institute for Computer Engineering and Informatics at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering in Skopje since 1997 as an assistant. In 2004, 2008, and 2013, he obtained the titles of associate professor, associate dean, and full professor, respectively. He is currently responsible for several undergraduate courses and three graduate courses. He also lectures in graduate programs at the Institute for Sociological, Political, and Legal Research at the University “St. Cyril and Methodius“ in Skopje. He is the author of more than 200 papers published in renowned international scientific journals and conference proceedings. He has participated in more than thirty applied research multilateral and bilateral projects with several universities from the European Union and the USA, within the framework of which he has undertaken numerous research stays. His research focuses on the areas of: distributed computer systems, distance learning support systems, service-oriented architectures, mobile services, and connected health.

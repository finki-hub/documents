| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | Course objectives (competencies):  The course includes the theoretical and practical aspects of managing a software project. It has  to develop an understanding of the theoretical aspects of project management and software development, and  as well as practical knowledge of the necessary skills such as planning, organizing,  Implementation and evaluation of a project.  Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:   to demonstrate the advanced application of ICT project management   to explain the current research trends and the problems in the management of  Projects  - to formulate, execute, and revise a project plan (to recognize the resources, important  moments and risks)  to solve problems with managing and motivating people  - to use advanced techniques and tools for project planning and control  to implement quality control tools during the project's development  to recognize and evaluate methods and standards |
| 11. | Course content | Content of the subject program:  Software life cycle  Planning and allocation  Phased development,  - important moments,  - PERT,  - gantograms,  - WBS - work packages;  Distribution of limited time and resources  - Project oversight (planning the goals, scope, resources, and estimation of  the project, the tools, the techniques)  - Communication (writing and proofreading reports, presentations, team communication,  conflict resolution  Management, leadership, and motivation  - Quality Management – Software Metrics  Process Improvement (SEI CMM Model, SPICE)  - Risk and Change Management  Tools and methods |

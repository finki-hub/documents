# Dr\. Panche Ribarski

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/doc.-d-r-panche-ribarski--731x1024.jpg)

[pance.ribarski@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:pance.ribarski@finki.ukim.mk)

He was born in 1984 in Štip. He completed elementary school through the fourth grade in Radoviš, and then both elementary and high school in Skopje. In 2003, he enrolled in computer science studies at the Faculty of Natural Sciences and Mathematics in Skopje, which he completed in 2008. During the last two years of his studies, he was hired as a teaching assistant at the Institute of Informatics. In 2008, he was appointed as an assistant at the Faculty of Information Technology, MIT in Skopje. In 2009, he was appointed as a research associate at the Institute of Informatics, Faculty of Natural Sciences and Mathematics in Skopje. Within the institute's teaching process, he participates as a collaborator in teaching the following subjects: Data Structures, Network Operating Systems, Security and Cryptography, Wireless and Mobile Technologies, Software Project Management, Software Design and Architecture, Operating Systems, and Software Requirements Analysis. Panche Ribarski's research topics are the application of security and cryptography in software engineering, with a special focus on electronic voting systems. In addition, he has a special interest in the field of mobile technologies and their application and ubiquity in everyday life. Panche Ribarski is the author of several papers in the area of cryptography, electronic security, and electronic voting systems. After graduating, he enrolled in graduate studies in the TEMPUS Software Engineering program. He has completed the requirements for the offered courses and is currently preparing a master's thesis in the field of cryptography and electronic voting systems. He is a member of the Association of Informaticians of Macedonia and is the secretary of the ICT-ACT Association of Skopje.

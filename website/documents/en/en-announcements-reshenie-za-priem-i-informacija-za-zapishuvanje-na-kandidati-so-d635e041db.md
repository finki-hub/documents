# Решение за прием и информација за запишување на кандидати согласно решението за прием во втор уписен рок 2025/2026 – ФИНКИ

Dear candidates, we inform you that the enrollment of ranked candidates according to **[the decision on admission](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/resenie_priem2025.pdf)**The second enrollment period will take place on **Switch in Student Services** (at the same place where the documents were submitted) per day **22.09** and **23.09.2025**

We wish all future students of FINKI success!

Ranked candidates must enroll during these deadlines and submit all required documents in full. Otherwise, they will forfeit their place, and if they wish to enroll at FINKI again, they will have to apply during the third enrollment period from scratch, provided there are still open spots.

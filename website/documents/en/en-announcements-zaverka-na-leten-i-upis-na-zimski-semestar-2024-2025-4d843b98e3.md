# Заверка на летен и упис на зимски семестар 2024/2025 – ФИНКИ

Dear Students,

The summer semester will conclude and the winter semester will begin. **from September 17, 2024 to September 24, 2024**. You can view the elective subjects that will be activated on the following **[LINK](https://raspredelba.finki.ukim.mk/active-subjects?name=&semesterType=WINTER&results=1000)**.

Information on financial obligations is available at the following **[LINK](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/mk/zafakultetot/finansii)**.

A further notice will follow regarding the submission of the statistical form and obtaining the stamp in the index.

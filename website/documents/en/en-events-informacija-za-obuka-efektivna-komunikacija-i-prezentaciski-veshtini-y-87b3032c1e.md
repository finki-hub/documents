# Информација за обука: Ефективна комуникација и презентациски вештини \| Youth Empowered – ФИНКИ

Dear all,

The Educational Center of Pivara Skopje is marking its thirtieth anniversary this year, with significant achievements in non-formal education and skills development among young people, skills that are necessary and applicable in the labor market in our country. In the past ten years alone, more than 10,000 young people have been supported in developing their personal and professional skills, contributing to their career and entrepreneurial development.

Continuing this successful work, the Educational Center is implementing the new phase of “Youth Empowered – Pivara Skopje Academy for Youth” as a modern learning platform that, through interactive trainings and webinars, mentoring and practical tools, enables the acquisition of key skills for personal and professional development.

Following the successful start of the new cycle, we are pleased to invite you to be part of the next training from the “Youth Empowered” Academy, dedicated to one of the most important skills for success today

“Effective Communication and Presentation Skills”

April 30 (Thursday)
14:00
FINKI / Amphitheater

This training will help you to:

 ● express your thoughts clearly, in a structured and persuasive way
 ● present with greater confidence in front of an audience
 ● create presentations that attract attention and are memorable
 ● overcome stage fright and handle real communication situations

The training includes practical exercises, mini-presentations and interactive work through which you will gain experience that you can immediately apply – at the Faculty, at an interview or in a professional environment.

Participation in the training is free of charge, and the number of places is limited.

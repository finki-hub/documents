| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | This course aims to prepare students to understand new business models, risks,  business processes and return on investment in the cloud. |
| 11. | Course content | The concept of „Everything as a Service“ (XaaS) in cloud computing. Open-source clouds and  Commercial clouds. Layers of cloud services. Modeling business, economics, and return.  on the cloud investment. Business analysis and consulting. Sales, negotiation, and marketing.  Cloud risk analysis and management. Management of autonomous business processes in the cloud.  Quality management and service level agreements. Proper modeling of pricing and for  for the supplier and for the client. Provider selection. Migration of the company's services to the cloud.  SLA. Guaranteed Service Level. Pricing modeling for PaaS via IaaS. Pricing modeling  for SaaS via PaaS and IaaS |

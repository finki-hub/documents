# QA Game Tester – Internship Position – ФИНКИ

---

## Description

Location: Skopje, North Macedonia
Type: On-site, Entry Level

At Indium Play, games aren't just entertainment—they're a way of life. Our mission is to deliver high-quality gaming experiences through relentless testing, player empathy, and technical curiosity. We're passionate about identifying bugs before players do and helping developers bring their best version to the world.
We work with some of the biggest names in the gaming industry, ensuring every release meets the highest standards for quality, performance, and fun.

Job Description
Are you passionate about video games? Ready to turn your hobby into a career?

Join our QA Game Testing team in Skopje, where you'll be part of an exciting, fast-paced, and supportive environment. No previous QA or testing experience is required – just a strong desire to learn, grow, and play a critical role in delivering top-tier games.

As a QA Game Tester, you will:
Play and test video games across various platforms and genres.
Report bugs, glitches, and gameplay issues clearly and accurately.
Follow test plans and provide insightful feedback.
Collaborate with international QA and development teams.
Learn about quality assurance in the gaming industry and grow your career.

Qualifications
What makes a great fit:
- Avid gamer who enjoys exploring different genres and platforms.
Detail-oriented with a knack for spotting inconsistencies others may miss.
A quick learner with a strong sense of curiosity and adaptability.
Tech-savvy and comfortable working with different software and platforms.
Natural problem solver with strong analytical thinking.
Able to follow structured processes while using critical thinking.
Excellent spoken and written English for global team collaboration.
Team player who thrives in a fast-paced, multicultural environment.

Additional Information
Full onboarding and QA fundamentals training provided.

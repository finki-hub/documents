| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | Introduction to the fundamentals of data-driven science. Students will be introduced to the process and methodology of working with data, starting with problem identification, followed by data collection, and then processing. Students will learn the basic techniques for data processing and pattern identification, as well as methods for visualizing and interpreting the results obtained. |

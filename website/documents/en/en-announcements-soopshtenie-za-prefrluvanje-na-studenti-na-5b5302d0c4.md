# Соопштение за Префрлување на Студенти на ФИНКИ – ФИНКИ

**Announcement on Student Transfers to FINKI**

**Dear Students,**

Факултетот за информатички науки и компјутерско инженерство (ФИНКИ) го отвора процесот за префрлување на студенти од други факултети за академската 2024/2025 година. Сите заинтересирани студенти треба да го достават своето барање најдоцна до **31 август 2025 година**.

**Required Documents:**

1. **Transfer Application**
   - Please ensure that you provide your contact telephone number and email address.
   - You can download the application form template [**here**](https://finkiukim-my.sharepoint.com/:w:/g/personal/webadmin_finki_ukim_mk/EaSXkYfSQ3RCivg5LaoKGbEB9Qr-8FAmHzM213NJFlKJsA?e=YLIeUd).
2. **Transcript of Passed Examinations**
   - Issued by the faculty at which you were previously enrolled.
3. **Curricula**
   - Detailed information on the curricula of the previously completed courses.
4. **Payment slip**
   - An amount of **MKD 3,000** paid to the FINKI account, with the payment reference “transfer from another faculty” (in accordance with the [FINKI fee schedule](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cenovnik_finki_2024-25-2.pdf)).
   - An example of the payment slip is available [**here**](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/Content/contentImages/about_faculty_menustructure/Finansii/finki-uplatnica-pp50.png).
   - A payment of **MKD 400** per recognized course, payable upon semester enrollment after receiving the decision on course equivalency.

**Transfer Requirements:**

- **Students from UKIM faculties:**
  - They will continue their studies under the same enrollment quota from which they are transferring.
- **Students from other universities:**
  - They will be able to continue their studies under the co-financing quota.

**Поднесувањето на документите се врши електронски со испраќање на потребните документи на**

The Transfer Committee will review all submitted applications and notify the candidates of the results. The results will also be published on the FINKI website.

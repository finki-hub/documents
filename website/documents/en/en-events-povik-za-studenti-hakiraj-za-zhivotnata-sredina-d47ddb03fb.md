# ПОВИК ЗА СТУДЕНТИ Хакирај за животната средина – ФИНКИ

Apply to participate in the innovation camp dedicated to the Balkan lynx localization challenge, where you will have the opportunity to work in a team of five over 48 hours to develop the most innovative solution. The winning team will receive 120,000 denars to implement their solution.

Further information is available at [the link](https://innocamps.lead.org.mk/?page_id=2975)

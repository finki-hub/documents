| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be familiar with the evolution of learning theories and will be able to understand the role and new trends in e-learning environments, as well as the transition from traditional to e-learning environments. Through examples of applications of video games, massive open online courses, remote and virtual laboratories, and virtual classrooms, students will become familiar with the possibilities of using ICT to support education. Students will acquire the ability to offer appropriate existing systems and tools for learning management and instructional content, as well as systems and tools for knowledge assessment for given business requirements. |
| 11. | Course content | Lectures:  1. Introduction to the course. Basic concepts of e-learning, m-learning, e-learning 2.0  2. Learning models/theories: Behaviorism  3. Learning models/theories: Cognitivism, constructivism...  4. Instructional Design  5. Testing and Assessment Models  6. Application of ICT in Education  7. Course management and instructional materials from the user's perspective  8. Course management and instructional materials from a teacher's perspective  9. Examples of e-learning systems and tools in various fields. Systems and tools for knowledge assessment.  10. Massive Open Online Courses, Remote and Virtual Laboratories, and Virtual Classrooms in Education  11.

# Услови – ФИНКИ

In accordance with [The Call](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/konkurs-ii-2026-27-mk.pdf) For the enrollment of students in graduate studies programs at the University „St. Cyril and Methodius“ for the 2026/2027 academic year, candidates who meet the following general conditions are eligible to apply:

- completed first-cycle studies in appropriate or related studies;
- to have earned **240 ECTS credits** if applying for **one-year master’s studies**;
- to have earned **180 ECTS credits** if applying for **two-year master’s studies**.

In addition to the general requirements, candidates must also meet the specific requirements set by the study programme for which they are applying, if such requirements are provided.

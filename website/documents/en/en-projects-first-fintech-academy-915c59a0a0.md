# First Fintech Academy – ФИНКИ

Are you looking for new and interesting challenges? Would you like to explore something new? Do you know what fintech is?

Join the first free Fintech Academy in North Macedonia, organised by young enthusiasts with support from leading industry experts. Develop your skills and knowledge in finance and technology and become the next innovator to create a new fintech solution!

Places are limited! Apply promptly using the link in the QR code on the poster by 19 October 2022. All selected participants will be informed by email in due time about the start of the Academy.

For any questions, please write to us by email ([fintech.macedonia@gmail.com](mailto:fintech.macedonia@gmail.com)) or visit our Facebook and LinkedIn pages.

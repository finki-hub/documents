# Професори од ФИНКИ во листата на најдобрите 2% научници во светот – ФИНКИ

According to the latest announcement from [Elsevier Data Repository](https://elsevier.digitalcommonsdata.com/) published on [the link](https://elsevier.digitalcommonsdata.com/datasets/btchxktzyw/6), our professors Prof. [Dr. Biljana Risteska Stojkoska](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/mk/staff/biljana-stojkoska) , Prof. [Dr. Marjan Gushev](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/mk/staff/marjan-gushev) and [Academician Dr. Ljupcho Kocarev](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/mk/staff/ljupcho-kocarev) are on the list of the world's top 2% scientists by the number of citations of their publications for 2023.

At the same time, Prof. Dr. Marjan Gušev enters the list of the world's top 2% scientists in terms of publications and citations over his entire career.

![vnatre.jpg](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/vnatre.jpg)

These successes once again confirm FINKI's commitment to the advancement of science.

We wish our professors even greater successes in the future.

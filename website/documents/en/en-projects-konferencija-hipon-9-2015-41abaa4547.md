# Конференција во рамките на ХИПОН – ФИНКИ

„**E-modules for Histopathology: a significant online tool for students, researchers, and professionals – HIPON**“is a project co-financed by **The Lifelong Learning Programme of the Executive Agency for Audiovisual and Cultural Education of the European Commission**, led by Prof. Dr. Andreas C. Lazaris from the Faculty of Medicine of the National Kapodistrian University of Athens, with the following partners: the Faculty of Medicine of the University of Zagreb, Croatia, the Radboud University Medical Center from the Netherlands, the Center for Advanced Research and Development in Educational Technology (CARDET) from Cyprus, and the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering of the University „St. Cyril and Methodius“ from Macedonia.

 Објавени се избраните апстракти и деталната програма  конференцијата и работилницата ‘IMPROVING PATHOLOGY TEACHING” која ќе се одржи во Атина од 22ри до 23ти октомври 2015 година.  Деталите за програмата и примените абстракти може да ги најдете на следниот [link](http://beta.finki.ukim.mk/Content/contentFiles/news/HIPON%20-%20Improving%20Pathology%20Teaching%20-%203rd%20circular%20announcement.pdf).

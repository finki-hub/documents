| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to independently think strategically about a company, its current business position, its long-term direction, its resources and competitive capabilities, to formulate strategies and achieve a sustainable competitive advantage; to acquire skills for conducting strategic analysis in various industries and competitive situations; to develop a business strategy, carefully considering strategic opportunities and making sound strategic decisions; to understand the managerial tasks that arise from implementing the company's strategy; to understand the need for strategic management of the business; to know how to assess risks to make sound decisions and achieve effective results. |
| 11. | Course content | Introduction to the concept of strategic management, the context in which strategic management takes place, conducting an external analysis and assessment of opportunities and threats, conducting an internal analysis and assessment of strengths and weaknesses, functional strategies, competitive strategies, corporate strategies, international strategies, strategies for entrepreneurial ventures and non-profit organizations, technology and strategy, strategic management of ICT, strategy execution, strategy evaluation and control, managers and strategic management. |

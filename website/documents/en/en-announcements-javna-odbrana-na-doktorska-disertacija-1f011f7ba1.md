# Јавна одбрана на докторска дисертација – ФИНКИ

The University „St. Cyril and Methodius“ – Skopje, Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering announces that on January 10, 2023, at 2:00 PM, in the Amphitheater of FINK and via the Faculty's electronic system (&lt;https://meet.google.com/ovx-bsgz-xsh&gt;), a public defense of the doctoral dissertation „Representation Learning-Based Methodologies for Natural Language Processing“ submitted by candidate Mr. Kostadin Mishev, mentor Prof. Dr. Dimitar Trajanov, submitted to the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering – Skopje.

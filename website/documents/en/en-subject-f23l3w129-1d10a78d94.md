| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | The goal of the course is for students to become familiar with modern mobile platforms and to learn how to program and develop mobile applications. They will learn to handle the constraints of mobile devices and to develop efficient programs. They will also learn how to publish the applications they create on the market and to monitor their usage. |
| 11. | Course content | (1) Introduction and trends in mobile application development (Android)  (2) Mobile Application Architecture  (1) Configuring mobile applications  (1) Resource management  (1) User interface concepts for mobile devices  (1) Development process for mobile applications  (1) Planning the user experience for the mobile application  (1) Development of compatible mobile applications  (1) Mobile application testing  (1) Publishing the market  (2) Mobile web |

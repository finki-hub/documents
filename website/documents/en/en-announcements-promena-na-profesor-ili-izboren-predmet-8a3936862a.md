# Промена на професор или изборен предмет – ФИНКИ

On **[the application system](http://molbi.finki.ukim.mk/)** Requests to change a professor have been enabled. The deadline for these requests is by the end of **03.03.2024**.

Additionally, if you need to change an elective subject, you can request it through **[Next form](https://forms.office.com/e/vEYJCBDXXS)**. The deadline for changing a subject is by the end of **03.03.2024.**

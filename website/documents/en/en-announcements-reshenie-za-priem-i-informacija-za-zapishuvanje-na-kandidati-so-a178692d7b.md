# Решение за прием и информација за запишување на кандидати согласно решението за прием во трет уписен рок 2023/2024 – ФИНКИ

Dear candidates, we inform you that the enrollment of ranked candidates according to **[the decision on admission](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/3-rok-finalna_rang_lista.pdf)**  и во третиот уписен рок ќе се изврши на **29.09.2023** at the student services counter from **9:00 AM to 12:00 PM** the hour.

We wish all future students of FINKI success!

**Required Documentation for Enrolling New Students for the 2023/2024 Academic Year**
1. **[Statement](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/izjava_0.pdf)** for submitted documents
2. Semester registration form (with attached photo), completed and signed (purchased from the bookstore)
 2.1. **[List](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/predmeti1semestar_2.pdf)** on items according to the guidelines that are filled out in the form.
3. Application form from the State Statistical Office of the Republic of Macedonia (form Voi. 20) (available for purchase at a bookstore)
4.a. Candidates admitted to **state quota** Submit proof of payment for the first installment of **One hundred euros** in denar equivalent at the central rate of the National Bank of the Republic of North Macedonia (**[Example payment slip](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/finki-uplatnica-pp50-irata-100evra_1.png)**)
 4.a.1. **[Contract](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/dogovor_za_studiranje_vo_drzhavna_kvota_3.pdf)** for studying in the state quota in two copies
4.b. Candidates in **co-financed quota** Submit proof of payment for the first installment of **200 euros** in denar equivalent at the central rate of the National Bank of the Republic of North Macedonia (**[Example payment slip](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/finki-uplatnica-pp50-irata-200evra_0.png)**), as well as
 4.b.1. **[Statement](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/izjava_za_studiranje_so_kofinansiranje_na_finki_1.pdf)** for consent to submit the study costs
 4.b.2. **[Contract](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/dogovor_za_studiranje_vo_kvota_so_kofinansiranje_4.pdf)** for studying in a co-funded quota, in two copies
4.b. Candidates admitted to **Professional Studies in Programming** Submit proof of payment for the first installment of **300 euros**in denar equivalent at the central rate of the National Bank of the Republic of North Macedonia (**[Example payment slip](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/finki-uplatnica-pp50-irata-300evra_.png)**), as well as
 4.b.1. **[Statement](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/izjava_za_studiranje_-_ssp.pdf)** for consent to submit the study costs
 4.b.2. **[Contract](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/dogovor_za_studiranje_-_ssp_0.pdf)** for studying SSP in two samples
5. Proof of payment for **750.00 denars** to the FINKI account for administrative and material expenses and insurance.**[Example payment slip](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/finki-uplatnica-pp50-750den-finki_1.png)**)
6. Proof of payment **750.00 denars** – for informational, informational, cultural, and sports activities of the students of the University “St. Cyril and Methodius” in Skopje, with the designation Decision of UKIM for IKSA, FINKI, (**[Example payment slip](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/finki-uplatnica-pp50-750-ukim_0.png)**).
7. Students who wish to grant a third party access to personal documents and documents from their student file must complete and submit **[Statement](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/izjava_za_pristap_do_informacii_i_dokumenti_od_treti_lica_na_finki.pdf)** for access to information and documents from third parties on FINKI
8. Index card (with a photo attached) and student ID card (with a photo attached), completed without an index number (purchased from the bookstore)

Candidates who do not enroll or withdraw from enrollment at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering for the 2023/2024 academic year can submit their documents. **повлечат до 06.10.2023 (09:00 – 12:00 часот)**. In doing so, it is necessary to sign. [**statement**](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/izjava_za_podignuvanje_na_dokumentite_1.pdf) for submitting the documents in 2 copies.

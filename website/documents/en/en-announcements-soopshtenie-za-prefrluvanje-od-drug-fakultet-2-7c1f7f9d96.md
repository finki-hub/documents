# Соопштение за префрлување од друг факултет – ФИНКИ

All students who wish to continue their studies at FINKI can do so no later than **10.09.2022** year.

**Required Documents:**

Transfer Application

Transcript of Passed Examinations

Curricula

[Payment slip](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/Content/contentImages/about_faculty_menustructure/Finansii/finki-uplatnica-pp50.png) to the FINKI account for 2000 MKD, with the designation for transfer from another faculty (in accordance with [price list](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/Content/contentFiles/about_faculty_menustructure/PravniAkti/Cenovnik_FINKI.pdf) on Finki)

**Conditions:**

Students transferring from the faculties of UКИМ continue to study under the same quota from which they transferred.

Students from other universities can continue their studies under a quota with co-financing.

Поднесувањето на документите се врши електронски, односно сите потребни документи скенирани се праќаат на [arhiva@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:arhiva@finki.ukim.mk) до 10.09.2022 година.

Хартиени документи ќе се доставуваат по конечност на решенијата.

Комисија за префрлање ќе ги разгледа сите пристигнати барања и ќе изготви решенија за префрлање. Сите студенти кои ќе добијат позитивно решение и кои се согласуваат со истото, ќе треба да се испишат од матичните факултети и запишат на ФИНКИ, во периодот до 24.09.2022.

**Upon enrollment, students should bring:**

Birth documents (certificates, diplomas, birth certificate, and certificate of citizenship)

Transcript from previous college

Application for Enrollment (Form A1)

Index and two pictures

[Payment slip](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/Content/contentImages/about_faculty_menustructure/Finansii/finki-uplatnica-pp50.png) to the FINKI account in the amount of 400 MKD for each of the recognized items according to the decision (in accordance with [price list](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/Content/contentFiles/about_faculty_menustructure/PravniAkti/Cenovnik_FINKI.pdf) at FINKY) (for example, for 3 recognized courses, you need to pay 3×400 MKD, i.e., 1200 MKD). An exception to this are students transferring to FINKY from PMF – Informatics Studies or FEIT, as their courses are mapped to the same curricula at FINKY.

After FINKI processes the index, students will need to register for the winter semester within the designated period.

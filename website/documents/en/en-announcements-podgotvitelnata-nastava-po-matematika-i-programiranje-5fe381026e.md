# Подготвителната настава по математика и програмирање – ФИНКИ

Dear Students,

We would like to inform you that preparatory math classes will begin on Friday, September 16, 2022, and will run for 6 days:

Friday, September 16

Monday, September 19

Tuesday, September 20

Wednesday, September 21

Thursday, September 22

Friday, September 23

Every day, classes will start at 9 a.m. and will end no later than 4 p.m., with a longer break for lunch.

In-person instruction will take place in the FINKI amphitheater, in the annex opposite the Faculty of Technology and Metallurgy building.

You will be able to follow the online classes via Teams using your high school accounts or any other user account you have. The link will be sent in an email to registered students.

The preparatory programming course will be held from September 26, 2022 (Monday) to September 30, 2022 (Friday). You will receive the detailed schedule in the coming days.

Each registered candidate will be able to attend classes in the mode they selected upon registration, i.e., in person, through FINKI's distance learning systems (online), or a combination of both.

In order to attend the preparatory course, you must submit a payment receipt for the appropriate amount.

– 2000 denars for preparatory programming instruction (if you are only taking programming)

– 4000 denars for preparatory math classes (if you are only taking math)

– 6000 denars for preparatory instruction in programming and mathematics (if you have selected both courses)

The payment slip (available[here](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/mk/zafakultetot/finansii "https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/mk/zafakultetot/finansii")) should contain the following information:

Inquiry purpose: preparatory course in programming/mathematics/programming and mathematics (depending on which course you will be taking)

Recipient title: RM Budget – UKIМ – Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering, Skopje

Recipient's bank: NBRM

Recipient's account: 100000000063095

Budget user account: 1600161700 788 13

Income code and program: 723012 41

You need to bring the payment slip to the first math class if you've chosen math or both courses, or to the first programming class if you've chosen only programming. Additionally, take a photo of or scan the payment slip and send it electronically to the email address. [podgotvitelna@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:podgotvitelna@finki.ukim.mk) by 4:00 PM on Friday, September 16, 2022. For those who have registered to attend the preparatory course online or in a hybrid format, it is sufficient to initially send a photo or scan of the payment receipt to the email address. by 4:00 PM on Friday, September 16, 2022, and they will then be notified separately.the procedure and the deadline for submitting the paper copy of the payment slip.

For any questions, you can write to us at [podgotvitelna@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:podgotvitelna@finki.ukim.mk).

We wish you a successful teaching.

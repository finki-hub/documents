# Прв академски час – ФИНКИ

Dear Students,

We inform you that on October 2, 2023, at 6:00 PM, the first academic hour for the 2023/2024 academic year of the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering at the University will be held at the Philharmonic of the Republic of North Macedonia. „St. Cyril and Methodius“ in Skopje.

All newly enrolled students are invited to the first academic lecture.

Classes begin on Tuesday, October 3, 2023, according to a schedule that will be posted on our website.

# Водич за текот на уписите на ФИНКИ – ФИНКИ

### **Dear Prospective Students,**

Enrollment will take place in the **new Main Amphitheater** at FINKI. To ensure that the process is carried out smoothly and in an organized manner, please follow the guidelines below:

---

#### **1. Entrance to the Building**

- Entry to the amphitheater is through the **side entrance facing the former outdoor sports court**.
- There is no sign on the door, but you will be able to identify it by its appearance, as shown in the image below.

[Exact Location of the Entrance](https://maps.app.goo.gl/YFoeZDTgDbC48RyZA)

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/upisi2026-1.png)

#### **2. Submission of Documents in the Amphitheater**

- You may be greeted by members of our team at the entrance for a **quick preliminary check of your documents**.
- Documents must be submitted **inside the amphitheater**, directly to the Faculty staff.
- Please follow the **marked arrows** throughout the area for guidance and orderly movement.

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/upisi2026-2.png)

#### **3. Exit Upon Completion of the Enrollment Process**

- Upon completion of the enrollment procedure, please exit the amphitheater through the **second door**, i.e. the **exit located opposite the entrance**.
- Movement through the area naturally leads toward this exit, and the route is additionally **marked with arrows and directional signs**.
- Please follow the instructions provided by the Faculty staff on site to ensure an orderly and efficient exit process.

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/upisi2026-3.png)

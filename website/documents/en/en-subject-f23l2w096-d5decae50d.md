| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | The goal of the course is to learn the digitization process and techniques. Students should master the basic principles of managing digital materials and their preservation, as well as the techniques and technologies for presenting them. |
| 11. | Course content | Lectures:  1. Introduction to Digitization  2. Planning a Digitization Project  3. Copyright  4. Metadata  5. Digitization of images by scanning  6. Digitization of textual data. Optical Character Recognition (OCR)  7. Digitization of audio recordings  8. Digitization of video recordings  9. Digitization of 3D objects  10. Digital Libraries and Archiving Software  11. Digital Preservation  12. Presentation of digitized records    Practical Classes:  1. Basic concepts of digitization  2. Project management for digitization needs  3. Copyright  4. Development of various metadata standards  5. Image Digitization Techniques. Image Quality  6. Algorithms and techniques for text digitization. Optical character recognition  7. Image digitization techniques. Audio recording quality parameters. Audio formats and compression  8. Techniques for digitizing video recordings. Video quality parameters. Video formats, containers, encoders, and compression.  9. Techniques for digitizing 3D objects. Formats and interpretation  10. Digital Libraries and Archiving Software  11. Techniques for preserving digitized files  12. Methods and standards for the presentation of digitized data |

| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | Applied Machine Learning teaches students some of the principal ideas in machine learning and data science, taking them from a real business problem to a functional AI solution deployed at scale.  The primary focus is on building real-world AI solutions by applying the skills acquired in the first semester. The emphasis is on practical knowledge rather than on mathematical and theoretical foundations. In balancing theory and practice, priority will be given to the practical and applied aspects of machine learning. |

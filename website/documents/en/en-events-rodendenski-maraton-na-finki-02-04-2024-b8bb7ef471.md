# Роденденски Маратон на ФИНКИ – 02\.04\.2024 – ФИНКИ

On the occasion of **Finney's 13th birthday**, on **April 2, 2024 (Tuesday)**, with **Starts at 10 o'clock**, will be held **birthday race**.

The cross-country race route is attached., **The start is in the old part of the City Park.**, on the Vardar embankment (**&lt;https://maps.app.goo.gl/PHaVSFXgNGhVDjjSA&gt;**to the entrance of the Government of the North Macedonia Republic as a symbolic act with which we want to highlight and raise awareness of the problem of the lack of adequate educational space for the students of FINKI.

Finishing the race in front of the Government will mark the start of a peaceful protest, where we will show that the number of affected students is large and that the demand for improved conditions is urgent and necessary.

On April 2, 2024, due to the race and the peaceful protest, there will be no classes for all FINK students.

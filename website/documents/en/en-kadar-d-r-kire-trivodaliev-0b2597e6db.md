# Dr\. Kire Trivodaliev

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/kire.trivodaliev.jpg)

[kire.trivodaliev@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:kire.trivodaliev@finki.ukim.mk)

He was born in Strumica in 1982. He graduated from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering in Skopje in 2006 and obtained his master’s degree from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technologies in Skopje in 2008. Throughout his education, he regularly received awards for academic achievement. As the best student of his generation, he was also recognised by Ss. Cyril and Methodius University and by other organisations and foundations, including the Engineering Institution of Macedonia, which awarded him the Golden Engineering Ring. He began working as a demonstrator at the Institute of Computer Science and Engineering within the Faculty of Electrical Engineering in Skopje in 2005. He was appointed a junior teaching assistant in 2007 and a teaching assistant in 2010. His teaching responsibilities have included practical classes in databases, artificial intelligence, data mining, data-analysis algorithms, logic and functional programming, and biocybernetics. As an author or co-author, he has contributed to more than 15 papers published in reputable international scientific journals and conference proceedings. His research interests focus on bioinformatics, computational biology, artificial intelligence, data mining, data-analysis algorithms and databases.

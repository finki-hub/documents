# Конкурс за стипендирање на 12 студенти 2022/23 во прва година – ФИНКИ

For the 2022/2023 academic year, the EDUKATA Foundation will award scholarships to full-time students enrolled in first-cycle undergraduate studies at public universities and higher education institutions in the Republic of North Macedonia.

More information can be found at the following [link](https://educata.mk/%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BD%D0%BA%D1%83%D1%80%D1%81-%D0%B7%D0%B0-%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B8%D0%BF%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B4%D0%B8%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%9A%D0%B5-%D0%BD%D0%B0-12-%D1%81%D1%82%D1%83%D0%B4%D0%B5%D0%BD%D1%82%D0%B8-2022-2/).

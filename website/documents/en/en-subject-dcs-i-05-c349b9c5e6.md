| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | The principal objective of coding theory is to identify codes that ensure rapid and  accurate transmission over a noisy channel. Different codes are optimal for different  applications. The purpose of the course is to introduce the fundamental error-detecting and  error-correcting codes and their practical applications. |
| 11. | Course content | - Introduction to error-correcting codes and their applications.  - Linear codes. Encoding and decoding of linear codes.  - Hamming codes. Cyclic codes.  - Reed–Muller codes and Reed–Solomon codes.  - Error-detecting codes and CRC codes. |

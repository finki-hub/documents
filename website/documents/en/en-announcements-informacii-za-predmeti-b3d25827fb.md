# Информации за предмети – ФИНКИ

An error was made on the website and in the course codes and levels for the courses: F23L1S026 Marketing and F23L2S066 Fundamentals of Cybersecurity.

Specifically, the subject **F23L1S026 Marketing** е **Level 2** and has a prerequisite for enrollment of a minimum of 36 credits earned, and the course **F23L2S066 Fundamentals of Cybersecurity** is from **Level 1** and there is no prerequisite.

The changes have been appropriately updated on the website.

| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | Personalized learning is a modern approach to instruction that meets the individual needs of each student. Students are equipped to recognize the specific ways in which elementary and secondary school students acquire knowledge. Students will learn how to meet the challenge of incorporating personalized learning into the traditional classroom and how to foster the acquisition of new knowledge among different learning styles. |

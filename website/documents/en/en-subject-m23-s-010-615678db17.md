| 11. | Course content | Energy-saving solutions. Methodologies for measuring energy efficiency. Measures and  Energy efficiency metrics. Thermal and energy monitoring. Energy efficiency.  on the hard drive.  Managing power consumption of servers in a data center.  Cloud computing as an ecological and cost-effective future for IT. Drawbacks of the cloud.  Energy-efficient resource allocation.  Optimization of IT equipment through server virtualization. Advantages and challenges of  Virtualization for a „green“ data center.  Environment, air, and cooling optimization. Modern cooling methods. Modeling and  Design of an energy-efficient data center.  Development of a business plan for ecological virtualization. |

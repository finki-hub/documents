# Petar Sekuloski

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/petar-sekuloski-demonstrator-731x1024.jpg)

[petar.sekuloski@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:petar.sekuloski@finki.ukim.mk)

Petar Sekulovski was born in 1994 in Struga. In 2013, he enrolled at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering, in the Computer Science and Engineering program. He graduated in 2017, earning the degree of Bachelor of Informatics and Computer Engineering, in the field of Computer Science and Engineering. He has been a student in the graduate studies at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering since 2017, in the Computer Science program. At the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering, he was first employed as a lab assistant, and since October 2018, as a teaching assistant. His involvement primarily covers the mathematics courses. He conducts in-class exercises for Calculus, Calculus 1, Calculus 2, Discrete Mathematics, and Discrete Structures 1, as well as laboratory exercises for these courses. He also teaches/has taught laboratory exercises for the courses: Probability and Statistics, Engineering Mathematics, Calculus 3, Structured Programming, Object-Oriented Programming, Geographic Information Systems, and Computer Components. His research interests include topological data analysis, computational topology, machine learning, as well as the application of methods from algebraic topology in the fields of computer science.

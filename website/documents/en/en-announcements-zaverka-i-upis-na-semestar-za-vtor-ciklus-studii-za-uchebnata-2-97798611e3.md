# Заверка и упис на семестар за втор циклус студии за учебната 2022/2023 – ФИНКИ

The summer semester certification and enrollment for the winter semester of the 2022/2023 academic year for second-cycle students will be conducted from **25.10.2022** to **28.10.2022** year. The full statement is available at this [link](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u616/zaverka-upis-vtor-ciklus-2022-23.pdf).

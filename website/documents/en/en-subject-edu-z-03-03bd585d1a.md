| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | The goal of the course is for listeners to become familiar with modern approaches to creating educational digital content and integrating it into online courses.  Listeners will acquire the following competencies: understanding the structure, functions, and significance of digital content across various media; understanding the technologies and strategies for developing and distributing digital content; and the ability to design and develop digital content for effective use. |
| 11. | Course content | Learning content for the future: analysis of successful applications. Learning theories. Design of learning content and its integration into online courses. Technological standards for digital content. Learning objects. |

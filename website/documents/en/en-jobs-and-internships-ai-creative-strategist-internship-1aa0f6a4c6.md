# AI Creative Strategist Internship – ФИНКИ

---

## Description

AI Creative Strategist Internship at Advertisable AI
(Full-time opportunity for top performers)
The Role
From week one, you'll be creating real video ads for real clients using AI—and owning them end to end.
What you'll do day to day:
Create AI ads - Generate video and image creatives from product links and briefs using AI tools: hooks, scripts, AI creators, the works.
Edit and polish - Cut, refine, and ship variations until the creative actually converts.
Talk to clients - Jump on calls, understand what they're selling, gather brand assets, and translate their goals into creative that performs.
Communicate clearly - Manage feedback, turn revisions around fast, and keep clients in the loop.
Test and iterate – spin up multiple angles, see what wins, and use the results to make the next batch sharper.
Who We're Looking For
We want AI power users – people who already live in these tools and get excited about what's coming next.
You'll thrive here if you:
Use AI daily with strong instincts for prompting image, video, and copy models. You know the tools and tricks, and you pick up new ones fast.
Have real creative taste – you can tell the difference between an ad that gets scrolled past and one that stops the thumb.
Move fast and ship – you'd rather make ten versions and test them than overthink one.
Communicate well - you can talk to a client, take feedback gracefully, and explain your creative choices.
Are genuinely curious about advertising - what makes people click, buy, and share.
We care about what you can make and how fast you can learn.
What You'll Get
Hands-on experience building ads at the forefront of AI creative.
Mentorship from a team shipping production ads with AI at scale.
The opportunity to earn a full-time offer.
Send us your CV, show us your best work, and come build the future of advertising with us.

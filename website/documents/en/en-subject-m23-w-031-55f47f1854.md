| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | This course offers professors  Strategies and ideas for incorporating multimedia projects into teaching. By doing so  will utilize the numerous advantages that the creation of multimedia  projects encourage in students, such as cooperative learning, the development of multiple  types of intelligence and constructiveness. The main goal is to learn how  for the professor to manage the process of creating multimedia projects from  The students' page. |
| 11. | Course content | Impact of multimedia projects in  Student learning. A model for designing and developing multimedia projects.  Planning multimedia projects. Organizing a multimedia project.,  A way of grouping students and encouraging cooperation. Management  on cognitive and research activities. Design of a multimedia project through  target audience selection and project planning. Development of multimedia  Components: graphics, animations, audio, and digital video. Authorization of  Multimedia project. Project evaluation: techniques and strategies for assessment and  Evaluation. Types of projects that can be developed. Review of  More scenarios for holding classes. |
| 12. | Learning methods | Lectures. Discussion between students and the instructor about scenarios for organizing various multimedia projects that could be developed by the students. Preparation of a seminar paper and a project. |

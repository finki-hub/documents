# Прв академски час – ФИНКИ

Dear Students,

We inform you that on **02.10.2023** year in **18:00** The first academic lecture for the 2023/2024 academic year at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering of the University „St. Cyril and Methodius“ in Skopje will be held at the Philharmonic of the Republic of North Macedonia.

All newly enrolled students are invited to the first academic lecture.

Classes begin on Tuesday, October 3, 2023, according to a schedule that will be posted on our website.

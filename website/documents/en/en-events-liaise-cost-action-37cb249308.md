# LIAISE COST Action – ФИНКИ

## FINKI hosts the meeting of Working Group 1 and Working Group 2 within the LIAISE COST Action.

The Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering (FINKI) is proud to announce that it is hosting the meeting of Working Group 1 and Working Group 2 within **@liaiseproject**!

**LIAISE COST Action** focuses on **Industrial Symbiosis (IS)** – an innovative approach where waste from one industry becomes a raw material for another. This initiative is crucial for moving away from the linear economy, promoting sustainable production, and achieving a balance between society, the environment, and the economy.

Through collaboration and innovation, **LIAISE COST Action** It opens the way to environmental sustainability and economic benefits.

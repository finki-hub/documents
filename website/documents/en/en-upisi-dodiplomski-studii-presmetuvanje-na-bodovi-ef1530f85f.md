# Пресметување на бодови – ФИНКИ

The basic criterion for selecting candidates for enrollment in first-cycle studies is the achievement attained in secondary education. The evaluation of candidates’ achievement is expressed in points.

The most relevant calculation of points is obtained after completing the admission application on the **[admissions portal](https://www.upisi.ukim.mk/)**.

# Гостинско предавање на тема: „Micro Frontend Architecture for Multi\-Brand Platforms” од Игор Николов \(Flutter CEE\) – ФИНКИ

As part of the course **Service-Oriented Architectures** a guest lecture will be held on the topic **„Micro Frontend Architecture for Multi-Brand Platforms: A Scalable Approach Using Micro Frontends”** by **[Igor](https://www.linkedin.com/in/igor-nikolov-86867611a/) [Nikolov](https://www.linkedin.com/in/igor-nikolov-86867611a/), Engineering Manager** in **Flutter CEE**, part of Flutter Entertainment.

Date and time: **April 29, 2026 (Wednesday)**, starting at **12:00**

Room: **B2.1**

Guest lecture on the topic **„Micro Frontend Architecture for Multi-Brand Platforms: A Scalable Approach Using Micro Frontends”**will cover the principles and practical aspects of applying micro frontend architecture when building multi-brand platforms. Students will become familiar with the challenges and solutions in designing scalable frontend systems that support multiple brands and markets, as well as the ways in which this approach builds on the concepts of service-oriented architectures.

**Igor Nikolov** is an Engineering Manager at Flutter CEE, part of Flutter Entertainment, where he leads the Sportsbook Frontend Engineering team of 20 engineers. His area of specialization includes frontend engineering, frontend architecture and building scalable multi-brand platforms. He has practical experience in technical leadership, frontend architecture, organization and development of engineering teams, improvement of development processes and delivery of B2B solutions for multiple brands and markets. In his work, he focuses on building stable, sustainable and flexible frontend architectures, improving delivery quality and developing engineering teams.

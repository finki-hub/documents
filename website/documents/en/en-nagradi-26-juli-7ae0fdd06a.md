# „26 Јули“ – ФИНКИ

**July 26 – Frank Manning Award„**

|  |
| --- |
| After the catastrophic earthquake of 1963, among the numerous countries, organizations, and individuals, Frank Harkord Manning, a philanthropist from London, Great Britain, also extended aid to Skopje, thereby expressing his love and attachment to the city. During his stay in Skopje in 1977, Frank Manning expressed his desire to award prizes each year to the best graduating students in the fields of architecture, social sciences, and the arts at our University. His wish was fulfilled on June 30, 1980, with the University Assembly's decision to establish the „July 26th - Frank Manning“ Award.   The first awards were presented on July 18, 1980, to the best students of the Faculty of Architecture and the Faculty of Philosophy. In 1983, during his stay in Skopje to commemorate the 20th anniversary of the catastrophic earthquake, the founder of the prize fund, Frank Harkord Manning, visited the University and personally attended the award ceremony.  The „July 26“ Award, at the request of the donor Frank Harkord Manning – honorary citizen of the city of Skopje and founder of the prize fund, is awarded each academic year to the best students in the fields of architecture, social sciences, and arts at the University. „St. Cyril and Methodius“ in Skopje.  The ceremony of presenting this award is symbolically linked to the history of the city of Skopje and the tragic, catastrophic earthquake of July 26, 1963. |

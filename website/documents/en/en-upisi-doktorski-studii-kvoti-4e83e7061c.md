# Квоти – ФИНКИ

### **Quotas**

In accordance with [The Call](http://www.ukim.edu.mk/mk_content.php?meni=152&glavno=8) for the enrollment of students in third-cycle studies in the study programmes of Ss. Cyril and Methodius University in Skopje in the 2022/23 academic year, the Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering will enroll a total of 128 doctoral students in the first admission period of the next academic year.

For students enrolling in doctoral studies at FINKI in the 2022/2023 academic year, there are a total of 2 (two) study programmes. The number of students who may enroll at FINKI in each study programme is given below.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Study Programme** | **Number of students** | **Tuition fee amount\* (EUR)** |
| [Informatics](https://finki.ukim.mk/studii-2/vidovi-studii/doktorski-studii/id/) | 64 | 4750 |
| [Computer Science and Engineering](https://finki.ukim.mk/studii-2/vidovi-studii/doktorski-studii/knid/) | 64 | 4750 |

\* The tuition fee includes the costs of organizing teaching, workshops and seminars, the mentor, committees and research-organizing units, but does not include the material costs of research on the topic.

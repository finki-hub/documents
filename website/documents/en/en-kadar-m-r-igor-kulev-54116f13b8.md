# MSc Igor Kulev

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/igor.kulev_.jpg)

[igor.kulev@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:igor.kulev@finki.ukim.mk)

He was born in 1988 in Kavadarci. In 2011, he graduated from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje. He earned his master's degree from the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering in 2013, with a master's thesis in the field of collaborative computer systems. During his studies, he was regularly awarded for his academic achievements and successfully participated in many national and international algorithmic programming competitions. He is the recipient of the “Gold Coin” award from the University “Saint Cyril and Methodius” and the “Golden Ring” from the Association of Engineers of Macedonia. Since 2011, he has been employed as a teaching assistant at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering. His teaching duties include conducting lecture and lab exercises for the following courses: Algorithms and Data Structures, Advanced Algorithms, Intelligent User Interfaces, Intelligent Information Systems, Multimedia Systems, and Computer Architecture and Organization. He is the author and co-author of numerous scientific and professional papers published in domestic and international conferences and journals. His areas of research interest include: recommendation systems, artificial intelligence, machine intelligence and learning, intelligent agents, and natural language processing.

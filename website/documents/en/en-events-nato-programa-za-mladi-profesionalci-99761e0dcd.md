# НАТО програма за млади професионалци – ФИНКИ

### Open call for applications to the NATO Young Professionals Programme

We would like to inform you that the call for applications is open for **the NATO Young Professionals Programme** (*Young Professionals Program – YPP*).

The application deadline is **May 31, 2026, by 23:59 CET**.

This programme is an opportunity for students and young professionals interested in a career at NATO.

All interested candidates should apply directly through the following link:

[**Application and additional information**](https://bit.ly/3QNQtz6)

# Јавна одбрана на докторска дисертација – ФИНКИ

The University „St. Cyril and Methodius“ – Skopje, Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering announces that on October 31, 2022, at 12:00 PM, in the Faculty's amphitheater and via the Faculty's electronic system, a public defense of the doctoral dissertation „A Framework for the Analysis, Monitoring, and Evaluation of the Quality of Open Government Data,” submitted by the candidate, will be held on 10/31/2022 at 12:00 PM, in the Faculty's amphitheater and via the Faculty's electronic system. **Mr. Wigan Racha**, mentor Prof. Dr. Goran Velinov, submitted to the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering – Skopje

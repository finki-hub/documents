# Јавна одбрана на докторска дисертација – ФИНКИ

Dear all,

University „St. Cyril and Methodius“ – Skopje, Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering announces that on December 5, 2022, at 11:30 AM, in the amphitheater of the Faculty of Technology and Metallurgy and via the Faculty's electronic system (&lt;https://meet.google.com/vxt-xrai-wnz&gt;), a public defense of a doctoral dissertation will be held „APPLICATION OF MACHINE LEARNING ALGORITHMS IN THE MODELING AND INTERPRETATION OF THE ROLE OF THE MICROBIOME IN THE DIAGNOSTICS AND THERAPY OF COLORECTAL CANCER“ submitted by the candidate**Miodrag Cekic, M.A.**, mentor Prof. Dr. Slobodan Kajaldjiski, submitted to the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering – Skopje

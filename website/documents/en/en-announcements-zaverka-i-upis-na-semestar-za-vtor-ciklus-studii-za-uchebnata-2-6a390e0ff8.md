# Заверка и упис на семестар за втор циклус студии за учебната 2022/2023 – ФИНКИ

The winter semester finalization and summer semester enrollment for the 2022/2023 academic year for second-cycle students will be conducted from **08.03.2023** to **17.03.2023** year. The full statement is available at this [link](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u616/zaverka-upis-vtor-ciklus_2023_1.pdf).

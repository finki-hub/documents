# Прва GoodBrother интернационална тренинг школа – ФИНКИ

Факултетот за информатички науки и инженерство во соработка со COST акцијата Good Brother (CA 19121) &lt;https://goodbrother.eu/&gt; (Network on Privacy-Aware Audio- and Video-Based Applications for Active and Assisted Living) организира мултидисциплинарна летна тренинг школа.

Целта на оваа тренинг школа е да се популаризираат етичките и правните предизвици, како и предизвиците поврзани со приватноста на податоците кои се наметнуваат при процесирање на аудио и видео податоци. На школата ќе учествуваат студенти и предавачи од 16 држави.

Тренери и теми покриени на школата:

- Francisco Flórez-Revuelta, Ph.D., University of Alicante, Spain, “Computer vision for AAL” and “Visual privacy preservation
- Cristina Miguel, Ph.D., University of Gothenburg, Sweden, “Privacy and surveillance in the context of AAL applications: Social and ethical implications”
- Lukács Adrienn, Ph.D, University of Szeged, Hungary, “From Big Brother to GoodBrother: Introduction to the legal regulation of privacy”
- Ivan Pires, Ph.D., University of Beira Interior, Portugal, “Examples of audio- or video-based AAL applications”
- María José Santofimia. Ph.D., Universidad de Castilla La Mancha, Spain, “Remote monitoring and/or fall detection and prevention”
- Petre Lameski, Ph.D., Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering, Macedonia, “Introduction to Explainable AI”
- Nina Hosseini Kivanani, Université du Luxembourg, Luxembourg, “How to apply augmentation techniques on small datasets: Neurodegenerative diseases datasets”
- Eftim Zdravevski, Ph.D., Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering, Macedonia
- Zoltán Alexin, PhD, University of Szeged, Hungary, “Pseudonymization and anonymization of datasets”
- Aleksandar Jevremovic, Ph.D., Singidunum University, Serbia, “Protecting data in Smart/IoT environments”

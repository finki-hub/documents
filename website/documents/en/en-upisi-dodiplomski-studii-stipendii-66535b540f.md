# Стипендии – ФИНКИ

The Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering will award study scholarships to all candidates who win first, second or third prize at a national competition in Informatics, Mathematics or Physics, as well as to all candidates who win first, second or third prize at an international competition in the same fields. For the best high-school graduates, the scholarship will apply throughout the entire duration of their studies, provided they maintain an average grade above 9 and complete their studies on time.

All candidates who have received scholarship certificates from FINKI must submit them upon enrollment at the Faculty. Candidates who do not have such a document but have awards from national or international competitions or Olympiads in Informatics, Mathematics or Physics should submit them when applying for admission to the Faculty. After reviewing the diplomas, candidates will receive a response from the FINKI Admissions Committee on whether they will be exempt from paying participation fees and whether the exemption will be full or partial.

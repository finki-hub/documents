# Milan Todorovikj

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/milan.todorovikj.jpg)

[milan.todorovikj@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:milan.todorovikj@finki.ukim.mk)

Milan Todorovic was born in 2001 in Kumanovo. He completed his primary and secondary education in Kumanovo with consistently excellent results, during which he participated in several regional and state competitions and national Olympiads in various fields, winning multiple awards and recognitions. He enrolled in his undergraduate studies in 2020 at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering, in the Software Engineering and Information Systems program. During his studies, he completed an internship at the company „Inteligenta“. From October 2022 to October 2024, he was employed as a lab assistant at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering, where he conducted laboratory exercises for the courses Structured Programming, Object-Oriented Programming, Web Programming, Databases, Integrated Systems, and Operating Systems. In July 2024, he graduated with the thesis „Analysis of the Correlation of Food Prices in the Balkan Countries“ and a grade point average of 9.88. In the same year, he enrolled in graduate studies at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering, in the Software Engineering program. In November 2024, he was selected as a teaching assistant at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering. His research interests include software engineering, application software, databases and data warehouses, algorithms, and data structures.

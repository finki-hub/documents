# Dr\. Boban Joksimoski

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/doc-d-r-boban-joksimoski--731x1024.jpg)

[boban.joksimoski@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:boban.joksimoski@finki.ukim.mk)

### Regular consultation hours

Day: Monday

10:00 - 12:00

Classroom: Online (Courses - Consultations), Online

He was born in 1986 in Skopje. He graduated from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in 2008. He is currently enrolled in the master's program in Software Engineering at the same faculty. After graduating, he briefly worked as a research assistant at the Faculty of Informatics at the European University and as a 2D/3D computer animator, web, and application developer. He began working in 2009 as a teaching assistant at the Institute of Computer Science and Informatics at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje. He is responsible for conducting lectures and laboratory exercises in courses on structured programming, object-oriented programming, computer graphics, web design, and computer animation. He is a member of the IEEE. His research interests include computer graphics, visualization, geographic information systems, and the application of information systems in medicine.

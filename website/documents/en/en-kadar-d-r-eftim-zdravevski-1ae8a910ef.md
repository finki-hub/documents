He was born in 1984 in Radovish. He graduated from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje in 2008. He earned his master's degree in the Intelligent Information Systems program at the same faculty in 2010, with a master's thesis in the field of data mining. During his studies, he was continuously awarded for his academic achievements. He has worked as a teaching assistant at the Institute of Computer Engineering and Informatics at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje since 2010. His teaching duties include preparing and conducting lectures and laboratory exercises for courses in the field of distributed computer systems, data analysis algorithms, and intelligent information systems, while his practical experience consists of designing and implementing various software solutions gained while working at NI TEKNA – Intelligent Technologies. He has been a member of Mensa Macedonia since 2004. As part of his research work to date, he has published a dozen scientific papers in international conferences. His active areas of scientific interest are: machine learning, data mining, expert systems, decision support systems, intelligent information systems, databases and data warehouses, wireless sensor-actor networks, and others.

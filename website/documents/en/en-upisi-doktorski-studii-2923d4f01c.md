# Докторски студии – ФИНКИ

### **PHD STUDIES**

Doctoral studies at FINKI are organized by [the UKIM Doctoral School](https://ukim.edu.mk/doktorska-shkola/). On the website of **[UKIM](http://ukim.edu.mk/)** more details can be found about the organization of the studies, including the [structure of third-cycle study programmes](https://ukim.edu.mk/doktorska-shkola/struktura/struchen-sovet-na-doktorski-studii/), [study rulebook](https://ukim.edu.mk/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/pravilnik-iii_ciklus-31.12.2020-glasnik_530.pdf) and [study guide](https://ukim.edu.mk/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/vodich-za-studenti.pdf).

**Third-cycle study programmes at FINKI:**

- **[Informatics](https://finki.ukim.mk/studii-2/vidovi-studii/doktorski-studii/id/)**(Head: Prof. Dr. Biljana Tojtovska Ribarski, e-mail [biljana.tojtovska@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:biljana.tojtovska@finki.ukim.mk))
- **[Computer Science and Engineering](https://finki.ukim.mk/studii-2/vidovi-studii/doktorski-studii/knid/)**(Head: Prof. Dr. Slobodan Kalajdziski, e-mail [slobodan.kalajdziski@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:slobodan.kalajdziski@finki.ukim.mk))

**[List of accredited mentors](https://finki.ukim.mk/upisi/doktorski-studii/akreditirani-mentori/)**

**REQUIREMENTS FOR ENROLLMENT IN THIRD-CYCLE STUDIES**

Enrollment of students in third-cycle studies will be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Call for enrollment of students in third-cycle studies at Ss. Cyril and Methodius University in Skopje, and in accordance with the Rulebook on the requirements, criteria and rules for enrollment and study in third-cycle studies.

**[The Call](https://ukim.edu.mk/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/konkurs-iii-ciklus-akademski-studii-2025-2026-01.pdf)** is published on the website of the Doctoral School.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| ***Application and enrollment deadline*** | ***Date*** |
| Application of candidates | 9.9.2025 – 22.9.2025 |
| Publication of preliminary lists | 24.9.2025 |
| Decision-making on appeals | 29.9.2025 |
| Publication of final lists | 1.10.2025 |
| Enrollment of candidates | 6.10.2025 – 10.10.2025 |

Note: Starting from the 2024/25 academic year, FINKI will have only one admission period for doctoral studies.

# Заверување на зимски и упис на летен семестар за прв циклус студии – ФИНКИ

Registration for the winter semester and enrollment for the summer semester will take place from February 1, 2019 to February 11, 2019. You can view the details in the official announcement.[statement](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u616/leten_2018_2019_finki_0.pdf)[.](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u616/leten_2018_2019_finki.pdf)

Also attached is [the list of items](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u616/lista-na-predmeti-leten-semestar-2019.pdf) for the summer semester of the 2018/2019 academic year. The list is preliminary, and the activation of elective courses depends on the number of students who register for the course.

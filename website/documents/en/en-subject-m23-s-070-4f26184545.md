| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | Introducing students to current techniques for the analysis and design of connected health systems. Review of case studies on the development of connected health systems using current methodologies. Training in the practical analysis of connected health systems at all stages of development, with an emphasis on recommendations, interaction, and data exchange that make the connected health system smart. Upon completion of the course, the student is expected to demonstrate knowledge of using modern approaches in the analysis and design process of connected and/or smart health systems. |
| 11. | Course content | Principles and goals of connected health, Data flows and processes in connected health, Specifics of connected health software architectures, Smart connected health, Connected health and IoT, Case studies |

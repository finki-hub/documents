# Информација за натпревар Balkathon 5\.0 – ФИНКИ

Balkathon is a regional online competition organized by the RCC since 2020. Balkathon 5.0 will focus on new, innovative, and business-oriented digital ideas and solutions. It is open to teams of up to 35 years old from the Western Balkans, and high school teams are especially encouraged to apply. If you want to turn your ideas into masterpieces, you are in the right place. Apply with your idea for Balkathon 5.0 and win one of the prizes from the 50,000-euro prize pool.

- Participants can only apply as a team.;
- Teams are encouraged to be composed of members who live in more than one economy (country) of the Western Balkans.;
- Team members should not be older than 35 years old.;
- Teams competing for the main prize are encouraged to be composed of qualified individuals such as students; legal entities, including start-ups; and non-governmental and educational institutions.;
- High school teams can be composed of a group of high school students from the same or different high schools, with a maximum of 7 members.

You can find more information about the match at the link: &lt;https://www.rcc.int/balkathon/&gt;

# ФИНКИ учесник во проект поддржан од иновациските ваучери на ФИТР – ФИНКИ

With support from innovation vouchers provided by the Fund for Innovation and Technology Development (FITD), FCSE participated in preparing a feasibility study to improve and optimise the products and capacities of the company [Hoyo Tech](https://hoyo.tech/)

Under the leadership of Assistant Professor Petre Lameski, recommendations and guidelines were prepared for developing artificial-intelligence algorithms incorporated into all Hoyo Tech products, including fleet-management, smart-home and smart-vending-machine products. In addition, the methods and algorithms developed can be applied to any future product of the company.

The feasibility study provides clear guidance for short-, medium- and long-term objectives for further product development. With the support of this study, the company’s products gain a significant competitive advantage and become considerably more competitive in both domestic and international markets.

He was born in 1981 in Skopje. He graduated from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering in Skopje in 2005. He received his master's degree from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technologies in 2008. He is currently working on his doctoral dissertation in the field of vulnerability of complex networks. Immediately after graduating, he began working at the Institute of Computer Engineering and Informatics at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering in Skopje as a teaching assistant. Since the end of 2007, he has worked as a junior assistant for courses in computer engineering and informatics. During his employment, he has made a significant contribution to the design, creation, and organization of lecture and laboratory exercises. He has actively participated in research in the field of complex and computer networks and has published 10 scientific papers in international and domestic conferences and journals. He has contributed to 4 books, as well as to numerous research and applied projects. His research interests include complex networks, computer networks, wireless mobile ad hoc networks, application software, and web technologies, as well as system-on-chip design.

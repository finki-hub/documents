# Решение за прием и информација за запишување на кандидати согласно решението за прием во прв уписен рок 2026/2027 – ФИНКИ

[Линк до листата на стипендирани студенти](https://finki.ukim.mk/announcements/nagradeni-novi-studenti-za-akademska-2026/)

Dear candidates, We inform you that the enrollment of ranked candidates according to [**the decision on admission**](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/konechni-rang-listi.pdf) The first enrollment period will be held in **the new large Amphitheater** (at the same place where the documents were submitted) according to the following schedule:

**on September 3, 2026**

- Ranked in Software Engineering and Information Systems (**SIS**) of **9:00 AM**
- Ranked in Internet, Networks, and Security **(IMB)** from **11:00**

**On September 4, 2026**

- Ranked in Information Technology Applications (**Pit**) of**9:00 AM**
- **All others starting at 11:00 AM**

We wish all future students of FINKI success!

Ranked candidates must enroll during these timeframes and submit all required documents in full. Otherwise, they will forfeit their place, and if they wish to enroll at FINKI again, they will have to apply during the second or third admission period, starting from the beginning, if there are still open spots.

# Уписи за магистерски студии на ФИНКИ – ФИНКИ

In accordance with **[The Call](http://www.ukim.edu.mk/mk_content.php?meni=187&glavno=40)** For the enrollment of students in second-cycle studies in the academic programs of the University „St. Cyril and Methodius“ for the 2023/24 academic year, the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering will enroll a total of **760 students** in master’s studies.

More information about enrolling new students can be found at the following **[link](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/mk/enrollment/magisterski-studii).**

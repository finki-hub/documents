# Igor Goshev

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/igor.goshev.jpg)

[igor.goshev@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:igor.goshev@finki.ukim.mk)

Igor Goshev was born in 2002 in Skopje. He completed his primary education at „Kocho Racin“ Elementary School in Skopje and his secondary education at „Josip Broz Tito“ High School in Skopje, consistently achieving excellent grades.

In 2021, he enrolled in undergraduate studies at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering in the Software Engineering and Information Systems major. During his studies, he worked for two years as a lab assistant at the faculty, where he conducted lab exercises for the courses Operating Systems, Structured Programming, Client-Side Internet Programming, Algorithms and Data Structures, and E-Learning. He completed his undergraduate studies in 2025 with a thesis titled „Design and Implementation of an Online Platform for Training and Organizing Informatics Competitions.“.

That same year, he enrolled in a master's program at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering in the Software Engineering track, after which he was selected as a teaching assistant at the faculty.

His areas of scientific research interest are software engineering, application software, artificial intelligence, and data engineering.

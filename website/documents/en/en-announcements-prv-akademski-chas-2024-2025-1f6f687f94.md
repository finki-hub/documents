# Прв академски час 2024/2025 – ФИНКИ

We inform you that the First Academic Hour of the University „St. Cyril and Methodius“ in Skopje will be held on October 1, 2024, from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM, on the plaza in front of the Rectorate.

This event marks the beginning of the academic year and is dedicated to all newly enrolled students.

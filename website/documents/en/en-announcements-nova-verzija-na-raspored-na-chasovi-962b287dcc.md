# Нова верзија на распоред на часови – ФИНКИ

Dear Students,

A new version of the schedule has been released, which includes all lectures and exercises.

Some classes will be held online via the BBB platform. To access these classes, links to join them will be shared for the relevant courses on courses.finki.ukim.mk.

For groups that are missing elective courses in their schedule, students can enroll with the professor assigned to them in iKnow.

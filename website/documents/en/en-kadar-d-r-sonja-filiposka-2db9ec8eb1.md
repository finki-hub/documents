# Dr\. Sonja Filiposka

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/prof.-d-r-sonja-filiposka--731x1024.jpg)

She was born in 1980 in Skopje. She graduated from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering in Skopje in 2003. She earned her master's and doctoral degrees from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje in 2007 and 2009, respectively. She began working at the Institute for Computer Engineering and Informatics at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering in Skopje immediately after graduating in 2003 as a junior assistant. In 2007, she was promoted to assistant, and in 2009 to associate professor. She has published over thirty scientific papers. She is also the author of several books aimed at educating in the field of using application programs. She has participated in several domestic and international scientific and applied projects. Her areas of research interest are wireless mobile ad hoc networks, complex networks, computer networks, application software, and electromagnetic compatibility.

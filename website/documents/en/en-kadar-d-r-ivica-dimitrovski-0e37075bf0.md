# Dr\. Ivica Dimitrovski

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/prof.-d-r-ivica-dimitrovski-731x1024.jpg)

[ivica.dimitrovski@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:ivica.dimitrovski@finki.ukim.mk)

He was born in 1981 in Kratovo. He graduated from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering in Skopje in 2005. He received his master's degree from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje in 2008. He began working at the Institute for Computer Engineering and Informatics at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering in Skopje in 2005, first as a teaching assistant, was appointed junior assistant in 2007, and assistant in 2009. His teaching duties include conducting labs for courses in the areas of structured programming, object-oriented programming, internet programming, user interfaces, visualization, and virtual reality. As part of his research work, he has participated in the preparation of several published research papers and three book chapters. Research interests include image and video processing, visual feature extraction for images, content-based multimedia data retrieval, and machine learning.

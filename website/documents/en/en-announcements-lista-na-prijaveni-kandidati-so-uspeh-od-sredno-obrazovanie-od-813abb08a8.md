# Листа на пријавени кандидати со успех од средно образование од прв уписен рок 2026/2027 година – ФИНКИ

Dear all,

Attached is the list of registered candidates for admission to FINKI in the first admissions round for the 2026/2027 academic year. You can find the list at [**the following link**](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/lista-kandidati-finki.pdf).

The list contains information based on the data provided in the submitted documents and does not indicate the candidates’ ranking. It includes the candidate number, first-choice study programme, whether the candidate selected co-financing, the order of study programme preferences by priority, and the points awarded based on academic achievement.

Following the publication of the list of applicants for enrollment in the first year of studies, objections regarding technical errors may be submitted no later than 25 August 2026 at 10:00. Objections should be submitted by completing the form available at [**the following link**](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/e/uHGANpuuy5).

Technical errors refer to:

1. Typographical error in the primary choice of study programme
2. Selection of co-financing option
3. Order of study programme preferences
4. Incorrectly calculated points (due to subject equivalency, type of secondary school leaving examination, etc.)

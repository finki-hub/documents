# Пријавување на испити од јануарска сесија за прв и втор циклус на студии – ФИНКИ

Dear Students,

Registration for FINKI first and second cycle exams for the January 2022/2023 exam session will take place from December 20, 2022, to January 14, 2023.

Only students with a proper electronic registration in the iKnow system will be eligible to take the exam.

Otherwise, they will not be allowed to take the exam.

All exams that the student plans to take during the session are registered.

All FINKI students register their exams with FINKI, regardless of where they took them. Students pay a 400.00 denar penalty for late exam registration if they do not register electronically within the specified deadline.

Online exam registration is done by logging into the FINKI Central Authentication Service (CAS) at the following address: [http://finki.iknow.ukim.edu.mk](http://finki.iknow.ukim.edu.mk/). Problem reporting is done through the ticket system at the address [http://help.students.finki.ukim.mk](http://help.students.finki.ukim.mk/). When reporting an issue, be sure to select the topic "Exam Reporting," as this ensures your issue will be forwarded to the correct place in the shortest possible time.

# Процедура за дипломска работа – ФИНКИ

The thesis is registered at the beginning of the semester. The student does not have to have chosen a mentor when registering for the semester, but must have chosen one by the time the semester is finalized. The mentor signs the student's transcript for the "Thesis" course.

—

The thesis can be registered no earlier than the first enrollment in the summer semester of the third year for three-year programs or the fourth year for four-year programs.

—

The thesis is submitted in a teaching and research field that exists at the Faculty and is carried out under the supervision of a mentor.

—

The thesis advisor may be a faculty member holding a teaching and research position at the Faculty.

—

A student may apply for graduation if they have earned a minimum of 168 ECTS credits in three-year programs, or 228 credits in four-year programs. The system or the student's file is checked to verify that this prerequisite has been met.

—

The registration of the thesis is carried out according to the procedure defined on [this side](http://diplomski.finki.ukim.mk/).

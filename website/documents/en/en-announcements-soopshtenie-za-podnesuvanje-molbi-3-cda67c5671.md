# Соопштение за поднесување молби – ФИНКИ

In the system for [petitions](http://molbi.finki.ukim.mk) The following are enabled:

- Request to Enroll More Than 35 Credits
- Request for payment in installments
- Request to enroll in a course without meeting the prerequisite (the student must have earned a minimum of 210 credits)

The deadline for submission is until **26.09.2024**.

David Arsov was born in 2002 in Skopje. He completed his high school education at SUGS „Orce Николов“ High School – Skopje, with consistently excellent grades. In 2021, he enrolled in the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering at the University „St. Cyril and Methodius“ in Skopje, in the Software Engineering and Information Systems program.

During their studies, they demonstrate exceptional academic results and actively participate in the teaching process. In October 2023, they were selected as a tutor for the faculty, where they conduct laboratory exercises for the courses Algorithms and Data Structures, Fundamentals of Web Design, Web Programming, Databases, and Integrated Systems. Alongside his studies, he works in the industry as a software engineer, developing application software and information systems.

Graduated in 2025 with a GPA of 9.77, with a thesis titled „Analysis of EEG-based systems for classification of wakefulness states,“ under the mentorship of Assoc. Prof. Dr. Aleksandar Stoimenovski.

In 2025, he enrolled in a master's program in Software Engineering. That same year, he was selected as a teaching assistant at the Faculty.

His areas of scientific research interest are software engineering, application software, databases and data warehouses, as well as the application of informatics in medicine.

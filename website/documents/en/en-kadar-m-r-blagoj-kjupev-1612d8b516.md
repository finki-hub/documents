# MSc Blagoj Kjupev

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/blagoj.kjupev.png)

He completed his high school education at the Gymnasium in Veles, and earned both his bachelor's and master's degrees from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering in Skopje. His entire career and education have been dedicated to the study and practice of embedded systems technologies. He is currently responsible for the Embedded Technologies division at Seavus, where he has worked for over 8 years on the design and development of various complex embedded systems for global companies that are leaders in this field. During his many years of collaboration with global companies, he has gained expertise in numerous advanced hardware platforms for developing portable devices, with a special focus on low power consumption and the efficient use of hardware subsystems.Organizer and active participant in numerous workshops promoting the technology used in embedded systems, with the goal of sharing his experience with younger colleagues and students at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering. A special focus of his work is the study and implementation of mechanisms for digital data protection and the development of highly reliable embedded systems.

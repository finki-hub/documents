# Dr\. Ivan Kitanovski

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/doc.-ivan-kitanovski--731x1024.jpg)

[ivan.kitanovski@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:ivan.kitanovski@finki.ukim.mk)

He was born in 1986 in Bitola. He graduated from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje in 2009. He is currently enrolled in a master's program at the same faculty. During his undergraduate studies, he was continuously awarded for his academic achievements. He gained his first work experience at T-Mobile in 2008, where he worked as a programmer. He began working as a teaching assistant at the Institute for Computer Engineering and Informatics at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje in 2009. He is currently responsible for conducting lecture and laboratory exercises in courses related to web programming and visualization. His research interests include image processing, data mining, machine learning, and software engineering.

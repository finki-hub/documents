# Решение за прием и информација за запишување на кандидати согласно решението за прием во прв уписен рок 2024/2025 – ФИНКИ

Dear candidates, we inform you that the enrollment of ranked candidates according to [**the decision on admission**](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/resenie-za-priem-2024-2025.pdf) во првиот уписен рок ќе се изврши во Room 117 (at the same place where the documents were submitted) according to the following schedule:

**на ден 11.09.2024**

- Ranked in Information Technology Applications (**Pit**) of**9:00 AM**
- Ranked in Software Engineering and Information Systems (**SIS**) of **12:00**

**На ден 12.09.2024**

- Ranked in Software Engineering and Information Systems (**Seven-Eleven**) и Стручни студии за програмирање (**Socialist Party**) of **9**:**00 часот**
- Ranked in Internet, Networks and Security (**International Monetary Fund**) and Computer Engineering (**Kiss**) of **10:30 часот**
- Ranked in Computer Science (**Korean New Year**) and Information Literacy (**Yes**) of **12:00**

We wish all future students of FINKI success!

Ranked candidates must enroll during these timeframes and submit all required documents in full. Otherwise, they will forfeit their place, and if they wish to enroll at FINKI again, they will have to apply during the second or third admission period, starting from the beginning, if there are still open spots.

# Програма за партнерство – ФИНКИ

#### [Partnership Program Details](https://finkiukim-my.sharepoint.com/:w:/g/personal/webadmin_finki_ukim_mk/Edd0HpQSmINIg35ovJGIItYBiZFzz3Vj3KdKGCck87oYKw)

**[Partnership Presentation](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/paketi-partnerstvo1.pdf)**

If you would like to establish a partnership with FINKI, you are required to sign the appropriate memorandum ([industry partner](https://finkiukim-my.sharepoint.com/:w:/g/personal/webadmin_finki_ukim_mk/IQC8FcjDmzZcQZGBGw-R6x25ATUyaiTu6KeIo1L5xCi-Suk?e=9gaeUY), [basic](https://finkiukim-my.sharepoint.com/:w:/g/personal/webadmin_finki_ukim_mk/IQDJyxfqxe0sSKEyJyUo12ZjASh-VwKSiv4ejLGSoxcOyuE?e=fIbdiI), [silver](https://finkiukim-my.sharepoint.com/:w:/g/personal/webadmin_finki_ukim_mk/IQAlGnfXJptySJDvvL9z6ejcAQmLlt4TUr1I-HNJCZdkmzE?e=gHujco), [gold](https://finkiukim-my.sharepoint.com/:w:/g/personal/webadmin_finki_ukim_mk/IQBCZ01aeln1SLCZchrM1wGhAQJ0O4sib3BDiYxac19G5S4?e=3hl9x2)) and submit it to [[sorabotka@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:sorabotka@finki.ukim.mk)](mailto:sorabotka@finki.ukim.mk).

Micro and small companies may also register as industry partners in accordance with [Decision Amending and Supplementing the Rulebook](https://finkiukim-my.sharepoint.com/:w:/g/personal/webadmin_finki_ukim_mk/IQCuGb4XU8tHQp5NGaQwaDy3AZ1k912GG0BnHDDnJ4uhGZ0?e=nE1y7u).

# Understanding the Internet – ФИНКИ

Jelena Ćosić

Community Development Officer at RIPE NCC

Join us for a special event with Jelena Ćosić on 10 December 2024, beginning at 11:00, in the FCSE Amphitheatre (AMF FINKI).

Jelena joined the RIPE NCC as a consultant for the Southeast Europe region. As part of her role, Jelena works on supporting local communities and engaging stakeholders from the SEE region, including RIPE NCC members, governmental agencies, academic institutions, and civil society organizations.

In addition to her work in the SEE region, Jelena is interested in Internet measurements and tinkering with RIPE NCC measurement tools like RIPE Atlas and RIS.

Before joining the RIPE NCC, Jelena worked as an Outreach Coordinator for Packet Clearing House. At PCH, she primarily focused on providing support to IXPs and expanding PCH's global infrastructure.

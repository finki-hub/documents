Bojan Ilijoski was born in 1987 in Skopje. He completed his primary education at ”Mirče Acev” Elementary School in Skopje and his secondary education at „Nikola Karev“ High School in Skopje, in the mathematics track. During his elementary and secondary education, he participated in regional and national competitions in mathematics and computer science. In 2006, he enrolled at the Faculty of Natural Sciences and Mathematics in Skopje, at the Institute of Computer Science, in the Software Engineering program. He graduated in 2010, earning the professional title of a graduate engineer in Informatics – Software Engineering. During his higher education, he participated in several programming competitions and in the construction and management of software projects. In September 2010, he enrolled in graduate studies at the Institute of Informatics at the Faculty of Natural Sciences and Mathematics, in the field of Computer Science. He received his master's degree in December 2013 with a thesis titled "Grouping of Comments in the Macedonian Language" under the supervision of mentor Prof. Dr. Zhaneta Popeskа. Since 2009, he has been active as a teaching assistant at the Institute of Informatics at the Faculty of Natural Sciences and Mathematics, responsible for conducting laboratory exercises in the courses Fundamentals of Programming, Object-Oriented and Visual Programming, data structures and algorithms, data structures, internet technologies, and human-computer interaction. His areas of scientific research interest are intelligent systems, data mining, text mining, databases, algorithms, grid computing, and high-performance computing.

**[Information on the enrollment of new students at FINKI](https://finki.ukim.mk/upisi/dodiplomski-studii/potrebni-dokumenti/)**

**[Instructions for implementing the Call](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/upatstvo_konkurs-2026-2027.pdf)**

In accordance with **[The Call](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/konkurs-i-2026-27-mk.pdf)** for the enrollment of students in first-cycle studies in the study programmes of Ss. Cyril and Methodius University in the 2026/27 academic year, candidate applications at the Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering will take place on:

- for the first admission period on **18.8.**and **19.8.2026**year
- for the second admission period on **10.9.**and**11.9.2026**year
- for the third admission period on**23.9.2026**year

**For more efficient processing of enrollment documents, and in accordance with item XI of the UKIM admission call, the documents in paper form should be submitted at the FINKI premises on the stated dates from 09:00 to 14:30.**

At FINKI, in all admission periods, high-school graduates who have passed the **[appropriate external and internal subjects](https://finki.ukim.mk/upisi/dodiplomski-studii/potrebni-predmeti/)** from the Matura, as prescribed by the Call, may enroll, as well as graduates who have non-matching external and internal subjects. In accordance with the UKIM instruction for applying the Call, graduates who have not passed the appropriate external and internal subjects will have a different formula for calculating points from the Matura and secondary education achievement. More detailed information is available at the link for [**Calculation of Points**](https://finki.ukim.mk/upisi/dodiplomski-studii/presmetuvanje-na-bodovi/).

The required external and internal subjects for enrollment at FINKI, as well as the method for calculating points, are given in the section **[Required Subjects](https://finki.ukim.mk/upisi/dodiplomski-studii/potrebni-predmeti/).**

The required documents for enrollment at FINKI are given in the section **[Required Documents](https://finki.ukim.mk/upisi/dodiplomski-studii/potrebni-dokumenti/).**

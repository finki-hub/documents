# Потребни документи – ФИНКИ

### **Documents for application and enrollment**

Before applying within the deadlines set by  **[The Call](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/konkurs-ii-2026-27-mk.pdf)**, each interested candidate must register at **[www.upisi.ukim.mk](http://www.upisi.ukim.mk/)** and complete the electronic application from the iKnow system. The number automatically assigned to the candidate by the electronic application system must also be entered in the application submitted for enrollment. Applications are submitted to the Student Affairs Office of the selected faculty.
When applying for enrollment at the faculties, candidates submit the following documents:

- application form for enrollment;
- electronic application (from [www.upisi.ukim.mk](http://www.upisi.ukim.mk/));
- original document confirming completion of first-cycle studies (diploma or certificate);
- short biography (CV);
- certificate of active knowledge of a foreign language (from Blazhe Koneski or an international certificate);
- proof of paid administrative fee in the amount of MKD 50.00 (Form PP 50: Recipient name: Treasury Account-Skopje; Recipient bank: NBRNM; Transaction account 100-0000000-630-95; Payment account 840-XXX-03161; Account 722313 00)
- proof of payment of MKD 300 to the [Faculty giro account](https://finki.ukim.mk/za-nas/administracija-i-dokumenti/finansii/) (a template of the giro account can be obtained in the iKnow system, in the Submission section)
- recommendations from teaching or scientific staff (if any);
- list of professional or scientific papers and a copy of them (if the candidates have any);
- documents for possible awards, recognitions and other documents.

Upon enrollment, admitted candidates submit:

- student index and application forms
- two photographs (3.5 x 4.5)
- Birth certificate (original)
- Proof of citizenship of the Republic of North Macedonia (certificate or decision)

# Пријавување на испити за прв и втор циклус на студии – септемвриска сесија 2023/2024 – ФИНКИ

Registration for FINKI first and second cycle exams for the September 2023/2024 session will be open until **25.8.2024**

Only students with a proper electronic registration in the iKnow system will be eligible to take the exam.
Otherwise, they will not be allowed to take the exam.

All exams that the student plans to take during the session are registered.

All FINKI students register their exams with FINKI, regardless of where they took them. Students pay a 400.00 denar penalty for late exam registration if they do not register electronically within the specified deadline.

Online exam registration is done by logging into the FINKI Central Authentication Service (CAS) at the following address: &lt;http://finki.iknow.ukim.edu.mk&gt;.

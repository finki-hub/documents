| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | The goal of the course is to introduce students to the techniques of managing the software testing process. Students will be introduced to the basic concepts of organizing the software testing process, which includes defining a test plan, managing the dynamics of testing, resources, time, and budget within the organization, an appropriate approach to selecting a model, standards and tools for testing, control and evaluation of the testing process, as well as opportunities for improving the software testing process.  Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:  - Critically assess the importance of managing the software testing process;  - to demonstrate knowledge of the concepts of the software testing management process;  - to efficiently implement the software testing management process;  to evaluate the results of the testing conducted and apply them to improve the management process. |
| 11. | Course content | Basic concepts of software testing.  Introduction to organizing the testing process.  What is a test plan and how is it written? Templates for test plans.  Managing the dynamics of testing.  Testing team management.  Defining resources, time, and budget for the testing process.  Test management tools.  Control and evaluation of the testing process.  Techniques for improving the testing process. |

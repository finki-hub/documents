| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | The main objective of the course is for students to become familiar with the analysis of communication signals and their transmission through a medium, the types of modulation, and the impact of noise on modulation techniques. Additionally, students will gain knowledge of software-defined radio, modern communication systems, and their current development. |
| 11. | Course content | General concepts and definition of communication systems. Analysis of communication signals and systems in the frequency domain. Transmission of communication signals through linear systems. Amplitude, angle, and phase modulation and demodulation. Sampling and pulse-code modulation. Basic principles of digital transmission. Communication systems with noise and the impact of noise on modulation techniques. Software-defined radio and modern communication technologies. Current developments in communication systems, optical and wireless communications. |

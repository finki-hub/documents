# Јавна одбрана на докторска дисертација – ФИНКИ

The University „St. Cyril and Methodius“ – Skopje, Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering announces that on October 31, 2022, at 2:00 PM, in the Faculty's amphitheater and via the Faculty's electronic system, a public defense of the doctoral dissertation „Analysis of Data Enriching Human-Machine Interaction for Users with Autism„ submitted by **Candidate M.A. Bojan Ilijoski**, submitted to the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering – Skopje, mentor Prof. Dr. Zhaneta Popeskа.

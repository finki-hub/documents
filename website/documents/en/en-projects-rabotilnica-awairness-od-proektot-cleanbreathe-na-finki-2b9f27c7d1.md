# Работилница AwAIRness од проектот CleanBreathe на ФИНКИ – ФИНКИ

Within FCSE’s CleanBreathe project and in cooperation with Magdeburg-Stendal University of Applied Sciences in Germany, a workshop involving students from Germany and FCSE students is being held this week, from 4 to 9 December 2022.

CleanBreathe is an FCSE project at Ss. Cyril and Methodius University in Skopje with the following objectives:

1. Raising awareness of pollution through education and direct citizen participation
2. Using a large number of low-cost sensors to predict pollution
3. Building a sustainable business model for products and applications that measure pollution and increase awareness of pollution-related problems.

The workshop aims to raise awareness of air pollution through interventions and innovative product and application design. The students’ projects will be presented on Friday, 9 December 2022, at 14:00 at the Netaville premises.

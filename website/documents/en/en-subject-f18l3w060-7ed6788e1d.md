| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | System administration is a complex process of operational management of the system and software components of computer systems, with the goal of providing secure, reliable, and available services and utilities to users. The organization of the system administration process is multifaceted and includes activities related to managing operating systems, network services, as well as application and other server systems. |
| 11. | Course content | (1) Management of users, files, and software on a client-server computer system (MANAGEMENT)  (2) Installation and configuration of network services for intranet and Internet domains (NETWORKING)  (2) Administration of network security policies in a Linux and Windows environment (SECURITY)  (1) Techniques for the interoperability of computer systems in a mixed environment (INTEROPERABILITY)  (2) Identification of possible sources of poor performance and potential solutions (PERFORMANCE DEBUGGING)  (1) Design of small and medium business IT infrastructures (Capacity Planning).  (2) Development of scripting mechanisms and automated scripts for performing complex administrative tasks (SYSTEM SCRIPTING)  Evaluation of various policies and mechanisms for ensuring the reliability of computer services and operations (backups)  (1) Installation and configuration of Linux and Windows virtual machines (Virtualization)  (1) Systems for handling large amounts of data for a large number of different users (DATA CENTERS) |
| 12. | Learning methods | Lectures using presentations, interactive lectures, exercises (using equipment and software packages), teamwork, case studies, guest lecturers, independent preparation and defense of a project assignment and a seminar paper. |

# Mr\. Kiril Cirovski

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/m-r-kiril-kjiroski--731x1024.jpg)

[kiril.kjiroski@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:kiril.kjiroski@finki.ukim.mk)

He was born in 1977 in Tetovo, where he completed his primary and secondary education. He graduated from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering in Skopje in 2001 with a degree in Computer Technology, Informatics, and Automation, defending his thesis titled “Augmented Reality.” From 2002 to 2003, he worked at Acocon GmbH in Bielefeld, Germany, as a programmer and systems engineer. From 2003 to 2006, he worked at Ein-Sof in Skopje as a systems engineer and head of the systems integration department. He joined II in April 2006.

| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | Upon completion of the course, students are expected to gain introductory knowledge for developing websites with modern design using HTML and cascading styles. Candidates are expected to:  1. They demonstrate a basic understanding of the importance of good website design.  2. Demonstrate practical knowledge of web design technologies and be able to apply that knowledge to basic website design.  3. To communicate using terminology specific to this field.  4. Critically evaluate examples of websites. |

# Dr\. Aleksandra Dedinec

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/doc.-d-r-aleksandra-dedinec--731x1024.jpg)

[aleksandra.kanevche@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:aleksandra.kanevche@finki.ukim.mk)

She was born in 1987 in Belgrade, Serbia. She graduated from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje in 2009 with a degree in Informatics and Computer Engineering. She earned her master's degree from the same faculty in 2011 in the field of Computer Networks and E-technologies, with a master's thesis in the area of modeling and analysis of complex network systems. During her undergraduate studies, she was continuously awarded for her academic achievements. She is participating in an international project, has presented a paper at an international conference, and has participated in two international workshops. She began working at the Institute for Computer Engineering and Informatics at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje in 2009 as a teaching assistant. Her teaching duties include conducting lectures and laboratory exercises for courses in the fields of logic circuits and discrete automata, and formal languages. Her research interests include optimization, analysis and modeling of network systems, formal languages, logic circuits and finite automata, and system reliability.

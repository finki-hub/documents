| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to: understand the principles (pedagogical, cognitive, engineering) that should be used in the design of educational software, the place and application of different types of educational software in education, the way educational software helps to achieve certain pedagogical and educational goals, as well as the basic principles for using educational software without compromising the didactics of the educational process. |
| 11. | Course content | (1) Models and theories of learning. (1) Traditional learning environments. (1) Tools used in traditional learning environments.  (1) Integration of technology into the learning process. (1) Basic models of ICT use in education. (1) Tools for technology-enhanced learning. (1) Learning content in the future: analysis of successful applications. (1) Design of learning content and technological standards for digital content. (1) Learning objects: reusable digital content. (1) Technical standards for learning objects. (1) Distribution and reuse of learning objects: technical aspect. (1) Examples of application of learning tools in primary and secondary education. (1) Tools for creating learning objects |

# Листа на пријавени кандидати со успех од средно образование од прв уписен рок 2023/2024 година – ФИНКИ

The list of registered candidates for enrollment at FINKI for the first enrollment period for the 2023/2024 academic year has been published. You can find the list at the following [**link**](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u616/preliminarna_rang_lista_1.pdf).

**The list contains information about what has been filled out in the documents and does not provide information on the candidates' rankings.** The list contains the candidate's number, the first-choice study program, whether the candidate opted for co-financing or not, the order of priority for program tracks, and the success points.

Upon the publication of the list of registered candidates for enrollment in the first year, starting from September 4, 2023, **by 10:00 AM on September 5, 2023** an objection may be filed for technical defects to [**the following link**](https://forms.office.com/pages/responsepage.aspx?id=e8TK8LPiG06lL0h9LZliiPyQ7oT_fHhMtm0WbOxrlOBUQThST0NGUTAySUMyTDFTRFBGWlRERDJVMS4u).

Technical errors refer to:

1. Printing error on the main program of study selection

2. Co-financed selection

3. Order of study programs

4. Incorrectly calculated points (due to subject equivalence, type of final exam, etc.)

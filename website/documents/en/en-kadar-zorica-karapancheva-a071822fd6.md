# Zorica Karapancheva

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/zorica.karapancheva.jpeg)

[zorica.karapancheva@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:zorica.karapancheva@finki.ukim.mk)

Zorica Karapancheva was born on June 26, 2000, in Skopje. She completed her high school education at SUGSS „Nikola Karev„ in Skopje with consistently excellent results. She has been awarded at several regional and national competitions in various fields. In 2018, she obtained a Microsoft Office Specialist certification.

He is continuing his higher education at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering – major in Computer Engineering. He graduated in 2022 with a grade point average of 9.13 and a thesis titled „Analysis and Implementation of the Data Vault Methodology in the Cloud Using the Amazon Web Services Platform,“ under the mentorship of Prof. Dr. Saso Gramativ.

In the 2023/24 academic year, they are enrolling in a master's program at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering, in the Cloud Computing track.

With three years of experience as a Data Engineer, they have worked on full-cycle projects with international and Macedonian clients, while also earning multiple certifications in Cloud Computing and Data Engineering.

For the 2023/2024 academic year, she was selected as a teaching assistant and conducts classroom and laboratory exercises for the courses Database Systems, Computer Networks, Computer Networks and Security, Physics, Structured Programming, Embedded Systems, Computer Architectures, and Operating Systems.
Her areas of scientific research interest are: databases, data analysis, business intelligence, and cloud computing.

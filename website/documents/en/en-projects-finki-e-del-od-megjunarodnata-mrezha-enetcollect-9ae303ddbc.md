# ФИНКИ е дел од меѓународната мрежа EnetCollect – ФИНКИ

FCSE participates in EnetCollect, a large COST-funded international network involving more than 100 participants from Europe and other countries. The Action aims to establish a foundation for research and innovation in crowdsourcing-supported language learning. Further information and opportunities to join the network are available at: &lt;http://enetcollect.eurac.edu/&gt;

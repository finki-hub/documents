# Прелиминарни ранг листи за првиот уписен рок за академската 2026/2027 година – ФИНКИ

Dear all,

**Preliminary ranking lists** for the first enrollment period for the 2026/2027 academic year are **[available at the following link](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/preliminarni-rang-listi.pdf)**.
Objections regarding the preliminary ranking list of applicants in the first enrollment period may be submitted via the [Objections to the Faculty Management 2026/27 – Fill out form](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/e/kQ5NqpZdVZ) no later than **27 August 2026** at **16:00**.

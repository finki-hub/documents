# Open Day ENDAVA – Graduates programme – ФИНКИ

Skopje, Macedonia | 5th September 2017

Endava Open Day is all about getting to know each other better. By joining us you will have the opportunity to meet the Endava team, see how we work and how we have fun. You will also be able to discover more about our Graduates Programme, both from our recruiters and from those who have already been part of this experience and are now Endavans. The Q&A session and Networking event will give you the chance to dive deeper into subjects of interest and interact with professionals from various fields.

The event gives 4th grade students and graduates interested in following a career in Software Testing and Software Development, a chance to sample what it means to work in Endava.

This year, our Open Day will take place on 5th of September and we are excited about meeting you then.

**REGISTRATION**

# Електронски документи – ФИНКИ

Dear Students,

We would like to inform you that FINKI has integrated the issuance of certain documents as electronically signed PDF files through the iknow system.

The following documents can be obtained electronically:

- Certificate of Regular Student Status
- Transcript of Passed Examinations
- Uppix form

The instructions on how to issue the documents are available at the following link:

**&lt;https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/mk/elektronski_dokumenti&gt;**

The electronic documents are valid and students receive them at their official email addresses. Additionally, students can access all electronic documents issued in the past year through the electronic document repository at the following link:

**&lt;https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/depo&gt;**

If you make the payment through iknow (for documents for which it charges according to the price list), students do not need to go to the Student Services office.

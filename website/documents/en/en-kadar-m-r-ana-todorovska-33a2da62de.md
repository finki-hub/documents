Ana Todorovska was born in 1998 in Bitola. She completed her primary and secondary education in Bitola with consistently excellent results.
In 2016, she enrolled in undergraduate studies at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering (FINKI), in the Computer Science and Engineering program taught in English.
During her studies, she completed practical work at the companies Sorsix and Idox Health, and in 2019 she was hired as a lab technician at FINKI.

She graduated in 2020 with the thesis „Platform for Sharing and Renting Clothing,“ under the mentorship of Prof. Dr. Dimitar Trajanov.
In 2022, she graduated with a master's thesis titled „Modeling the Correlation between Digital Currencies and Traditional Economic Indicators,“ also under the mentorship of Prof. Dr. Dimitar Trajanov.
That same year, she enrolled in doctoral studies at FINKI, in the Computer Science and Engineering program.

She has been employed at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering since December 2020. Her research interests include machine learning, data-driven science, natural language processing, and big data.

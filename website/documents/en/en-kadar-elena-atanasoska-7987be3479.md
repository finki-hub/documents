Elena Atanasoska was born in 2001 in Skopje. She completed her primary and secondary (gymnasium) education in Skopje with consistently excellent results.

In 2020, they enrolled in undergraduate studies in the Software Engineering and Information Systems program at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering – Skopje. They graduated in September 2024 with a thesis titled „Design and Implementation of a Scheduling System for Academic Processes Optimization at FCSE“.

Starting in March 2023, they begin working as a lab instructor at FINKI. They continue this role into the following academic year. They are constantly working to maintain and improve student services within the faculty. While studying, they work in the industry as a software engineer.

In the 2024/2025 academic year, they are enrolling in graduate studies at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering, in the Data Science in Computer Science and Engineering program. In October 2024, she was selected as a teaching assistant and conducts classroom and laboratory exercises for the courses: Web Programming, Software Design and Architecture, and E-commerce and Mobile Commerce.

Her areas of scientific interest are web programming, software engineering, mobile application development, data science, and machine learning.

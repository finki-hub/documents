# Контакт 2025 – Вратата кон твојата ИТ кариера\! – ФИНКИ

**FINKI**in cooperation with the [**Student Parliament of FINKI (SPFINKI)**](https://spfinki.mk/), proudly supports [**Contact 2025**](https://kontakt.mk/), the largest job and internship fair in the IT industry! The event will be held from **March 17 to 20, 2025** in the hall of **Faculty of Technology and Metallurgy (FTM)**.

Are you ready to take the next step in your career? Contact 2025 gives you the opportunity to meet top companies, hear firsthand about the opportunities they offer, and connect with potential employers!

**What awaits you at Contact 2025?**

- **Exhibition Hall** Meet the company representatives in person and find out what opportunities they offer.
- **Online Presentations** – Attend inspiring sessions from industry experts and learn more about the latest technologies.
- **Internship and Employment Opportunities** Get direct access to open job positions.
- **Networking and new contacts** Build your professional network while you're still a student.

This is your chance to take a step forward in your career and connect with leading companies in the IT world!

**Save the date and get ready for new opportunities!**

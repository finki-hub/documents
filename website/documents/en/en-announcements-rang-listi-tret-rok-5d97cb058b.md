# Ранг листи, трет рок – ФИНКИ

Dear candidates, on [FINKI's Portal for Prospective Students](http://join.finki.ukim.mk/TretUpisenRok.aspx) They have been published. [Preliminary rankings](http://join.finki.ukim.mk/Dokumenti.aspx?id=208) for the third enrollment period at FINKI in the 2011/2012 academic year.

The deadline for submitting objections to the published preliminary rankings is September 19, 2011, at 2:00 PM. Objections are submitted according to [published procedure](http://join.finki.ukim.mk/Files/Трет уписен рок/Приговори/Postapka za prigovor 3 upisen rok.pdf).

**Unranked candidates**

All candidates who are not ranked or who wish to change or supplement their selection of study programs or mode of study (state quota or co-financing) have the opportunity to re-declare their interest in study programs with available seats and to be ranked for a study program that is not fully filled. The new ranking will be made without changing the order of the previously ranked candidates.

The change of the chosen study program or mode of study is made by filling out the [Declaration for the Supplementation or Change of Selected Study Programs](http://join.finki.ukim.mk/Files/Трет уписен рок/Приговори/Izjava za nov izbor na SP.pdf), in accordance with the available seats per study program. Statements for supplementing or changing selected study programs (enrolling in a study program with available seats) or changing a statement of intent to study under a co-financing quota (if this has not been done on the internal application form) will be accepted at the FINKI Info Desk until 2:00 PM on Monday, September 19, 2011.

He was born in 1996 in Skopje, where he completed his primary and secondary education. He graduated from the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering in Skopje in 2019 with a degree in Network Technologies. He earned his master's degree in 2020 in Internet Technologies. During his undergraduate studies, he was continuously awarded for his achievements. He participates in the implementation of several national and international projects. He began working at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering in Skopje in 2018 as a junior demonstrator. His teaching responsibilities include conducting classroom and laboratory exercises for courses in the field of computer networks and cybersecurity. His areas of research interest are computer networks, distributed systems, application software, and cybersecurity.

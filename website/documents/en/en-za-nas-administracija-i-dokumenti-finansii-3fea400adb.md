## **SCHEDULE OF FEES PAYABLE BY STUDENTS FOR EDUCATIONAL AND ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES AT THE FACULTY OF COMPUTER SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING – SKOPJE (Simplified Version)**

## Article 1

The price of one ECTS credit is determined based on the amount of the participation fee or tuition fee (state-funded quota/co-financing/self-financing) for one semester and is calculated by dividing the respective fee by 30 ECTS credits. The applicable amount is determined in accordance with the first agreement signed by the student, i.e. the student’s currently valid agreement.

## Article 7

This price list is a single document containing all the fee schedules the Faculty charges to students, including the Faculty's Price List applicable to students enrolled before the 2024/25 academic year, Annex to the Regulation on the Conditions, Criteria, Rules, and Procedure for Enrollment and Study in First Cycle Studies (Undergraduate Studies) and Second Cycle Studies (Graduate Studies) at the University „St. Cyril and Methodius“ in Skopje (Univ. Glasnik 689/2024) and Decision on Establishing Additional Fees for Services Rendered or Expenses Related to Specifics in the Implementation of the Study Programs at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering.

## Article 7

This fee schedule is in effect for the 2024/25 academic year, covers all students currently enrolled at the faculty, and remains in effect until a new one is adopted. This regulation repeals the Decision on the Harmonization and Supplementation of the Price Lists for the Amount of Fees Paid by Students for Educational and Administrative Services at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering, adopted between 08/22/2014 and 08/21/2014.12.2023.

| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | The goal of the course is for students to acquire the skills necessary for academic and technical writing and presenting, with due regard for ethics and critical thinking. Upon completion of the course, the student is expected to be able to produce high-quality academic texts (summary, essay, CV, formal letter) in English and Macedonian. to differentiate between various technical texts (specification, specification sheet, report, technical documentation) and to identify their parts; to deliver an effective presentation on a given topic and to answer questions related to the presentation; |
| 11. | Course content | Lectures:  1. Introduction to the subject  2. Communication skills  3. Writing a biography  4. Writing an email and a cover letter  5. Writing a recommendation and a business letter  6. Interview Preparation and Practice  7. Organizing events  8. Business Meetings, Negotiation, and Teamwork  9. Writing reports and essays  10. Academic writing  11. Presentation Preparation and Practice |
| 12. | Learning methods | Lectures using presentations, interactive lectures, exercises (using equipment and software packages), teamwork, case studies, guest lectures, independent preparation and defense of a project assignment and a seminar paper. |

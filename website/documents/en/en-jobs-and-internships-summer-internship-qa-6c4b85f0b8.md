A global digital transformation company with a team of more than 4,500 global players in 24 countries - in Europe, America and Asia, working together to put organizations at the forefront of innovation by bringing together talent, knowledge and technology.

Our expertise is rooted in Adobe Experience Cloud but extends beyond it with mobile development, integrated chatbots, and cutting-edge technologies such as virtual reality.

About the internship:

For our office in Skopje, we are looking for open-minded and personable people, who love to work on web development. Grab the opportunity to work on international projects and play a role into creating and executing test cases, identifying and documenting and work closely with our development teams to resolve issues. We attach great importance to a cooperative team spirit and a delightful working atmosphere. The main focus of the internship will be with manual testing in AEM (Adobe Experience Manager), and also Magnolia as CMS.

We expect:

Student or graduate in Computer Science and Engineering
- Basic knowledge in manual testing of web and mobile apps
Team player, with a positive attitude towards agile development methods (Scrum)
Proficiency in the English language

It is an advantage if you have knowledge in:

- Creating test cases (Xray)
- JIRA and Confluence
- Automation testing with Cypress, Selenium, Playwright or similar related platforms

We offer:

As a trainee at VASS North Macedonia, you will work on cutting-edge technologies within an international team of young and progressive engineers. You will benefit not only from working with a great team but also from receiving our excellent terms:

Paid internship
An organization that is open to your ideas
Exciting projects and the use of the latest technologies and modern infrastructure
Attractive career opportunities
- Opportunity for development by attending various trainings and conferences
A flexible working time model

# Решение за прием и информација за запишување на кандидати согласно решението за прием во прв уписен рок 2025/2026 – ФИНКИ

Dear candidates, we inform you that the enrollment of ranked candidates according to [**the decision on admission**](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/resenie-priem-2025-26.pdf) во првиот уписен рок ќе се изврши во **the new large Amphitheater** (at the same place where the documents were submitted) according to the following schedule:

**на ден 04.09.2025**

- Ranked in Software Engineering and Information Systems (**SIS**) of **09****:00 часот**
- Ranked in Internet, Networks, and Security **(IMB)** from **12:00**

**На ден 05.09.2025**

- Ranked in Information Technology Applications (**Pit**) of**9:00 AM**
- **Сите други останати од****12:00**

We wish all future students of FINKI success!

Ranked candidates must enroll during these timeframes and submit all required documents in full. Otherwise, they will forfeit their place, and if they wish to enroll at FINKI again, they will have to apply during the second or third admission period, starting from the beginning, if there are still open spots.

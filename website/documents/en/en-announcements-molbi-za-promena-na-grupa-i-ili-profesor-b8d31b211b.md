# Молби за промена на група и/или професор – ФИНКИ

Electronic requests to change a group and/or professor are submitted through the system. **&lt;http://molbi.finki.ukim.mk&gt;** from **October 10, 2022 to October 14, 2022**. Changing a group and/or professor is only allowed if there is one available. **overlapping with other objects** and upon submission of the application **It was specified exactly which subjects are being discussed.**.

# Известување за плаќање преку iknow – ФИНКИ

Dear Students,

We would like to inform you that starting this semester, the payment card payment system has been launched on Iknow.

Online payments are subject to a bank fee for cashless payment of 1.14% of the original amount (minimum fee 3.00, maximum 300.00 MKD). Anyone who wishes to pay by card can do so under the conditions stated above. The options for payment via online banking and bringing payment slips to the student services counter remain, as does the most expensive solution: paper payment slips deposited at a bank and then brought to the student services counter.

Respectfully

FINKI

# Заверка и упис на семестар за втор циклус студии за учебната 2024/2025 – ФИНКИ

The winter semester finalization and summer semester enrollment for students of the 2024/2025 academic year will be conducted from **28.10.2024** to **01.11.2024** year, as of 11/01/2024, from **09:00** to **12:00 PM** at the Student Service desk of FINKI in the TMF building.

**Required documents for enrolling in the winter semester:**

1. Index
2. Semester Endorsement Form
3. Semester enrollment form (to be submitted by all students who have not yet earned their master's degree, i.e., for enrolling an empty semester)
4. [Internal Application Form](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/interen_prijaven_list_magisterski_20207337_1.pdf) (only for students registering for courses)
5. [Payment slip](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/untitled_9.png) for the next tuition installment, in accordance with the study agreement

For students who are retaking a course, the following is additionally required:

Payment slip (for the value of the credits for courses being retaken according to the study program)
The cost of 1 credit = 1/60 of the value of the study program for one-year programs, and 1/120 for two-year study programs.
Payment is made in the denar equivalent according to the NBRM average exchange rate on the date of payment.

**The deadline for submitting the documents is November 1, 2024.**

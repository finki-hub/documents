# Jovan Simonoski

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/jovan.simonoski.jpg)

[jovan.simonoski@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:jovan.simonoski@finki.ukim.mk)

Jovan Simonoski was born in 2002 in Ohrid. He completed his primary and secondary education in Resen.

He enrolled in the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering in 2021, in the Internet, Networks, and Security program.

During his studies, he was continuously recognized for his achievements and was hired as a tutor for the Faculty's laboratory exercises.

Graduated in 2025 with the thesis „Bona Fide? A System for Verifying Researchers“ under the mentorship of Prof. Dr. Sonja Filiposka, with an average grade of 9.88.

That same year, he enrolled in a master's program in Internet technologies and cybersecurity.

His areas of scientific research interest are computer networks, application software, and cybersecurity.

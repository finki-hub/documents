| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | The course is an introduction to the fundamental mathematical concepts necessary for advanced computer science courses. Advanced techniques for proofs, induction, and recursion will be studied. Students should acquire skills in solving combinatorial problems and become familiar with basic terminology and the fundamentals of graph representation. |
| 11. | Course content | Relations, ordinations. Equivalences. Induction and recursion. Counting. Basic combinatorics. Pigeonhole principle. Advanced combinatorial techniques. Binomial formula. Graphs (basic concepts, representations, and properties) Isomorphism, path planarity. Trees and their properties. Search trees |

# AI Engineer Internship – ФИНКИ

---

## Description

We are looking for an AI-savvy intern who actively uses tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Make.com, n8n, and other AI agents. Prompt engineering, automation skills, and creative use of AI tools are highly valued.

You'll support our team on real-world AI projects, learning how AI is applied in production.

The Mentality We Value: We're looking for builders with a "move fast and ship things" attitude. You should be someone who runs toward challenges, not away from them. We value the courage to experiment, the drive to build quickly, and the willingness to take calculated risks. If you see a problem and immediately think "I could automate that" or "let me prototype a solution," you'll fit right in.

What You'll Do: Build and test AI-powered workflows and applications
Clean and prepare datasets
Use AI tools to automate workflows and research
Collaborate with developers and product teams
Document experiments and results

What We're Looking For: Strong interest in AI, prompt engineering, and LLMs, Full Stack App Development
Hands-on with Python and libraries like Hugging Face, LangChain, OpenAI
Curious, analytical, and self-driven

Bonus Skills: Experience with Claude, Gemini, or OpenAI APIs
Familiarity with cloud platform: AWS

Perks: Work on impactful AI products
Mentorship from experienced engineers
Flexible remote work
Potential full-time offer

To Apply:

Send us your CV and show us how you think with AI. Whether it's a cool prompt you've built, an automation you've created, or a mini-project—impress us. Be creative. Show, don't tell.

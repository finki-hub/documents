# Настан за информирање за новиот повик на FF4EuroHPC – ФИНКИ

Dear all,

It is our particular pleasure to invite you to a virtual event organised within the EuroCC project—National Competence Centres for High-Performance Computing—which will be held on **01.07.2021** and will present the second FF4EuroHPC open call for collaboration among industry, academia and HPC centres, with the aim of enabling business users, particularly small and medium-sized enterprises, to use HPC. **Through these calls, FF4EuroHPC provides grants for collaboration among the participating partners.**

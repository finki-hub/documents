# Гостинско предавање во МАНУ – ФИНКИ

Macedonian Academy of Sciences and Arts (MASA) in cooperation with the Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering at the University “Ss. Cyril and Methodius”- Skopje organized quest lecture on inspiration from nature**: sensors for surgery and underwater marine research, and invites all interested to attend**.

The lecture will be held by proffesor **Viktor Gruev, Junior Professor of Computer Engineering, Bio-Medical Electronics and the Washington University in St. Louis**.

The lecture will be held in the amphitheater of the **MASA 17.11.2015 (Tuesday) at 12:00.**

The abstract for the lecture and brief biography of the lecturer can be viewed the following [link](http://194.149.138.7/Content/contentFiles/news/Oglasi/2015%20-%20Bio%20Inspired%20Imaging.pdf).

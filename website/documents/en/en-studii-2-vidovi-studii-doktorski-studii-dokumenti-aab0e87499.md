**PHD STUDIES**

Doctoral studies at FINKI are organized by **[the UKIM Doctoral School](https://ukim.edu.mk/doktorska-shkola/)**. At **[the UKIM website](https://ukim.edu.mk/)** more details can be found about the organization of the studies, including the **[structure of third-cycle study programmes](https://ukim.edu.mk/doktorska-shkola/struktura/struchen-sovet-na-doktorski-studii/)**, **[study rulebook](https://ukim.edu.mk/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/pravilnik-iii_ciklus-31.12.2020-glasnik_530.pdf)**and **[study guide](https://ukim.edu.mk/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/vodich-za-studenti.pdf)**.

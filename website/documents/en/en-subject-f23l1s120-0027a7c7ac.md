| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | The student will acquire the ability to think critically and develop skills in thinking beyond standard norms and principles. They will be able to frame the problem and present it correctly, as well as solve unstructured problems. They will have improved general problem-solving skills for various types of problems that stimulate logical thinking. |
| 11. | Course content | Lectures:  1. Introduction  2. Understand the problem  3. Deal with intuition  4. Make a model  5. Mathematical principles  6. Limitations  7. Optimization  8. Probability  9. Statistics  10. Simulations  11. Pattern recognition  12. Strategies |
| 12. | Learning methods | Lectures supported by slide presentations, interactive lectures and exercises, case studies, guest lecturers, independent preparation and defense of a project assignment, and learning in an electronic environment (forums, consultations). |

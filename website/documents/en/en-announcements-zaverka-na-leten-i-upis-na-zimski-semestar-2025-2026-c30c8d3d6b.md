# Заверка на летен и упис на зимски семестар 2025/2026 – ФИНКИ

Dear Students,

The summer semester will conclude and the winter semester will begin. **од 17.09.2025 до 23.09.2025**. You can view the elective subjects that will be activated on the following **[LINK](https://raspredelba.finki.ukim.mk/active-subjects?name=&semesterType=WINTER&results=1000)**.

Information on financial obligations is available at the following **[LINK](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/mk/zafakultetot/finansii)**.

A further notice will follow regarding the submission of the statistical form and obtaining the stamp in the index.

Dear students,

The signing of the summer semester and the enrollment of the winter semester will take place **from 17.09.2025 to 23.09.2025**. The elective courses that will be activated can be reviewed at the following **[Link](https://raspredelba.finki.ukim.mk/active-subjects?name=&semesterType=WINTER&results=1000)**.

Information about the financial obligations is available at the following **[Link](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/mk/zafakultetot/finansii)**.

Additional information will follow regarding the submission of the statistical form and obtaining the stamp in the index.

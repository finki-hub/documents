# MSc Aleksandar Tenev

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/aleksandar.tenev_.jpg)

[aleksandar.tenev@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:aleksandar.tenev@finki.ukim.mk)

He was born in 1988 in Strumica. He graduated from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje in 2010. He enrolled in a master's program at the same faculty in 2010. His master's studies are in the field of bioinformatics. During his undergraduate studies, he was consistently awarded for his academic achievements. He began working as a teaching assistant at the Institute for Computer Engineering and Informatics at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje in 2009. His teaching responsibilities include conducting lectures and laboratory exercises for courses in structured programming, object-oriented programming, introduction to the internet, and programming methodologies. His areas of research interest are machine learning and artificial intelligence, neuroscience (functional and anatomical connectivity in the human brain), and bioinformatics (structure of DNA and protein interactions).

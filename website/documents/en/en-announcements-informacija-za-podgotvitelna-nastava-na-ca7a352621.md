# Информација за подготвителна настава на – ФИНКИ

We would like to inform you that based on the number of registered candidates, the preparatory course in mathematics and programming will be held. Due to the high interest and the messages we have received, the registration deadline has been extended to Sunday, September 17, 2023, at 4:00 PM (you can find details about the course and how to register at this link). [link](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/mk/content/%D0%BF%D0%BE%D0%B4%D0%B3%D0%BE%D1%82%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BB%D0%BD%D0%B0-%D0%BD%D0%B0%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B0%D0%B2%D0%B0-%D0%B7%D0%B0-%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%82%D0%B5-%D1%81%D1%82%D1%83%D0%B4%D0%B5%D0%BD%D1%82%D0%B8)

The preparatory programming course will begin on Monday (9/18/2023) and will run through Friday (9/22/2023) in-person. A detailed schedule will be sent to registered candidates in the coming days.

The preparatory mathematics course will be held in a hybrid format from September 23, 2023, to September 29, 2023. A detailed schedule, along with information on how to access the course via FINKI's remote learning systems, will be sent to registered candidates during the following week.

In order to attend the preparatory course, you must submit a payment receipt for the appropriate amount.

– 3000 denars for preparatory programming instruction (if you are only taking programming)

– 5000 denars for preparatory math classes (if you are only taking math)

– 8000 denars for preparatory instruction in programming and mathematics (if you have selected both courses)

The payment slip (available [here](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/mk/zafakultetot/finansii)) should contain the following information:

Inquiry purpose: preparatory course in programming/mathematics/programming and mathematics (depending on which course you will be taking)

Recipient title: RM Budget – UKIМ – Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering, Skopje

Recipient's bank: NBRM

Recipient's account: 100000000063095

account of a budget user: 1600161700 788 13

Income code and program: 723012 41

The payment receipt must be submitted by September 20, 2023, if you have selected programming or both courses, or by September 25, 2023, if you have selected only mathematics. Candidates who have chosen to take the mathematics course through FINKI's distance learning systems will receive additional information on when to submit the payment slip.

For any questions, you can write to us at [podgotvitelna@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:podgotvitelna@finki.ukim.mk).

We wish you a successful teaching.

# Магистерски студии – ФИНКИ

### Second-cycle studies

In accordance with **[The Call](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/konkurs-ii-2026-27-mk.pdf)** for the enrollment of students in second-cycle studies in the study programmes of Ss. Cyril and Methodius University, candidate applications for the first admission period at the Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering will be held from **October 01** to **October 16** **2026**. Candidate enrollment will take place on October 21 and 22, 2026.

**For more efficient processing of enrollment documents, the paper documents should be submitted at the counter of the Student Affairs Office – FINKI on the stated dates from 09:00 to 12:00.**

The documents that interested candidates must submit when applying for admission to second-cycle studies at FINKI are given in the section [Required Documents](https://finki.ukim.mk/upisi/magisterski-studii/potrebni-dokumenti/).

**NOTE: Enrollment in second-cycle studies must first be completed electronically, through the system for [enrollment of new students at UKIM](http://upisi.ukim.mk/).**

Interested candidates will be able to choose to enroll in one of the following study programmes:

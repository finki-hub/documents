# Прелиминарни ранг листи за првиот уписен рок за академската 2023/2024 година – ФИНКИ

They have been published**[Preliminary rankings](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/finki-rang-lista.pdf)** for the first enrollment period for the 2023/2024 academic year.

The submission of **[objection](https://forms.office.com/e/11sv00nHJR)** The preliminary ranking of applicants for the first admissions period will be conducted. **until September 6, 2023** year **until 12:00**.

All candidates who **Not ranked** They have the opportunity to clarify their interest in the study programs that have open spots.**Computer Engineering** – state and private quota, **Computer Science** – private quota, **Professional Studies in Programming** – private quota). The new ranking will be made without changing the order of the previously ranked candidates. Changing your program of study or mode of study is done by filling out the **[Statement for Supplementing Selected Study Programs](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/izjava_za_dopolnuvanje_ili_promena_na_izbranite_studiski_programi_3.pdf)**and sending of **[contact@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:contact@finki.ukim.mk)**latest **until September 6, 2023**year **until 12:00**.

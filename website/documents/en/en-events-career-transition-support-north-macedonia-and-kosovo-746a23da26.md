# Career Transition Support North Macedonia and Kosovo – ФИНКИ

Are you studying at a Macedonian public university? Do you want to gain practical experience to improve your skills and job prospects? Then this program is for you!

We are pleased to present the Career Transition Support internship program in companies in Macedonia or Kosovo, of your choice. This program by the World University Service (WUS) is funded and supported by the government of the German state of North Rhine-Westphalia.

To participate, you need to register at the link below:

&lt;https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSc_i9XqhM-EX8y5cjurCPCu5eRRyQqhHMHrTQW4SOh60FcM0Q/viewform?usp=sf_link&gt;

Zoom link: [https://us06web.zoom.us/j/81946850383](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://us06web.zoom.us/j/81946850383&sa=D&source=calendar&usd=2&usg=AOvVaw1LoWcyuMwNR0MYONIypjmH)

**Who can apply?**

• Students who are in the final phase of their studies (will graduate in October or November 2023);

• Those who graduated no more than 6 months before the application deadline (11/30/2023)

• Those who are in a master's program.

**What We Offer:**

Training on how to find your dream job (creating a resume, cover letter, and your first interview).

-15,000 denars per month for a period of 3-6 months

– Any kind of consultation for internships and starting your own business

– 2 training courses on how to make your internship successful

Fifty scholarships are available for 50 students (priority will be given to those who submit the best applications and those from low-income families).

For more information, you can contact Dimitar Kalyoshevski at [northmacedonia@wusgermany.de](mailto:northmacedonia@wusgermany.de) or at 072 227 940

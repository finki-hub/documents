# Прв академски час – 2021/2022 – ФИНКИ

Dear freshmen, it is our special honor to invite you to attend the first academic class for the 2021/2022 academic year at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering at the University „St. Cyril and Methodius“ in Skopje.

The event will be held online on October 4, 2021, starting at 12:00 PM and will be available at the following **[LINK](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meetup-join/19%3ameeting_Y2VkYTFkMGItYzA1Mi00MDRjLThkZmItMTAxZTgzYjg0MTc4%40thread.v2/0?context=%7b%22Tid%22%3a%22f0cac47b-e2b3-4e1b-a52f-487d2d996288%22%2c%22Oid%22%3a%22b462cddd-7936-4ae7-982c-ad11fc56fb0b%22%2c%22IsBroadcastMeeting%22%3atrue%7d&btype=a&role=a)**.

The following will speak at the first academic session:

- **Prof. Dr. Nikola Jankulovski**– Rector of the University “St. Cyril and Methodius”,
- **Prof. Dr. Ivan Chorbev**– Dean of the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering
- **Mr. Aleksandar Stamboliev**– Director of the University of Macedonia Business Accelerator (BAU)
- **Renata Sokolovska**– President of the Faculty Student Assembly (FSS) of FINK

We wish you a successful start to the new school year!

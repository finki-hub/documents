Vlatko Spasev was born in Sveti Nikole in 1997. He completed his primary education at Kiril i Metodij Primary School and his secondary education at Kocho Racin Secondary School in Sveti Nikole, graduating as the top student of his generation. During his education, he participated in numerous regional and national competitions in different fields and received several awards and distinctions.
He enrolled at the Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering in 2015 in the Computer Science and Engineering programme. During his studies, he was recognised as one of FCSE’s best students. He graduated in 2019 with a grade average of 9.53 and a bachelor’s thesis entitled “Camera Management System for Route Monitoring”. In the 2019/2020 academic year, he enrolled in postgraduate studies at FCSE in Software Engineering. In March 2018, he was appointed a laboratory assistant at FCSE and conducted laboratory classes in Structured and Object-Oriented Programming, Microprocessor Systems, and Fundamentals of Web Design. In November 2019, he was appointed a demonstrator and began conducting tutorial and laboratory classes in Internet Programming, User Interface Design Patterns, Fundamentals of Web Design, Object-Oriented Programming, and Introduction to Cognitive Science. His research interests include robotics, software engineering, the management and control of unmanned aerial vehicles, embedded systems and sensor systems.

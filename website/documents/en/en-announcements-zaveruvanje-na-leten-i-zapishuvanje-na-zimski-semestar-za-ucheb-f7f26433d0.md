# Заверување на летен и запишување на зимски семестар за учебната 2023/2024 година за студентите од втора, трета и четврта година на – ФИНКИ

The registration for the summer semester and the enrollment for the winter semester will take place from **15.09.2023** to **22.09.2023** year. You can see the details in the official announcement at **[Contribution](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/upis_zimski_2023_2024_1.pdf).**

Also in [**Contribution**](https://raspredelba.finki.ukim.mk/active-subjects?name=&semesterType=WINTER)This is the list of courses for the Winter semester of the 2023/2024 academic year. The list is preliminary, and the activation of elective courses depends on the number of students who register for the course.

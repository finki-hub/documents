# Dr\. Biljana Stojkoska

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/von.-prof.-d-r-biljana-risteska-stojkoska--731x1024.jpg)

Born in 1982 in Ohrid. She graduated and received her master's degree from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje in 2006 and 2008, respectively. She is currently working on her doctoral dissertation. She began working as a teaching assistant at the Institute of Computer Engineering and Informatics at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering in Skopje in 2005. In 2009, she was appointed a junior assistant. She is responsible for conducting labs for courses in the areas of algorithms and data structures, distributed computer systems, expert systems, mobile information systems, design of embedded computer components, and information systems. As part of her research work to date, she has published several papers in conference proceedings and in international scientific journals.

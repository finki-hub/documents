She was born in 1986 in Skopje. She graduated from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje in 2009. She is currently enrolled in a master's program at the same faculty, in the Content-Based Retrieval track. During her undergraduate studies, she was continuously awarded for her academic excellence. In 2005, as a student, she received the “13th November” award from the City of Skopje for her outstanding achievements in education. She gained her first work experience at the information services company “Nextsense“ d.o.o. In 2009, she began working as a teaching assistant at the Institute for Computer Technology and Informatics at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje. She is currently involved in teaching exercises for courses in the areas of algorithms and data structures, object-oriented systems, information systems, object-oriented analysis and design, and intelligent information systems. Her research interests include artificial intelligence, bioinformatics, data mining, machine learning, and databases.

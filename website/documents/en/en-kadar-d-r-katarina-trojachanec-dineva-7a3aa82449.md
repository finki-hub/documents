# Dr\. Katarina Trojachanec Dineva

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/doc.-d-r-katarina-trojachanec-dineva--731x1024.jpg)

[katarina.trojacanec@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:katarina.trojacanec@finki.ukim.mk)

She was born in 1986 in Kumanovo. She graduated from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje in 2008. She earned her master's degree from the same faculty in 2010, with a thesis in the field of content-based retrieval of magnetic resonance images. During her undergraduate studies, she was continuously awarded for her academic achievements. As the top student of her graduating class, she was honored by the Faculty, the University “St. Cyril and Methodius,” and various other foundations, and she received an engineering ring from the Association of Engineers of Macedonia. She gained her first work experience at the Faculty of Informatics of the European University in 2008. She began working as a teaching assistant at the Institute of Computer Technology and Informatics at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje in 2008. She is currently responsible for conducting lecture and laboratory exercises for courses in structured programming, object-oriented programming, internet programming, and user interfaces. Her areas of research interest are image processing, data mining, machine learning, and software engineering.

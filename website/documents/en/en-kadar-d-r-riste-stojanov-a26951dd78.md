# Dr\. Riste Stojanov

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/doc.-d-r-riste-stojanov-731x1024.jpg)

He was born in 1985 in Štip. He graduated from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje in 2008, with a degree in Informatics and Computer Engineering. He earned his master's degree from the same faculty in 2010, with a thesis in the field of semantic web technologies. During his undergraduate studies, he was regularly awarded for his academic achievements. His first work experience was at the “Genrep” d.o.o. company in 2008. He began working as a teaching assistant at the Institute for Computer Technology and Informatics at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje in 2009. His teaching responsibilities include conducting labs for courses in the areas of system software, network programming, and network software. As part of his research work to date, he has published ten research papers. He is the co-author of one book. His research interests include design patterns, the semantic web, parallel processing, distributed systems, computer networks, and unstructured data processing.

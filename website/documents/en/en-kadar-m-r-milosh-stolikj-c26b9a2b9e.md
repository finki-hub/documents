# MSc Milosh Stolikj

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/milosh.stolikj.jpg)

[milosh.stolikj@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:milosh.stolikj@finki.ukim.mk)

He was born in 1984 in Skopje. He graduated from „Gjorgi Pulevski“ Elementary School and „Rade Jovchevski – Korzhagin“ High School in Skopje. He graduated from the Faculty of Natural Sciences and Mathematics, Institute of Informatics in Skopje on January 27, 2007, with a thesis titled „Virtual Private Networks and Their Implementation in a Heterogeneous Environment“ under the mentorship of Prof. Dr. Marjan Gušev. He earned his master's degree from the Institute of Informatics at the Faculty of Natural Sciences and Mathematics in Skopje on October 14, 2010, with a master's thesis titled „Statistical Machine Translation of the Macedonian Language“ under the mentorship of Prof. Dr. Katerina Zdravkova.During his high school education, he participated in numerous computer science competitions, winning several awards, including first place in the 2002 Regional Informatics Competition, third place in the 2001 National Informatics Competition, and a Bronze Medal at the 6th Macedonian Informatics Olympiad in 2002.During his studies, he received awards and commendations for above-average academic performance, and in his final years, he also worked as a teaching assistant at the Institute of Informatics and as a programmer at CableTel in Skopje. In 2006, he worked as an external expert collaborator at New York University in Skopje, teaching Systems Administration, Introduction to Programming, and Computer Networks. During this time, he obtained all four instructor certifications from the Cisco Certified Network Associate program and worked as a Cisco instructor at the Institute of Informatics. Since 2006, he has volunteered with the Macedonian-language edition of Wikipedia, and since 2009, he has participated in the international projects SEEGRID-SCI, EGI, and HP-SEE. Since 2009, he has worked at the Institute of Informatics at the Faculty of Natural Sciences and Mathematics as a professional associate. Including a previous position as a teaching assistant, he has led labs for courses in system software, wireless networks, intelligent systems, parallel and distributed processing, operating systems, microprocessors and microcomputers, and computer network design.

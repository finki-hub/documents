# Одлука за распишување на избори за претседател и членови на факултетското студентско собрание на – ФИНКИ

It is being published **The decision to call for elections for the president and members of the faculty student assembly** at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering (FINKI) of the University „St. Cyril and Methodius“ in Skopje.

- **Date of the elections:** June 30, 2025 (Monday)
- **Time:** from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM
- **Location:** FINKI, Skopje

-Elections are being called for **president and 9 (nine) members** at the faculty student assembly.
The elections will be held by secret ballot.
The candidates are determined on **Student count by academic year**, which will be held on **June 20, 2025 at 4:00 PM** at the FINKI Amphitheater and online.
All candidates must submit **Consent to Participate**, and the candidates for president and **Work program**, no later than **June 24, 2025**.

The details of the procedure, the composition of the electoral commission, the criteria for candidacy, and other information are contained in the Decision itself.

The decision is available as an attachment to this post.

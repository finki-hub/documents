# Менторска програма за градење капацитети за научна комуникација – ФИНКИ

Dear all,

Within the European project **[STREAM-IT](https://streamit-project.eu/)**, the Foundation for Management and Industrial Research is organizing **a mentoring programme for building scientific communication capacities** for students (undergraduate, master’s and postgraduate studies) and researchers from STEM fields (science, technology, engineering and mathematics).

![STREAM-IT Mentor Program](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/streamit-project.png)

The programme enables participants to develop their communication and presentation skills and compete in a national science communication competition, with the possibility for the winner to participate in an international competition in Budapest in June 2026.

**Application deadline:** 05.12.2025

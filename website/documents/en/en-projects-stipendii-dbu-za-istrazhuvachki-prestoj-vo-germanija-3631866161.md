# Стипендии ДБУ за истражувачки престој во Германија – ФИНКИ

Over the past decade, the Macedonian Ecological Society has actively cooperated with the German Federal Environmental Foundation (Deutsche Bundesstiftung Umwelt), which last year marked the 25th anniversary of its internship programme in Germany for students from South-East European countries. Students may undertake a research stay of six to twelve months at organisations, companies, institutes or universities in Germany.

The scholarship programme supports a broad range of professions, provided that the proposed project concerns environmental protection, ecology, sustainable development, climate change or renewable energy. More recently, the programme has also supported digitalisation projects with a connection to ecology or the environment.

On 23 January 2023, on the [Zoom platform at 17:00](https://www.facebook.com/events/411032541207035?ref=newsfeed), DBU and the Macedonian Ecological Society will organise an online information session on the conditions for internships in Germany.

Further information about the scholarships is available at the following link:

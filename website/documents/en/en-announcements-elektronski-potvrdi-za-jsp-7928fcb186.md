# Електронски потврди за ЈСП – ФИНКИ

![index_1.jpg](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/index_1.jpg)

Dear students,

Starting today (Wednesday, 10/26/2022) the process of **Issuance of annual/monthly public transportation cards** is significant **eased** using electronic receipts.

At the ticket office of **Public Transport** which is located at **Pedagogska (across from Hotel Karposh)** You can get an JSP ticket. **without carrying a paper confirmation** for a regular student.

The only remaining document required is the standard one. **Request for a JSP ticket** – **Index is not needed**.

In the future, based on the collaboration with JSP – Skopje, FINKI plans to make this functionality available at other locations as well.

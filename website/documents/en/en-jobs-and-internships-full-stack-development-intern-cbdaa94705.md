# Full\-Stack Development Intern – ФИНКИ

---

## Description

Location: Hybrid (Inscale office + working from home)
Duration: 3 months, with the possibility of a further 3-month extension based on performance
Team: Verba AI

Role Description

As a Full-Stack Intern, you will work on developing and improving the Verba AI web application and internal tool. You will contribute to building interfaces, back-end functionality and integrations with AI systems.

Responsibilities:
• Development of new front-end and back-end functionality
• Work with React, Node.js, TypeScript or related technologies
• Integration with REST APIs and AI modules
• Support in developing the internal Verba administration panel
• Optimisation of UX/UI components
• Collaboration with the Machine Learning and Data Science team to improve the product

Required Qualifications:
• Basic knowledge of JavaScript/TypeScript
• Knowledge of React or another front-end technology
• Knowledge of Node.js, Express or another back-end solution
• Basic database skills (SQL or NoSQL)
• Excellent command of English
• Creativity, an organised approach and a willingness to learn

What We Offer:
• Work on a real AI product used by companies across Europe
• Mentoring by experienced engineers and the business team
• A flexible hybrid working model
• The possibility of full-time employment following successful completion of the internship
• A certificate of successful internship completion

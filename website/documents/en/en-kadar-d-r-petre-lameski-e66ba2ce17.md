# Dr\. Petre Lameski

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/prof.-d-r-petre-lameski-731x1024.jpg)

[petre.lameski@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:petre.lameski@finki.ukim.mk)

### Regular consultation hours

Day: Wednesday

11:00 - 13:00

Classroom: Dean's Office

He was born in 1985 in Kavadarci. In 2008, he graduated from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje. He earned his master's degree from the same faculty in 2010, with a thesis in the field of robotics. During his studies, he was regularly on the Dean's List for academic excellence. Since 2008, he has worked as a teaching assistant at the Institute for Computer Engineering and Informatics at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology in Skopje. His teaching duties include conducting labs for courses in robotics, distributed computer systems, artificial intelligence, data analysis algorithms, information systems, and mobile information systems. His areas of scientific and research interest are machine intelligence and learning, intelligent mobile robots, time series data analysis, and cognitive systems.

# Документи – Заштита на лични податоци – ФИНКИ

[Information](https://finki.ukim.mk/en/za-nas/administracija-i-dokumenti/zashtita-na-lichni-podatoci/)
[Documents](https://finki.ukim.mk/en/za-nas/administracija-i-dokumenti/zashtita-na-lichni-podatoci/dokumenti/)

The Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering – FINK at the University „St. Cyril and Methodius“ in Skopje, within the scope of its activities, processes personal data of students, applicants for enrollment, employees, external collaborators, visitors, and other natural persons.

FINKI processes personal data in accordance with **The Personal Data Protection Act** and applicable regulations, in compliance with the principles of lawfulness, transparency, purpose limitation, data minimization, accuracy, storage limitation, and integrity and confidentiality.

**1. Personal Data Controller**

**Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering – FINK**
University „St. Cyril and Methodius“ in Skopje
16 Rudezh Boshkovich Street, 1000 Skopje
Republic of North Macedonia

FINKI, within the scope of its competencies and activities, processes personal data in order to fulfill its statutory, educational, scientific research, administrative, and other functions.

**2. Data subjects**

Personal data may particularly refer to:

- candidates for enrollment in studies;
- undergraduate, master's, and doctoral students;
- Graduates and alumni;
- faculty and staff;
- administrative and technical personnel;
- job applicants;
- external collaborators and engaged individuals;
- participants in projects, conferences, training, and other activities;
- users of FINKI's electronic services and systems;
- visitors to the website and other individuals whose data is processed within the Faculty's legal authority.

**3. Data Protection Officer**

FINKI has a certain **Personal Data Protection Officer**, which carries out the tasks established by the Personal Data Protection Act.

**Personal Data Protection Officer:**
[Igor Cvetanovski](https://finki.ukim.mk/kadar/m-r-igor-cvetanovski/)

**E-mail:** [igor.cvetanovski@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:igor.cvetanovski@finki.ukim.mk)

You may contact the Officer with questions regarding the processing and protection of your personal data and to exercise your rights as established by law.

---

**4. Rights of data subjects**

Data subjects have the right, in accordance with applicable legal provisions, to request:

- information about the processing of their personal data;
- access to personal data;
- correction and completion of inaccurate or incomplete data;
- erasure of personal data, when the legal conditions are met;
- restriction of processing;
- objection to processing, when the legal conditions are met;
- portability of personal data, where applicable;
- withdrawal of given consent, when the processing is based on consent;
- other rights in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act.

**5. Request for the exercise of rights**

The data subject may submit a written request to FINKI to exercise their rights.

The request can be submitted:

**Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering – FINK**
16 Ruggiero Boscovich Street
1000 Skopje

or electronically to:

**[igor.cvetanovski@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:igor.cvetanovski@finki.ukim.mk)**

The request must contain sufficient information to identify the applicant and the right that is sought to be enforced.

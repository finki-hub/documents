Dimitar Peshovski was born in 2001 in Bitola, Macedonia. He completed his primary education at „St. Clement of Ohrid“ Elementary School in Bitola and his secondary education at „Josip Broz Tito“ High School in Bitola, with consistently excellent results.

In 2020, they enrolled in undergraduate studies at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering in the Computer Science program, He graduated in 2024 with a grade point average of 10.00, defending his thesis „Analysis of Economic Structure and Complexity through the Construction of Input-Output Production Networks using Generative Artificial Intelligence“ under the mentorship of Prof. Dr. Dimitar Trajanov. During his studies, he was awarded as one of the best students of the Faculty every year. In his final year of studies, he was employed as a lab assistant at the Faculty for the courses Introduction to Data Science and Operating Systems.

In 2024, he enrolled in a master's program at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering in the Data Science in Computer Science and Engineering track. In the same year, he was selected as a teaching assistant for the faculty.

His areas of scientific research interest are: data science, machine learning, artificial intelligence, parallel and distributed processing, and software engineering. In his research work to date, he has published several scientific papers as an author and co-author in domestic and international conferences and journals.

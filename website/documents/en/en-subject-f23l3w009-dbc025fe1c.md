| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | The goal of the course is for students to learn the main concepts of object-oriented analysis and design. To become familiar with refactoring techniques, design patterns, and various software architectures. Upon completion of the course, candidates will be able to identify limitations and assess the qualities of software systems. For a given software specification, they will be able to assess its completeness and  consistency, as well as designing a software architecture in accordance with the given requirements. |
| 11. | Course content | (1) Design concepts, definition of design, basic problems in design (for example, persistent data, warehouse management, and exceptions)  (1) Design context within multiple software development life cycles, design principles (information hiding, cohesion, and coupling)  (1) Interactions between design and requirements, design for quality attributes (for example, reliability, usability, sustainability, performance, testing, safety, and fault tolerance)  (1) Design compromises, design strategies, functional design, object-oriented design  (1) Data-driven design, aspect-oriented design, architectural design  (3) Architectural styles, templates, and frameworks  (1) Architectural exchanges between different attributes  (1) Hardware and system engineering issues in software architecture, requirements for traceability in architecture  (1) Service-Oriented and Cloud Architectures  (1) Architectures for network, mobile, and embedded systems  (1) The relationship between product architecture, organizational structure, and the market |

| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | Ecoinformatics is the science of information (informatics) in ecology and environmental science. It integrates ecological and informatics sciences to define entities and natural processes with a language common to humans and computers. Ecoinformatics aims to facilitate environmental research and management by developing ways to access and integrate knowledge from different sources of environmental information, and by developing new algorithms that enable the combination of different environmental data to test ecological hypotheses. |

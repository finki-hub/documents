# Прелиминарни ранг листи за првиот уписен рок за академската 2022/2023 година – ФИНКИ

They have been published [Preliminary rankings](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/preliminarni_rang_listi.pdf) for the first enrollment period for the 2022/2023 academic year.

Objections to the preliminary ranking list of applicants for the first admissions round must be submitted by 12:00 PM on September 6, 2022.

[Objections](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/prigovor_za_preliminarna_raspredelba17951.pdf) are delivered to the email [upisi-finki@ukim.edu.mk](mailto:upisi-finki@ukim.edu.mk) with the message subject: PRIGOVOR[ID], where ID represents the number of the application to which the objection pertains.

All candidates who are NOT ranked or who wish to change or supplement their choice of study programs or mode of study (state quota or co-financing) have the opportunity to apply for study programs with available seats and be ranked for a program that is not fully filled. The new ranking will be made without changing the order of the previously ranked candidates. The change of the chosen study program or mode of study is made by filling out the [Declaration for the Supplementation or Change of Selected Study Programs](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/izjava_za_dopolnuvanje_ili_promena_na_izbranite_studiski_programi_2.pdf)

Declarations to supplement or change selected study programs (enrollment in a study program with available seats) or to modify a declaration for studying under a co-financing quota (if this was not done on the internal application form) will be accepted via email. [upisi-finki@ukim.edu.mk](mailto:upisi-finki@ukim.edu.mk) by 12:00 PM on September 6, 2022. The subject line of the message should read IZJAVA[ID], where ID is the number of the application to which the statement pertains.

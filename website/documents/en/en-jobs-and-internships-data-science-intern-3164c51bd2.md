# Data Science Intern – ФИНКИ

---

## Description

Location: Hybrid (Inscale office + working from home)
Duration: 3 months, with the possibility of a further 3-month extension based on performance
Team: Verba AI

Role Description

As a Data Science Intern, you will work on data processing, analysis, and visualization, supporting the development of AI models and production features. You will participate in building datasets, metrics, and algorithms used to improve the product.

Responsibilities:
• Systematization, processing, and analysis of real data
• Creating visualizations, reports, and metrics
• Support in preparing data for ML/LLM models
• Working with Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib)
• Testing model performance and preparing recommendations
• Work with the product and engineering team to improve products

Required Qualifications:
• Foundations in statistics, data analysis, and modeling
• Hands-on experience with Python
• Knowledge of SQL is a plus
• Machine learning fundamentals – preferred
• Excellent command of English
• Analytical thinking and attention to detail

What We Offer:
• Work on a real AI product used by companies across Europe
• Mentoring by experienced engineers and the business team
• A flexible hybrid working model
• The possibility of full-time employment following successful completion of the internship
• A certificate of successful internship completion

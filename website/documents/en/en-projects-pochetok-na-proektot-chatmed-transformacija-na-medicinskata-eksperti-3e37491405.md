# Почеток на проектот ChatMED – Трансформација на медицинската експертиза преку генеративна Вештачка интелигенција – ФИНКИ

On November 12, 2024, starting at 11:00, a public event will be held in the FINKI amphitheater to mark the launch of the project **ChatMED** – an initiative dedicated to transforming medical expertise through innovative solutions based on generative Artificial Intelligence.

The project, whose detailed description can be found at [this link](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101159214 "https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101159214"), aims to show how generative Artificial Intelligence can improve the quality of medical services and bridge the challenges faced by the healthcare and IT sectors in our country. We will focus on what is necessary to keep pace with global trends and how, through cooperation and innovation, we can improve medical practice.

The event will include:

- Presentation of the project and its potential,
- A series of presentations of related successful solutions by our partners,
- A discussion on what is missing in order to improve medical practice through new technologies,
- Cocktail reception and small exhibition – networking opportunities.

To participate, please fill in the following [form](https://forms.office.com/e/UWc2fR9qVU "https://forms.office.com/e/UWc2fR9qVU")Your presence will be especially valuable for advancing cooperation between the public/private healthcare sector and the scientific-research IT sector.

We look forward to sharing visions for the future of healthcare.

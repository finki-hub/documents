# Решение за доделување на стипендии на кандидатите од прв упсен рок 2022/2023 – ФИНКИ

**Top 10 candidates** with completed high school graduation, in the regular student category, during the first admissions period **Scholarship recipients from UКИМ**, ...that is, they are exempt from paying tuition fees only for the 2022/2023 academic year. The list of these students has been posted. **[here](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/resenie_stipendii_ukim_najdobri_10.pdf).** Students become FINKI scholarship recipients and retain the scholarship throughout their studies only if they have **Average over 9.50**.

Candidates who have won diplomas at national and international competitions in computer science, mathematics, and physics are **sponsored by FINKI with** **Waiver of co-payment**. Candidates who have attached their diplomas to the submitted documents and have been exempted are as follows:**[Solution for FINKI Scholars](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u620/resenie_stipendii_finki_diplomi.pdf)**. Students retain the scholarship throughout their studies only if they have **Average over 9.50**. Candidates who have such diplomas but have not submitted them may do so on September 12, 2022, when they bring the other documents.

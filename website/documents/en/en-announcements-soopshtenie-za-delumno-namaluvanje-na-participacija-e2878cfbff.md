# Соопштение за делумно намалување на партиципација – ФИНКИ

Dear Students,

In accordance with established practice, and for the 2022/23 academic year, the Faculty will allocate funds to partially reduce tuition for students from socially disadvantaged families and children of single parents.

FINKI students who need a waiver of part of their tuition for the 2022/23 academic year can submit a request for a partial tuition reduction by providing supporting documents.

The documents, along with the application form containing personal data, index number, field of study, and quota, must be submitted to the faculty archives no later than October 30, 2022.

Note: The eventual reduction in participation is on an annual basis and is calculated in the summer semester of the 2022/23 academic year.

| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | Familiarizing students with the current elements of the field of digital libraries (DL): Fundamentals and architectures of digital libraries, technologies, management, organization and development of digital resources, knowledge presentation and its discovery (information gathering), metadata, schemas and standards, and relevant intellectual property rights. Depending on current interest, students will be introduced to specific digital library application software, operational practices, and their evaluation. Upon completion of the course, the student is expected to demonstrate knowledge of the basic elements of a digital library, technologies for its development and construction, methods of application, and its management and organization. |
| 11. | Course content | Introduction, definition, and overview of digital libraries (DL). History, basic characteristics, and vision of DL. Types of digital content and their organization, metadata, format-specific metadata, standards and practices in DL (OAI-PMH, Z39.50, Dublin Core, and others), ensuring interoperability. Digital library architectures and systems, representation schemes, and content organization. User-oriented design of digital libraries. Methods and principles of information gathering and semantic information gathering. Results presentation and data interaction, usability, evaluation. Management of digital library support systems. Performance enhancement. Comparison and connection of digital and traditional libraries. Protection of digital content, copyright. Examples of digital libraries: textual DBs, ancient manuscripts, video recordings, maps, and others. The role of digital libraries in education and e-learning. Institutional, social, and professional challenges of DLs. Future development trends in DLs. |

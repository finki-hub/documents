# Летна школа за сајбер безбедност на ФИНКИ – ФИНКИ

The Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering (FCSE) at Ss. Cyril and Methodius University in Skopje is hosting the first BEiNG-WISE Summer School. The event is being held on 26–27 June 2024 and is dedicated to “Cybersecurity and the Human Factor in the Era of Advanced Communication Technologies”. Organised by COST Action CA22104, the School brings together students, researchers and professionals from across Europe to explore and address cybersecurity challenges. Participants engage in interdisciplinary research and practical applications with the aim of advancing their skills and knowledge in this critical field.

![picture3.jpg](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/picture3.jpg)

The Summer School is hosted by Professor Vesna Dimitrova and Professor Ivan Chorbev.

For further information about the School, visit the Action’s website **[BEiNG WISE](https://beingwise.eu/first-training-school/)**.

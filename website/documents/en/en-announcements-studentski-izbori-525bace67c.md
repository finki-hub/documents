# Студентски избори – ФИНКИ

Dear colleagues,

According to the FSS FINKI Statute, students who wish to run in this year's elections for student representatives must run in a short**Student meeting**organized by the current FSS, and then to submit**statement**to the Faculty Archives.

The nomination meeting will be held.**today at 8:00 PM on this[link](https://meet.google.com/tkg-hndx-kgz)**. The statement is in the announcement (objAveno nа mail) and you fill it out for member or president, depending on what you are running for (for example, if you are running for member, wherever it says member/president it should say only member). The completed statement can be submitted in person to the Archive or scanned to [arhiva@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:arhiva@finki.ukim.mk), no later than**Monday, April 3, 2023, until 4:00 PM**. If someone cannot join the meeting tonight for valid reasons, their candidacy will remain valid as long as you notify us of their absence. [fss@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:fss@finki.ukim.mk) and submit a statement to the Archive.

We encourage all students who wish to represent their classmates in their major, or who would like to represent the entire student assembly as president, to apply for the current elections. Some of the duties of FSS members include:

- Advocating for students' interests and needs and responding to reported problems.
- Organizing various types of events, workshops, parties, etc.
- Collaboration with the Student Affairs Office in creating lecture and exam schedules.
- Social media management for FSS FINKI
- Participation in the Academic-Scientific Council
- Participation in the University Student Assembly of UKI

Over the next week, there will be an additional meeting open to all student candidates, where they can introduce themselves to their peers and elaborate on what they will advocate for within FSS. Afterwards, there will also be an opportunity to ask the candidates questions.

# Заверување на зимски и запишување на летен семестар за учебната 2023/2024 година за студентите на – ФИНКИ

Graduation for the winter semester and enrollment for the summer semester will take place from **30.01.2024** to **February 10, 2024**. You can see the details in **[The official statement](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/upis_leten_2023_2024_1.pdf)** in support of.

Also attached is [**the list of items**](https://raspredelba.finki.ukim.mk/active-subjects?name=&semesterType=SUMMER) for the summer semester of the 2023/2024 academic year. The list is preliminary, and the activation of elective courses depends on the number of students who register for the course.

# Вебинар за Математичка едукација – ФИНКИ

On October 16, 2020 (the exact time will be announced soon), the iTEM Project is organizing a webinar in Mathematical Education.

The webinar will take place via Zoom and is specially tailored for math teachers and students.

All the information can be found here: &lt;http://www.athenaeuropeanuniversity.eu/webinar-in-mathematics-education/&gt;. The registration form can be found on the website and in the program as well.

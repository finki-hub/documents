| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | The goal of the course is to equip students with the ability to use state-of-the-art classroom techniques to capture students' interest and encourage them to acquire new knowledge during classroom instruction. Students will be trained to conduct active classroom instruction, using creative techniques appropriate to the students' ages and the material according to the curriculum. |

# Потребни документи – ФИНКИ

### **Documents for application and enrollment**

Before applying within the deadlines set by the Call, each interested candidate should register at**[www.upisi.ukim.mk](http://www.upisi.ukim.mk/)**and complete the electronic application from the iKnow system.

The number automatically assigned to the candidate by the electronic application system must also be entered in the application submitted for enrollment.

**The following documents should be submitted with the application:**

1. University enrollment application – signed A1 form (pink sheet, available for purchase on the day of document submission from bookstores near the Faculty)
2. Electronic application from the Faculty, internal application form (printed from the iKnow system, signed)
3. Original certificates from all school years for inspection and COPIES of them
4. Original document for passed State Matura (secondary education completion certificate) for inspection and a COPY of it
5. Original birth certificate for inspection and a COPY of it
6. Original proof of citizenship of the Republic of North Macedonia for inspection and a COPY of it
7. **[Statement on the authenticity and accuracy of the copies of the submitted documents](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/izjava_za_verodostojnost_i_podiganje_na_docs.pdf)**
8. Statement of consent for personal data processing (printed from the iKnow system, signed)
9. Proof of payment:
   - MKD 50.00 administrative fee (paid using a **[payment slip](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/admin_taksa-1.jpg)**or via **[SMS](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/upatstvo-za-taksa-preku-sms.pdf)**)
   - proof of payment of MKD 300 to the **[Faculty giro account](https://finki.ukim.mk/za-nas/administracija-i-dokumenti/finansii/)** (a template of the giro account can be obtained in the iKnow system, in the Submission section)

**For more efficient processing of enrollment documents, and in accordance with item XI of the UKIM admission call, the documents in paper form should be submitted at the FINKI premises on the stated dates from 09:00 to 14:30.**

| 10. | Objectives of the course programme (competences) | The course emphasizes the need to study elements of digital forensics and their practical application. The goal is to examine the phases of digital forensics and to apply them in practice using appropriate tools and methods. |
| 11. | Course content | Introduction to digital security disciplines that will help in developing, implementing, and auditing security systems. Introduction to elements related to digital security management. Study of designs and methods for conducting forensic IT investigations and analysis of forensic tools and their applications. Analysis of the principles involved in searching and collecting digital evidence from data storage devices and large data sets and their application. Analysis of the legal, social, and ethical challenges associated with the presentation of digital evidence. Analysis of practical digital forensics scenarios. |

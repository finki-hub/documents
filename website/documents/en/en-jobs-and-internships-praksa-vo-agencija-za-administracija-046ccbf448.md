# Пракса во Агенција за администрација – ФИНКИ

---

## Description

The Agency for Administration will conduct practical work during June, July and August 2026, intended for students who carry out practical work as part of the educational programme of higher education institutions.

Students currently enrolled in first-cycle or postgraduate studies in the following fields are eligible to participate:
- information sciences

The Agency is announcing a call for interested students to apply for the following positions:

1. Technical support for the Agency’s IT infrastructure and systems
2. Technical support for maintaining the Agency’s website
Candidates must meet the following requirements:

For item 1:

- Be full-time FINKI students who have completed the 2nd year
- Knowledge of Windows operating system administration will be considered an advantage
- Knowledge of computer networks and computer networking equipment will be considered an advantage
- Communication skills, organization, systematic and thorough work, timely and creative fulfillment of duties, a sense of teamwork and responsibility

For item 2:

- Be full-time FINKI students who have completed the 3rd year

The practical work will be carried out in accordance with the capacities and needs of the organizational units of the Agency for Administration.

For more information, contact kontakt@aa.mk.

She was born in 1997 in Skopje. She completed her high school education at the “Orce Николов” High School in the natural-mathematical track with consistently excellent grades. She continued her education at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering in the Computer Science and Engineering track. During the 2018/2019 and 2019/2020 academic years, she conducted laboratory exercises for the courses Database Systems, Object-Oriented Programming, and Object-Oriented Analysis and Design. She graduated in 2020 with a grade point average of 9.38, and her thesis is part of MANU's scientific research activities during the COVID pandemic. After graduating, she works at the company Loka as part of the Backend team. In 2023, she completes a study stay in Dresden, Germany, as a DAAD scholarship recipient. Since 2024, she has continued her studies at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering in the Cloud Computing program. She is the author and co-author of several scientific papers. Her areas of interest include cloud infrastructure and technologies, computer networks, distributed systems, and software development.

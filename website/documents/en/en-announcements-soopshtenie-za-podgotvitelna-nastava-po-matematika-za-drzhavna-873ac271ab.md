# Соопштение за подготвителна настава по математика за Државна матура – ФИНКИ

### Free Online Preparatory Mathematics Classes for the State Matura Examination

**Dear Secondary-School Graduates and Future Students,**

The Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering (FCSE) is pleased to announce **FREE online preparatory classes in MATHEMATICS** for the external examination subject **MATHEMATICS** within the State Matura examination, during the period **9 May 2026 – 3 June 2026**.

The classes will be delivered by FCSE professors and teaching assistants.

The preparatory classes are organised to help secondary-school graduates—our future students—achieve the best possible results in the Mathematics examination for the State Matura, which will be held on **6 June 2026**.

The preparatory classes will comprise **57 teaching hours** (**42 hours of core instruction + 15 hours of supplementary instruction**), distributed evenly across **13 days** and will cover all content prescribed by the Mathematics Examination Programme for the State Matura in general and vocational secondary education, which includes the following five areas:

- **AREA 1:** MATHEMATICAL LOGIC AND SETS **(weighting: 7%)**
- **AREA 2:** REAL AND COMPLEX NUMBERS **(weighting: 12%)**
- **AREA 3:** ALGEBRA **(weighting: 40%)**
- **AREA 4:** GEOMETRY **(weighting: 34%)**
- **AREA 5:** TRIGONOMETRY **(weighting: 7%)**

### Mathematics Examination Programme for the State Matura

The Mathematics Examination Programme for the State Matura is provided below.

#### **A. CONTENT**

#### **AREA 1: MATHEMATICAL LOGIC AND SETS**

**1. MATHEMATICAL LOGIC**

- The concept of a proposition
- Logical operations (negation, conjunction, disjunction, implication and equivalence)
- Compound propositions
- Laws of logic (commutative, associative and distributive laws; implication replacement; law of non-contradiction; law of the excluded middle; De Morgan’s laws; modus ponens; modus tollens; and hypothetical syllogism)

**2. SETS**

- The concepts of a set and a subset
- Methods of defining sets
- Operations on sets
- Laws governing operations on sets

#### **AREA 2: REAL AND COMPLEX NUMBERS**

**1. NUMBERS**

- Natural numbers
- Integers
- Rational numbers
- Real numbers
- Complex numbers

**2. OPERATIONS ON NUMBERS**

- Natural numbers
- Integers
- Rational numbers
- Real numbers
- Complex numbers

**3. PROPORTIONALITY OF QUANTITIES**

- Ratios and proportions; direct and inverse proportionality
- Simple and compound rule of three
- Percentage calculations: percentage, below one hundred and above one hundred
- Proportional division
- Percentage calculations: of one hundred, below one hundred and above one hundred
- Interest calculations

#### **AREA 3: ALGEBRA**

**1. ALGEBRAIC RATIONAL EXPRESSIONS**

- Powers with natural-number exponents
- Integral rational expressions: monomials and polynomials
- Fractional rational expressions

**2. LINEAR EQUATIONS AND LINEAR INEQUALITIES**

- Linear equations in one unknown
- Linear inequalities in one unknown

**3. LINEAR FUNCTIONS, SYSTEMS OF LINEAR EQUATIONS AND SYSTEMS OF LINEAR INEQUALITIES**

- Real-valued functions
- Linear functions
- Systems of two linear equations in two unknowns
- Systems of two linear inequalities in one unknown

**4. QUADRATIC EQUATIONS AND QUADRATIC FUNCTIONS**

- Incomplete and complete quadratic equations
- Nature of the solutions of a quadratic equation
- Vieta’s formulas and their application
- The concept of a quadratic function
- Graphs of elementary quadratic functions
- Canonical form of a quadratic function; vertex of a parabola

#### **AREA 4: GEOMETRY**

**1. GEOMETRIC FIGURES IN THE PLANE**

- Basic geometric figures
- Rays, line segments and half-planes
- Circumference and circle
- Angles and types of angles
- Polygonal lines and polygons
- Vectors; operations on vectors

**2. PERIMETER AND AREA OF PLANE FIGURES**

- The concepts of perimeter and area
- Perimeter and area of rectangles, triangles, quadrilaterals and regular polygons
- Circumference of a circle and length of a circular arc
- Area of a circle and parts of a circle

**3. GEOMETRIC SOLIDS**

- Points, lines and planes
- Geometric solids
- Polyhedra and solids of revolution
- Intersections of geometric solids with a plane

**4. SURFACE AREA AND VOLUME OF GEOMETRIC SOLIDS**

- The concepts of surface area and volume of a solid
- Prisms
- Pyramids
- Cylinders
- Cones
- Spheres

#### **AREA 5: TRIGONOMETRY**

**1. TRIGONOMETRIC FUNCTIONS IN A RIGHT-ANGLED TRIANGLE**

- Measurement of angles (degrees and radians)
- Concept and definition of the trigonometric functions of an acute angle
- Trigonometric functions of complementary angles
- Values of the trigonometric functions of the angles 30°, 45° and 60°
- Fundamental trigonometric identities
- Solving a right-angled triangle

# ФИНКИ се грижи за своите студенти\! – ФИНКИ

We are proud to announce the launch of FOCUS Lab.

On June 3, the FOCUS Lab programme was presented at FINKI – a space for student cooperation, support and collegiality, where every student will have the opportunity to develop their potential.

FOCUS Lab was welcomed by the Dean of FINKI, Prof. Petre Lameski, and the work plan was presented by the Head of FOCUS Lab – Prof. Ana Madevska Bogdanova, the President of the FINKI FSS – Georgina Ilievska, and Eng. Stefanija Filipashikj, author of the FocusMe application for easier focus while studying. After the official part, an informal meeting was held.

What does FOCUS Lab offer?

Peer-to-peer support: sessions with mentors (senior colleagues, tutors and assistants) who will not provide ready-made solutions, but will help students master the materials THEMSELVES – without fear of mistakes, points or signatures!

Smart, not hard, learning: workshops on learning techniques, time management and reducing academic stress.

Group work for a specific course – teamwork: building friendships and a network of colleagues who help each other with course obligations.

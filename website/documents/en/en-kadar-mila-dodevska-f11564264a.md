Mila Dodevska was born in Skopje, Republic of Macedonia. She completed her high school education at SUGS „Rade Jovchevski – Korzhagin“ High School in Skopje, with consistently excellent academic performance. In 2019, she enrolled in the undergraduate program at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering, in the Information Technology Applications track. She graduated in 2023, under the mentorship of Prof. Dr. Petre Lameski.

In the 2023/24 academic year, they are enrolling in a master's program at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering, in the Software for Embedded Systems track. He/She holds lecture and lab exercises for the following courses: Advanced Web Design, Mobile Information Systems, Mobile Platforms and Programming, Concepts of the Information Society, Digital Libraries, Human-Computer Interaction Design, Media and Communications.

# Promotion de la Summer School Cyber in Normandy – ENSICAEN GREYC – ФИНКИ

We are pleased to announce that, **ENSICAEN will be organizing its very first Summer School this year, titled “Cyber in Normandy”**, from the **June 24th, until July 5th, 2024**.

As you may have guessed, the focus of this Summer School will be cybersecurity, and it will bring together experts in the field from all over Europe. The target audiences are master's students, doctoral and postdoctoral researchers with prior knowledge and experience in cybersecurity.

**Application Deadline**March 1st

**Participation fees**€800 (including accommodation, breakfasts, and lunches from Monday to Friday, and social activities to discover Normandy)

You will find all the information on the website: [Summer School in Cybersecurity in Normandy 2024 – Sciencesconf.org](https://cyberinnormandy2024.sciencesconf.org/)

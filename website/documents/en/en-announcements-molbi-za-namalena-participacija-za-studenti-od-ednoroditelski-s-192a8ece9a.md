# Молби за намалена партиципација за студенти од еднородителски семејства – ФИНКИ

In accordance with **[одлука](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/odluka-ednoroditelski2025.pdf)**на ректорска управа на УКИМ 16.9.2025, Interested students are invited to apply for the internal job posting.

Право на учество на **[Огласот](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/interen-oglas-ednoroditelski2025.pdf)** има секој студент кој запишал зимски семестар на прв циклус на студии во учебната 2025/2026 година, а кој има само еден родител со родителско право (во натамошниот текст – апликант), односно има:

- only one parent is registered on the birth certificate, or;
- deceased parent, or
- a parent who has lost parental rights.

Апликантите, најдоцна до 16.10.2025 година,onелектронска адреса [arhiva@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:arhiva@finki.ukim.mk) They must submit an application in which they state:

– for freshmen: the total number of points by which, in accordance with the Admissions Competition, the student was enrolled in the first year of studies

– for upper-year students: average grade point average

With the application, applicants submit the following documents in PDF format:

– proof of salary for employed family members for the most recently paid salary,

– For social assistance recipients, a most recent decision/confirmation from the social services center is submitted,

For retired family members, a document proving the pension amount is provided.

– A court judgment establishing legal support for the applicant, which is considered monthly income.

– for a parent who has been stripped of parental rights – a decision from a competent court and/or

– for a deceased parent – a death certificate.

If necessary, it may be requested that the documents also be physically delivered to the FINKI archive.

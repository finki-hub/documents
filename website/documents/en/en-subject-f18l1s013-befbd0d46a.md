| 11. | Course content | (1) Definition of a function. Properties of functions. Operations on functions. (1) Rules. Families of functions. (1) Inverse functions. Exponential and logarithmic functions. (3) Limits. Calculation of limits. Continuity. (2) Definition of the derivative. Techniques of differentiation. Derivative of a composite function. (1) L'Hospital's rule. (1) Application of derivatives: monotonicity of functions, convexity and concavity of functions, local extremes. (1) Investigating the properties and sketching the graph of a function. Global extremes. (1) Integrating: indefinite integral, integration by substitution. (1) Definite integral. Fundamental Theorem of Calculus. |

# Соопштение за Префрлување на Студенти на – ФИНКИ

**Announcement on Student Transfers to FINKI**

**Dear Students,**

The Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering (FINKI) is pleased to open the transfer process for students from other faculties, giving them the opportunity to continue their studies in a modern academic environment. All interested students must submit their application no later than **September 20, 2024**.

**Required Documents:**

1. **Transfer Application**
   - Please ensure that you provide your contact telephone number and email address.
   - You can download the application form template [**here**](https://oldsite.finki.ukim.mk/sites/default/files/u703/molba_za_prefrluvanje_na_studenti_na_finki.pdf).
2. **Transcript of Passed Examinations**
3. **Curricula**
   - Detailed information on the curricula of the previously completed courses.
4. **Payment slip**
   - An amount of **MKD 3,000** paid to the FINKI account, with the payment reference “transfer from another faculty” (in accordance with the [FINKI fee schedule](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cenovnik_finki_2024-25-2.pdf)).
   - A payment of 400 denars per recognized course upon semester enrollment after receiving the equivalency decision.

**Transfer Requirements:**

- **Students from UKIM faculties:**
  - They will continue their studies under the same enrollment quota from which they are transferring.
- **Students from other universities:**
  - They will be able to continue their studies under the co-financing quota.

**The submission of documents is done electronically.** **by sending** **of the required documents** [**archive****@finki****.University of Kentucky****.mk**](mailto:arhiva@finki.ukim.mk)

The Transfer Committee will review all received applications and will notify the candidates of the results, which will also be published on our website.

# Marija Stojcheva

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/marija.stojcheva.jpg)

[marija.stojcheva@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:marija.stojcheva@finki.ukim.mk)

Maria Stojcheva was born in 2002 in Skopje. She enrolled in the undergraduate program at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering in 2020, in the Software Engineering and Information Systems track. During her studies, she was recognized as one of the top students at FINK and was employed as a lab assistant at the faculty. She graduated in 2024 with a grade point average of 9.82 and a thesis titled „Examining Air Pollution Awareness in Macedonia: A Three-Year Comparative Social Media Study,“ under the mentorship of Prof. Dr. Bojana Koteska. In the same year, she enrolled in graduate studies at FINKY, in the Data Science in Computer Science and Engineering program.
Her areas of scientific research interest are natural language processing, machine learning, deep learning, and software engineering.

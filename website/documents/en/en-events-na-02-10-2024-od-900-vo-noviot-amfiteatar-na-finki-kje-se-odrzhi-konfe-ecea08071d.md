# On October 2, 2024, at 9:00 AM, the conference „The Future of Digital Innovation – A Symposium on Collaboration with Academia and Industry“ will be held in the new amphitheater at FINKI\.

At the conference, information will be shared about the newly established Center for Digital Innovation – DigitMAK, including its core objectives and the services it offers to support digital transformation across various sectors.

Several panel discussions and presentations will cover important topics such as the upcoming open calls for project proposals and European initiatives for corporate innovation, as well as a presentation of the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development (EBRD) programs designed to support innovation and growth. Additionally, the specific opportunities from the EIT (European Institute of Innovation and Technology) available to the Macedonian business community will be discussed, as well as experiences in fostering collaboration and development from the EEN (Enterprise Europe Network) and UNICA, the network of Europe's capital city universities.

The conference will showcase the significant progress within the EuroCC project.,2 which aims to improve European high-performance computing and artificial intelligence capabilities. There will also be a session focused on the essential aspects of intellectual property rights to guide organizations in protecting their innovations.

After the main program, there will be opportunities for short meetings focused on DigitMac's services, including:

- Assessment of the innovative maturity of companies
- Educational and training programs
- Digitalization and digital services
- Access to finance

You can find more information about the conference at [the link](https://digitmak.mk/mk/the-future-of-digital-innovation-academia-and-industry-collaboration-symposium/).

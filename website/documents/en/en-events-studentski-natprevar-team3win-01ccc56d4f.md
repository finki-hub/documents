# Студентски натпревар TEAM3WIN – ФИНКИ

The University „St. Cyril and Methodius“ in Skopje, the Faculty of Mechanical Engineering – Skopje and the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering, in cooperation with ADAC Automotive, a renowned company that supplies its solutions for car entry and exit to companies such as Ford, General Motors, Stelantis, Honda, BMW, Mercedes, Tesla, and others, is issuing an open call for participation in a competition to develop a conceptual solution for car entry/exit. The challenge of the competition is to develop a concept for how people will enter and exit their vehicles in the future, and the Team3Win competition final will be held in the first week of June 2024.

The Team3Win competition is a continuation of a series of such competitions that were unfortunately interrupted by the pandemic. This time, the competition is in collaboration with an established international company in the automotive industry based in Michigan, USA. The theme of this year's competition is the development of a conceptual solution for car entry/exit in the year 2035.

The call for submissions for the competition is open until March 25, 2024.

Through a strategic partnership with UGIM, ADAC Automotive enables UGIM students to work on a conceptual solution to a global challenge in the automotive industry.

All students from UKI are invited to apply for the competition. Teams should consist of 4 to 5 members, but if you want to participate and don't have a team, you can contact us so we can find a solution. Maybe your solution will be featured on future cars! It is recommended that team members be from at least three different degree programs. The top teams in the competition will receive attractive cash prizes, prototype fabrication at the FABLAB, and the opportunity to visit the company in the USA.

# Bojana Marojevikj

![](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/bojana-683x1024.jpg)

[bojana.marojevikj@finki.ukim.mk](mailto:bojana.marojevikj@finki.ukim.mk)

Bojana Marojević was born in Skopje, where she completed her elementary and high school education with consistently excellent results. In 2020, she enrolled in undergraduate studies in the major of *Software Engineering and Information Systems* at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering – Skopje, where they graduated in September 2024. Their thesis is titled „Architecture and Implementation of an Integrated Solution for Faculty Services at FCSE,“ under the mentorship of Prof. Dr. Riste Stojanov.

In December 2023, she was hired as a lab instructor at FINKI. In this role, she participates in maintaining and improving the student services at FINKY, provides technical support and mentorship to students, as well as leading consultation hours for project activities in the Web Programming course. In the 2024/2025 academic year, she enrolled in graduate studies at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering, in the study program *Data Science in Computer Science and Engineering*. In October 2024, they continue their engagement with the faculty to develop and maintain faculty services. They participate in medically oriented research projects related to data analysis and software development. In July 2026, they graduate with a master's thesis titled „Proteasix: A Data-Driven Platform for Reverse Protease Prediction from Peptide Cleavage Signatures,” under the mentorship of Prof. Dr. Riste Stojanov and in collaboration with the Institute for Metabolic and Cardiovascular Diseases in Toulouse, France.

Pece Georgiev was born in 2002 in Bitola. He completed his primary and secondary education in Bitola, where he actively participated in competitions in various subjects.

In 2021, he enrolled in undergraduate studies at the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering (FINKI) in Skopje, majoring in Computer Science. During his studies, he completed an internship at the company „Netcetera“ as a machine learning intern. He graduated in 2025 with a grade point average of 9.92, with a thesis titled „A Metaheuristic Approach for Solving the NP-Hard Optimization Problem of Exam Scheduling,“ prepared under the mentorship of Prof. Dr. Sonja Gievska.

In 2025, they enrolled in graduate studies at FINKI, in the Statistics and Data Analytics program. During the same period, he participates in teaching, conducting lecture and laboratory exercises, as well as preparing, editing, and improving teaching materials for the exercises in the following courses: Linear Algebra and Applications, Probability and Statistics, Mathematics 3, and Engineering Mathematics.

His areas of scientific research interest include artificial intelligence, statistics and data analytics, machine learning, optimization theory, game theory, algorithm design, and software engineering.

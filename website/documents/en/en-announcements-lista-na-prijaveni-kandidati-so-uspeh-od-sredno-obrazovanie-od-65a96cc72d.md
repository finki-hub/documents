# Листа на пријавени кандидати со успех од средно образование од прв уписен рок 2024/2025 година – ФИНКИ

The list of registered candidates for enrollment at FINKI for the first enrollment period for the 2024/2025 academic year has been published. You can find the list at the following **[link](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/lista_kandidati_prv_rok.pdf)**.

The list contains information about what has been filled out in the documents and does not provide information on the candidates' rankings. It includes the candidate's number, their first choice of study program, whether the candidate has chosen co-financing or not, the order of priority for program tracks, as well as the success points.

After the publication of the list of applicants for enrollment in the first year, beginning on September 3, 2024, and no later than 10:00 AM on September 4, 2024, objections regarding technical errors may be submitted. Objections are submitted by completing the form at the following link. **[link](https://forms.office.com/e/CMx4ErJdpd)**.

Technical errors refer to:

1. Printing error on the main program of study selection

2. Co-financed selection

3. Order of study programs

4. Incorrectly calculated points (due to subject equivalence, type of final exam, etc.)

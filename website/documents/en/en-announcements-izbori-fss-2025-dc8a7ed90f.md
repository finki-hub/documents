# Избори ФСС 2025 – ФИНКИ

Dear Students,
On Monday, June 30, 2025, from 8 a.m. to 2 p.m., this year's elections for the Faculty Student Assembly of FINK will be held.
Attached are:

1. **[Voter list](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/izbirachki_spisok.pdf)**
2. **[Candidacies](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/kandidaturi_fss_finki_2025.pdf)**
3. **[Ballot Paper](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/glasachko_livche.pdf)**
4. **[Instructions for the Election Committee](https://finki.ukim.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/upatstvo_za_rabota_na_izbornata_2025.pdf)**

You are required to verify whether your name is included on the electoral roll and, should you identify any issue, contact the Election Committee no later than 12 hours before the elections. Based on the submitted candidacies, you may review the candidates and decide whom you wish to vote for using the attached ballot.
On the day of the elections, all eligible voters will receive an email containing a unique voting link, which may be used only once. Voting will be anonymous, and further instructions will be provided on the day of the elections.

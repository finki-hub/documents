# GRADING SCALE

| Grade | ECTS | Percentage | Description |
|---:|:---:|:---:|---|
| 10 | A | 91–100 | This mark indicates outstanding performance. In addition to having mastered the content of the course, the student has demonstrated the ability to apply the course material in new and creative ways has shown an understanding of its wider context. |
| 9 | B | 81–90 | This mark indicates very good performance. The student has demonstrated excellent knowledge of the course material and comprehension of its essential concepts. |
| 8 | C | 71–80 | This mark indicates good performance. The student has demonstrated a thorough knowledge of the course material and comprehension of its essential concepts. |
| 7 | D | 61–70 | This mark range indicates satisfactory performance. The student has demonstrated an adequate knowledge of course material. |
| 6 | E | 51–60 | This mark indicates poor performance. The student has minimally fulfilled the requirements for the course. |
| 5 | F | 0–50 | This mark indicates that the student has failed to meet the minimum requirements. |
